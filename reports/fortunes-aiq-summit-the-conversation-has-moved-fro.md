**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
At Fortune's AIQ Summit, executives from ServiceNow, HPE, Palo Alto Networks, Bank of America, Honeywell, Booking Holdings, and others discussed moving AI efforts from scattered pilots to enterprise-wide transformation, covering workforce reskilling, governance, physical/regulated environment constraints, and ROI measurement. Key trends explored: Moving from experimentation to enterprise transformation; Building an AI-ready workforce; Managing governance, trust, and risk; Applying AI in physical and regulated environments; Measuring ROI, controlling costs, and creating growth.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The article highlights that successful AI transformation depends less on technology access and more on leadership vision, workforce psychological readiness (curiosity, ability to 'unlearn'), trust-building through governance, and avoiding burnout from AI-enabled overproduction—core organizational behavior themes rather than purely technical ones.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat AI adoption as a workforce and culture transformation, not a tooling rollout: invest disproportionately in talent development and retention (the widest gap between high- and low-performing firms), redesign workflows rather than automate old ones, build governance systems that increase psychological trust and deployment speed, and monitor for AI-driven burnout and false productivity signals.
