## Summary
A billionaire CEO's aggressive rant about implementing surveillance cameras to monitor remote workers went viral, resulting in 23 employees quitting overnight. The incident highlights the tension between employer concerns about remote work productivity and employee autonomy, with data suggesting employee distraction is a legitimate managerial concern.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case illustrates how coercive surveillance and authoritarian communication styles destroy psychological safety and trust, triggering mass voluntary turnover. It also reflects the tension between organizational control mechanisms and employee autonomy, a core self-determination theory dynamic.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders addressing legitimate productivity concerns must choose communication strategies that preserve trust and dignity. Heavy-handed surveillance threats backfire—CHROs and COOs should invest in outcome-based performance management and transparent accountability systems rather than invasive monitoring, as the cost of turnover from destroyed trust far exceeds the cost of occasional distraction.
