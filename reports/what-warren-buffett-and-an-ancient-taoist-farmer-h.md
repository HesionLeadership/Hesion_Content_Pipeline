## Summary
The article draws a parallel between Warren Buffett's investment philosophy and an ancient Taoist parable about a farmer who responds to every event with 'maybe,' arguing that effective leaders should embrace uncertainty and avoid premature judgment about whether outcomes are good or bad.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This connects to cognitive flexibility and tolerance for ambiguity in leadership—research shows leaders who avoid premature closure and maintain equanimity under uncertainty make better strategic decisions and foster more adaptive organizations.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should cultivate a 'maybe' mindset in their leadership teams—encouraging leaders to resist snap judgments about setbacks or wins, instead maintaining cognitive openness that allows for better long-term decision-making and organizational resilience.
