# Why Employees With Working-Class Roots Are Better at Getting Their Ideas Heard

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Mon, 31 Aug 2026 11:00:02 +0000  
**URL:** [https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/why-employees-with-working-class-roots-are-better-at-getting-their-ideas-heard/](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/why-employees-with-working-class-roots-are-better-at-getting-their-ideas-heard/)

## Summary
Research from MIT Sloan reveals that white-collar employees with working-class origins are more effective at getting their ideas heard by managers. These employees employ a communication style characterized by concrete, practical framing that resonates with decision-makers, a skill developed through navigating class transitions in their careers.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Social class origin shapes communication strategies in organizations — employees who have crossed class boundaries develop a bicultural fluency that allows them to frame ideas in ways that reduce managerial resistance, highlighting how socioeconomic background influences voice behavior and idea endorsement.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that diverse socioeconomic backgrounds on teams aren't just an equity consideration — they bring tangible communication advantages. Organizations can train all employees in the concrete, practical idea-framing techniques that come naturally to class-mobile workers, and should audit whether idea evaluation processes inadvertently favor certain communication styles over others.
