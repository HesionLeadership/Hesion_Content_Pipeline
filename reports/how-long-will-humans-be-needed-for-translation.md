## Summary
The article examines how AI translation tools have rapidly improved, reducing but not eliminating the need for human translators, especially in high-stakes fields like law and medicine where subtle errors can have serious consequences.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates the human-automation trust and oversight dilemma: as AI output becomes more fluent and harder to distinguish from human-quality work, organizations face increased risk of complacency and reduced vigilance in quality control (automation bias).

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs deploying AI tools in knowledge work should calibrate human oversight to risk level, not just output quality—build in structured verification processes for high-stakes domains, and resist reducing human review simply because AI output 'sounds' competent.
