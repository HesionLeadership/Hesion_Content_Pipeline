# Goodles Built a Brand by Keeping It Weird

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Mon, 31 Aug 2026 09:44:09 -0400

## Summary
Goodles founder Jen Zeszut discusses how she built a distinctive brand in the crowded mac-and-cheese category by embracing weirdness and unconventional branding. Her core argument is that playing it safe is the bigger risk, and authenticity in brand identity—even when it feels uncomfortable—drives differentiation and growth.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights how founder identity and tolerance for ambiguity shape organizational culture and brand strategy. Leaders who encourage creative deviance and resist conformity pressures can foster innovative cultures, but this requires psychological safety and conviction at the leadership level.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that cultivating a distinctive organizational identity requires leaders who can tolerate discomfort and resist defaulting to industry norms. Building a culture that rewards creative risk-taking—rather than penalizing deviation—can be a genuine competitive advantage, but it demands intentional alignment between leadership values and organizational practices.
