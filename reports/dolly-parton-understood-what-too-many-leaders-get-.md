## Summary
The article distinguishes between personal brand and leadership legacy, using Dolly Parton as an example. A personal brand is what people know you for, while a true leadership legacy is what continues to help people after you're gone—illustrated by Parton's Imagination Library and investments in her community that outlast her personal fame.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The distinction between self-serving leadership (brand-building) and servant leadership (legacy-building) maps onto research on prosocial motivation, transformational leadership, and the long-term organizational impact of leaders who prioritize institutional capacity over personal visibility.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should evaluate whether their leadership development programs cultivate leaders who build sustainable systems, talent pipelines, and cultural norms that persist beyond their tenure—not just leaders who accumulate personal influence and recognition.
