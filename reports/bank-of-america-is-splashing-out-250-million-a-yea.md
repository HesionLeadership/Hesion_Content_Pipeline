# Bank of America is splashing out $250 million a year on weight loss drugs for its staff: ‘We see a great impact on employees,’ CEO says

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Fri, 07 Aug 2026 15:06:01 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/08/07/bank-of-america-splashing-out-250-million-a-year-on-weight-loss-drugs-for-staff-great-impact-ceo-says/](https://fortune.com/2026/08/07/bank-of-america-splashing-out-250-million-a-year-on-weight-loss-drugs-for-staff-great-impact-ceo-says/)

## Summary
Bank of America is spending $250 million annually to cover GLP-1 weight-loss drugs like Ozempic and Wegovy for employees, with CEO Brian Moynihan citing significant positive impact on employee health and productivity. Nearly a third of workers say they would switch jobs for employer-covered weight-loss drug benefits.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects the growing strategic role of employee benefits as a talent attraction and retention lever, with health-related perks increasingly functioning as a competitive differentiator in the labor market. The willingness of employees to switch jobs for a specific benefit highlights how perceived organizational support for personal well-being drives organizational commitment.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should evaluate high-impact health benefits not just as cost centers but as strategic investments in talent retention and productivity. When a significant proportion of the workforce would change employers for a specific benefit, it signals a shift in the psychological contract—leaders who recognize and respond to evolving employee expectations around holistic well-being can gain meaningful competitive advantage in talent markets.
