## Summary
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang admitted to an affair with a woman alleged to be a Chinese spy, triggering a national security investigation and a 4.3% stock drop that wiped out ~$230 billion in shareholder value. The article argues this exposes massive 'key man risk' given Huang and Nvidia's outsized, deeply entangled influence across the entire AI private markets ecosystem (as GPU supplier, investor, and financing partner), but predicts Huang will survive and remain CEO.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a textbook case of 'key man risk' and single-leader dependency, where an organization's entire strategic and financial ecosystem becomes dangerously concentrated around one individual's judgment, reputation, and personal conduct, creating systemic fragility beyond normal succession planning concerns.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and boards should treat founder/CEO over-centralization as an enterprise risk, not just a leadership style issue—this means building genuine succession depth, separating personal reputation from institutional brand, and establishing governance safeguards (conflict-of-interest disclosures, security vetting protocols) before a crisis forces reactive decisions under market pressure.
