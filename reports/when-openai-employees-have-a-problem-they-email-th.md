# When OpenAI employees have a problem, they email this special address to see if Sam Altman will solve it immediately

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Tue, 11 Aug 2026 23:19:20 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/08/11/openai-employees-email-friction-address-to-eliminate-bureaucratic-bottlenecks-sam-altman/](https://fortune.com/2026/08/11/openai-employees-email-friction-address-to-eliminate-bureaucratic-bottlenecks-sam-altman/)

## Summary
OpenAI has created a friction@openai.com email address where any employee can report operational frustrations—from parking problems to technical bottlenecks—directly to senior leadership including Sam Altman, who often resolves issues immediately. The initiative is designed to eliminate bureaucratic inefficiency and maintain startup agility as the company scales.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This mechanism serves as a formalized upward communication channel that flattens hierarchy and signals leadership accessibility, potentially enhancing psychological safety and employee voice. However, it also risks creating dependency on a single leader for problem-solving rather than building organizational systems, and may not scale as the company grows.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider creating structured, low-barrier channels for employees to surface operational friction directly to leadership—but pair them with systematic process improvement so that recurring issues are addressed structurally rather than through heroic individual intervention. The real value is in the signal (what problems keep surfacing) rather than the CEO personally fixing parking.
