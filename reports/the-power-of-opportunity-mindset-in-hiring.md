## Summary
The article explores the 'opportunity mindset' in hiring—the idea that hiring managers should look beyond traditional credentials and consider candidates who may have been overlooked, reflecting on moments when someone gave them a chance that changed their career trajectory.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This connects to research on inclusive hiring practices, bias reduction in selection, and how expanding candidate pools beyond pedigree-based screening can improve organizational performance and diversity outcomes.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should encourage hiring managers to adopt an opportunity mindset—actively identifying high-potential candidates from non-traditional backgrounds—which can unlock untapped talent pipelines and reduce systemic biases embedded in conventional screening criteria.
