# Transforming the New York Liberty Into WNBA Champions

**Source:** Bloomberg

## Summary
Clara Wu Tsai discusses how she transformed the New York Liberty into WNBA champions by relocating the team to Brooklyn's Barclays Center, investing in players, facilities, and management, creating an organizational turnaround that led to a championship.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a case study in organizational turnaround through strategic resource investment, talent acquisition, and infrastructure upgrades. Wu Tsai's approach illustrates how ownership commitment to facilities, personnel, and management quality can shift organizational culture and performance outcomes.

## Leadership Lesson
Organizational transformation requires aligned investment across multiple dimensions simultaneously—talent, infrastructure, and leadership—rather than piecemeal improvements. CHROs and COOs should recognize that upgrading facilities and working conditions signals organizational commitment to employees, which can attract top talent and elevate performance.
