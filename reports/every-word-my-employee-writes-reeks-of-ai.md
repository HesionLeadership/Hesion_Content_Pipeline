## Organizational Psychology Angle
Both scenarios touch on critical management challenges: the first involves performance management and trust erosion when employees use AI tools without transparency, raising questions about skill development, authenticity, and accountability. The second addresses psychological safety and privacy boundaries in workplace communication.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders must establish clear policies around AI tool usage in the workplace, including expectations for transparency and quality standards, while also coaching managers on how to address performance concerns directly. For sensitive personal information like health diagnoses, leaders should proactively create norms around privacy and confidentiality rather than reacting after gossip spreads.
