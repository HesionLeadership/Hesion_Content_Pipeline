## Summary
Nvidia's board is reportedly meeting to decide whether to oust CEO Jensen Huang following revelations of an extramarital affair with an alleged Chinese intelligence asset and a separate affair with a direct report; Nvidia stock fell sharply amid the uncertainty, compounded by the company's lack of a clear succession plan despite Huang's 33-year tenure and 60 direct reports.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The crisis exposes classic risks of founder-centric leadership: extreme centralization of authority (60 direct reports, minimal delegation) left the organization without a viable succession pipeline, and a power-distance dynamic (affair with a subordinate) raises governance and conflict-of-interest concerns.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and boards should treat succession planning and manager-span-of-control as core risk-management functions, not optional extras—especially for founder-led companies where power concentration can mask governance blind spots until a crisis forces a reckoning.
