# How Leaders Talk About AI Predicts Adoption

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-09-30T12:15:50Z

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
New BCG research finds that how leaders narrate AI's impact—whether framing it as uncertain, threatening, or empowering—materially predicts employee adoption outcomes. Leaders face a choice between narratives of fear, transparency, or opportunity, and each choice carries consequences for how AI is embraced.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This connects to sensemaking theory and narrative leadership research, which show that leaders shape collective meaning and emotional framing during organizational change, directly influencing employee anxiety, motivation, and behavioral adoption.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat AI communication as a deliberate narrative strategy, not an afterthought—framing change with clarity and growth-oriented language to reduce fear-driven resistance and increase genuine adoption.
