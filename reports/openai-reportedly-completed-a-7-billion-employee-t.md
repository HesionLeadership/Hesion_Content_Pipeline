## Summary
OpenAI reportedly completed a $7 billion employee tender offer, allowing employees to sell shares in a secondary transaction. The story also notes implications for San Francisco's housing market due to the potential influx of newly liquid wealth.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Employee tender offers are powerful retention and motivation tools, signaling organizational commitment to sharing value creation with employees. The ability to liquidate equity can significantly impact employee psychological contracts, reducing 'golden handcuffs' resentment while reinforcing perceived organizational support.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should consider how equity liquidity events shape employee engagement and retention—offering periodic tender offers can strengthen the psychological contract by converting abstract equity promises into tangible rewards, but leaders must also manage expectations and perceived fairness in who gets to participate and at what valuation.
