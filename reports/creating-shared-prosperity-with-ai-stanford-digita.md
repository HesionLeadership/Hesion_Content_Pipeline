## Summary
Stanford economist Erik Brynjolfsson argues that the biggest barrier to realizing AI's potential isn't the technology itself, but rather the people, organizations, and institutions that must adapt to use it effectively. He challenges leaders to shift from asking 'what will AI do to us?' to 'what will we do with AI?' to create shared prosperity.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The framing that organizational and institutional redesign—not technology—is the primary bottleneck to AI adoption speaks directly to research on organizational change readiness, resistance to change, and the sociotechnical systems perspective that emphasizes co-evolution of technology and work design.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should invest as heavily in organizational redesign, workforce reskilling, and institutional adaptation as they do in AI technology itself; the return on AI depends more on how work processes, roles, and culture are restructured than on the sophistication of the tools deployed.
