## Summary
Stanford economist Erik Brynjolfsson argues that AI's impact depends not on the technology itself but on how people, organizations, and institutions choose to deploy it. He challenges leaders to shift from a deterministic view of AI to one focused on intentional organizational redesign for shared prosperity.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights that organizational structures, human decision-making, and institutional inertia are the primary barriers to realizing AI's benefits — not the technology itself. This frames AI adoption as fundamentally a change management and organizational design challenge.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that successful AI integration requires redesigning workflows, roles, and decision-making processes rather than simply deploying new tools. Leaders must invest in organizational readiness, employee reskilling, and institutional adaptation as the critical path to AI-driven value.
