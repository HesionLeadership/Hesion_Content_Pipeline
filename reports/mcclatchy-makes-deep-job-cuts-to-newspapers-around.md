# McClatchy Makes Deep Job Cuts to Newspapers Around the Country

**Source:** NYT Business RSS  
**Published:** Fri, 11 Sep 2026 02:25:29 +0000  
**URL:** [https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/10/business/media/mcclatchy-miami-herald-layoffs.html](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/09/10/business/media/mcclatchy-miami-herald-layoffs.html)

## Summary
McClatchy, owner of The Miami Herald and other newspapers, is conducting widespread layoffs across its properties, with one employee describing the cuts as a 'blood bath.' The company cited declining subscriber interest as not supporting current investment levels.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Mass layoffs described as a 'blood bath' signal a failure in managing organizational change with dignity and transparency, likely devastating survivor morale and trust. The framing of cuts as purely market-driven overlooks leadership's responsibility in workforce transition planning and psychological safety for remaining employees.

## Leadership Lesson
When executing large-scale workforce reductions, leaders must invest in transparent communication, procedural fairness, and survivor support programs—employees who remain often experience guilt, anxiety, and disengagement that can accelerate organizational decline if unaddressed.
