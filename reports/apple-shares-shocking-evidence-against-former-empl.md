# Apple shares ‘shocking evidence’ against former employee accused of stealing company data for OpenAI

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** Tue, 01 Sep 2026 00:13:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/apple-shares-shocking-evidence-against-former-employee-accused-of-stealing-company-data-for-openai/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/apple-shares-shocking-evidence-against-former-employee-accused-of-stealing-company-data-for-openai/)

## Summary
Apple has presented evidence that a former employee, Rui Zhang, allegedly stole proprietary data related to AI projects before leaving to join OpenAI, and then destroyed evidence after learning of the investigation against him.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case highlights the critical risks organizations face during employee offboarding, particularly when employees depart for direct competitors. It raises questions about insider threat detection, psychological contract violations, and the tension between employee mobility and intellectual property protection.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure robust offboarding protocols—including monitoring of data access patterns, clear IP agreements, and timely access revocation—especially for employees in sensitive roles. Proactive insider threat programs must balance trust with verification to protect organizational assets without creating a culture of surveillance.
