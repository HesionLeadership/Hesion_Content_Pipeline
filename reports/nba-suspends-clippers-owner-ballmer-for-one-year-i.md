# NBA suspends Clippers owner Ballmer for one year in Kawhi Leonard salary cap probe

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Thu, 03 Sep 2026 04:52:41 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/nba-suspends-clippers-ballmer-kawhi-leonard-salary-cap.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/nba-suspends-clippers-ballmer-kawhi-leonard-salary-cap.html)

## Summary
The NBA suspended Clippers owner Steve Ballmer for one year and fined the team $30 million, along with forfeiting five first-round draft picks, following an investigation into salary cap violations related to Kawhi Leonard's compensation arrangements.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case illustrates how leaders who circumvent regulatory frameworks and governance structures—even with competitive intent—can trigger severe organizational consequences, undermining the very competitive advantage they sought to gain.

## Leadership Lesson
Senior leaders must recognize that circumventing compliance structures for short-term competitive advantage creates existential organizational risk. CHROs and COOs should ensure that compensation practices are transparent, auditable, and aligned with governing rules, as the reputational and structural damage from violations far outweighs any gains.
