# Two Top Tech Leaders Stepped Back for Health Reasons. It’s a Warning About AI’s Endless Work

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Thu, 06 Aug 2026 14:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/scott-hutcheson/openai-thinking-machines-lab-step-back-biology/91385964](https://www.inc.com/scott-hutcheson/openai-thinking-machines-lab-step-back-biology/91385964)

## Summary
Two senior tech leaders—from Thinking Machines Lab and OpenAI—stepped back from their roles due to health issues, highlighting the unsustainable pace and intensity of the AI industry's relentless work culture.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The AI arms race is creating extreme work demands that exceed human capacity for sustained performance, illustrating how hyper-competitive industry dynamics can normalize burnout at the highest leadership levels and erode the boundary between dedication and self-destruction.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs in high-velocity industries must proactively build systemic safeguards against executive burnout—including succession depth, mandatory recovery periods, and cultural norms that treat leader well-being as a strategic asset rather than an afterthought. If your top leaders are burning out, it's an organizational design failure, not an individual weakness.
