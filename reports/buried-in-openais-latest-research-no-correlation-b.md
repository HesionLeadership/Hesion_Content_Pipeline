# Buried in OpenAI’s latest research: No correlation between AI use and revenue per employee

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Thu, 13 Aug 2026 20:28:57 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/buried-in-openais-latest-research-no-correlation-between-ai-use-and-revenue-per-employee/](https://fortune.com/2026/08/13/buried-in-openais-latest-research-no-correlation-between-ai-use-and-revenue-per-employee/)

## Summary
OpenAI's own research found no correlation between ChatGPT Enterprise usage and revenue per employee among its corporate customers, raising fundamental questions about the ROI of generative AI tools in the workplace.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the persistent gap between technology adoption enthusiasm and measurable productivity gains—echoing the 'productivity paradox' first identified with IT investments. Organizations may be deploying AI tools without the complementary changes in work design, training, and processes needed to realize value.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should not assume that simply providing AI tools will drive productivity improvements. Leaders must pair technology adoption with intentional job redesign, upskilling programs, and clear use-case identification—measuring actual workflow impact rather than tool usage metrics.
