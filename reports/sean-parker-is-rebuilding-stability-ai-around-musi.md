# Sean Parker is rebuilding Stability AI around music

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Sean Parker and CEO Prem Akkaraju are repositioning Stability AI, the image-generator startup that nearly collapsed after founder Emad Mostaque's ouster, to focus on music AI tools. The company secured $76 million in funding from Sony, Warner, and Universal, who also licensed their catalogs, and has released new audio-generation and editing models.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates organizational recovery after founder-led instability and overspending, with new leadership explicitly adopting a more compliance-oriented, relationship-building strategy (licensing deals with labels) rather than the disruptive 'ask forgiveness' approach that defined Parker's earlier venture.

## Leadership Lesson
When rebuilding a troubled organization, new leadership should prioritize repairing stakeholder trust and establishing legitimacy (e.g., formal licensing agreements) before pursuing aggressive growth, especially if past organizational failures stemmed from cutting corners or ignoring external partners.
