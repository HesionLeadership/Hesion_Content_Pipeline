# The Hidden Costs of Monitoring Employees with AI

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-09-17T12:15:03Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/09/the-hidden-costs-of-monitoring-employees-with-ai](https://hbr.org/2026/09/the-hidden-costs-of-monitoring-employees-with-ai)

## Summary
The article examines how AI-powered workplace surveillance tools, while increasingly accessible and affordable, carry hidden costs for organizations including eroded trust, reduced intrinsic motivation, and potential damage to organizational culture. Leaders are urged to carefully weigh the trade-offs before implementing employee monitoring technologies.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
AI surveillance in the workplace directly undermines psychological safety and perceived autonomy, two foundational elements of employee engagement and intrinsic motivation. Research on self-determination theory suggests that monitoring can shift employees from autonomous to controlled motivation, reducing creativity, discretionary effort, and organizational commitment.

## Leadership Lesson
Before deploying AI monitoring tools, CHROs and COOs should conduct a thorough cost-benefit analysis that accounts for intangible costs—diminished trust, lower morale, increased turnover intent, and reduced innovation. If monitoring is deemed necessary, leaders should involve employees in co-designing transparent policies, clearly communicate the purpose and scope, and establish governance structures that protect employee dignity.
