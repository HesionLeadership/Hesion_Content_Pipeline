# American Express is cutting 5% of its workforce to 'prepare for AI'

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Fri, 23 Oct 2026 18:57:48 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/23/american-express-job-cuts.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/23/american-express-job-cuts.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
American Express is cutting 5% of its global workforce (from ~77,000 employees) and taking a $336 million restructuring charge to become 'leaner' and prepare for AI automation, according to CEO Stephen Squeri and CFO Christophe Le Caillec. The move mirrors a broader financial services trend, with Barclays also cutting 5% of staff for similar reasons.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The restructuring reflects a deliberate organizational redesign strategy—'reducing layers and increasing spans of control'—before automation, illustrating how companies are reshaping hierarchical structures and job design in anticipation of AI integration rather than reactively.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that successful AI adoption requires restructuring workflows and organizational architecture first; simply layering automation onto existing inefficient structures will not yield gains. Leaders must also manage the psychological impact of job cuts tied to technology narratives, as framing layoffs as 'AI preparation' can affect employee trust and morale even amid strong financial performance.
