## Summary
The article argues that effective leadership requires genuine 'toughness'—not bullying or passivity—and outlines five practices: (1) Tell & Live a Good Story, (2) Assemble a Team You Trust to Care, (3) Use Emotions Strategically, (4) Address Mistakes Honestly & Quickly, (5) Make Accountability a Non-negotiable.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The piece touches on psychological safety, emotional regulation, and accountability systems as mechanisms for balancing high standards with humane treatment of employees, avoiding both toxic micromanagement and conflict avoidance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build structured accountability systems (clear owners, tracking, consequences) rather than relying on charisma or fear, and should train leaders to regulate emotional expression so intensity translates into constructive urgency rather than anxiety-inducing outbursts.
