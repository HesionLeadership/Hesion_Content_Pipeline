## Summary
India's HDFC Bank stock fell to a 2.5-year low amid ongoing legal challenges and leadership concerns, reflecting investor anxiety about the bank's governance and executive stability following its mega-merger with Housing Development Finance Corporation.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Leadership instability and legal uncertainties in the aftermath of a major merger can erode stakeholder confidence and organizational identity, illustrating how unresolved governance and succession issues compound post-merger integration challenges.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure that leadership continuity plans and governance frameworks are firmly established before and during major mergers, as lingering ambiguity in these areas can undermine both market confidence and internal organizational cohesion.
