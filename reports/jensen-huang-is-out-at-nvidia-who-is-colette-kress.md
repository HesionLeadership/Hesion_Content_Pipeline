## Summary
Nvidia's Board forced out co-founder and longtime CEO Jensen Huang effective immediately following reports of affairs with a direct report and an alleged Chinese spy; CFO Colette Kress has stepped in as interim CEO while the company searches for a permanent successor, with earnings due Tuesday.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the risks of founder-centric leadership with no succession plan, and the governance failure of allowing a CEO to have a romantic relationship with a direct report, which creates power-abuse and conflict-of-interest risks regardless of performance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should mandate succession planning even for seemingly indispensable founder-CEOs, and enforce strict policies on relationships with direct reports to prevent power imbalances, favoritism perceptions, and security/governance risks that can force a company into reactive, high-stakes leadership transitions.
