# Leaders Should Stop Assuming the Team Got the Message

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Tue, 22 Sep 2026 08:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/inc-video/leaders-should-stop-assuming-the-team-got-the-message/91407130](https://www.inc.com/inc-video/leaders-should-stop-assuming-the-team-got-the-message/91407130)

## Summary
Marcos Redondo argues that leaders should not assume their team understood a message after delivering it once. Effective communication requires ongoing clarification, follow-up check-ins, and rapid coaching when misunderstandings occur.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This aligns with research on communication breakdowns in organizations, where message distortion increases with hierarchical distance. Leader-member exchange theory suggests that high-quality dyadic communication—including feedback loops and active checking for understanding—directly improves task performance and reduces errors.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build communication verification into leadership development programs. Train managers to treat every directive as incomplete until they've confirmed understanding through follow-up, and create norms where team members feel safe signaling confusion without fear of appearing incompetent.
