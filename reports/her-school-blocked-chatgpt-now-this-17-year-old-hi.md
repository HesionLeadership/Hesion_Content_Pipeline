# Her school blocked ChatGPT. Now this 17-year-old high school senior runs an AI startup with 1,000 users

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sun, 27 Sep 2026 11:00:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/09/27/17-year-old-ai-founder-banned-in-school/](https://fortune.com/2026/09/27/17-year-old-ai-founder-banned-in-school/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
A 17-year-old high school senior built a free AI-powered platform (Raaha) helping neurodivergent job seekers evaluate job listings before applying, and a second tool (Legible) translating special-education paperwork into plain language. She credits her school's ChatGPT ban with building her independent thinking skills, but argues regulation—not prohibition—is the right long-term approach to AI, echoing survey data showing many professionals hide their AI use out of fear of being seen as less competent.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story surfaces two organizational psychology dynamics: (1) constraint-driven skill development, where removing a tool (AI) during formative learning periods forced deeper cognitive engagement, and (2) AI disclosure stigma, where workers/students who admit to using AI are perceived as less competent or lazier even when output quality is identical.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that blanket bans on new tools may inadvertently build valuable skills before rollout, but sustainable AI adoption requires shifting evaluation criteria from 'did you use AI' to 'how did you use it and refine it'—reducing disclosure stigma so employees don't hide usage and lose the benefit of transparent best-practice sharing.
