## Summary
A four-year ethnographic study of a professional services firm reveals how leadership anxiety during major organizational transformation can derail change efforts. The research, based on nearly 760 hours of observation and over 300 interviews, examines the psychological dynamics that cause leaders to undermine the very transformations they initiate.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Leadership anxiety during transformation creates a paradox where leaders' emotional responses to uncertainty cause them to revert to controlling behaviors, micromanagement, or abandonment of change initiatives—effectively sabotaging the strategic shifts they championed. This reflects deep tensions between cognitive commitment to change and emotional threat responses.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build organizational mechanisms—such as peer coaching, structured reflection, and psychological safety for leaders themselves—to help senior leaders recognize and manage their own anxiety during transformation, rather than assuming leadership buy-in at the outset guarantees sustained commitment through execution.
