# Big Brands Have a New Secret Weapon for Sales: Their Own Employees

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Wed, 29 Jul 2026 12:29:39 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/big-brands-have-a-new-secret-weapon-for-sales-their-own-employees/91381539](https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/big-brands-have-a-new-secret-weapon-for-sales-their-own-employees/91381539)

## Summary
Major brands are increasingly investing in turning their own employees and micro-creators into brand ambassadors, finding that these authentic voices outperform traditional mega-influencers in driving sales.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
When employees become brand ambassadors, it signals and reinforces organizational identification—employees who publicly advocate for their employer internalize brand values more deeply, creating a virtuous cycle of engagement and authentic representation. This also taps into psychological ownership, where employees feel a stake in the brand's success beyond their job description.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that employee advocacy programs serve a dual purpose: they are both a marketing strategy and an engagement lever. Investing in employees as brand voices can strengthen organizational identification and intrinsic motivation, but leaders must ensure participation is voluntary and supported—not mandated—to preserve authenticity and avoid cynicism.
