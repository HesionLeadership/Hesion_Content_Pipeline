# Nvidia’s new chairman Harvey Jones is a ‘charming elder statesman’ set to lead the search for the next CEO. But did he enable the current crisis?

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Thu, 12 Nov 2026 21:32:48 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-new-board-chair-harvey-jones-likely-to-lead-ceo-search/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/12/nvidia-new-board-chair-harvey-jones-likely-to-lead-ceo-search/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Following Jensen Huang's departure from Nvidia amid revelations of extramarital affairs (including one with a direct report) that violated company conduct codes, longtime board member Harvey Jones has become the company's first Chairman and will likely lead the CEO search. The story explores Jones' background and raises questions about whether the board, including Jones, failed to identify or act on the reputational risks posed by Huang's conduct and lack of succession planning.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case highlights how long board tenure and close relationships with a powerful founder-CEO can create blind spots in oversight, allowing conflicts of interest and misconduct to persist unchecked despite formal codes of conduct.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure that codes of conduct are actively enforced regardless of an executive's seniority or performance, and that board oversight mechanisms don't become captured by long-standing personal relationships with founder-CEOs; excessive span of control (e.g., 60 direct reports) and absent succession planning are structural governance failures that compound personal misconduct risk.
