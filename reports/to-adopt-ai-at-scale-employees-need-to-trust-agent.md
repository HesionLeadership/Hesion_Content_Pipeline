# To Adopt AI at Scale, Employees Need to Trust Agents

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-09-09T12:15:08Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/09/to-adopt-ai-at-scale-employees-need-to-trust-agents](https://hbr.org/2026/09/to-adopt-ai-at-scale-employees-need-to-trust-agents)

## Summary
Research shows that AI agent adoption at scale improves when organizations are transparent about what agents can and cannot do, building employee trust through explicit communication of capabilities and limitations.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Trust in technology adoption is fundamentally an organizational psychology challenge—employees' willingness to delegate tasks to AI agents depends on perceived reliability, transparency, and psychological safety around failure. This mirrors established research on trust formation in human-automation interaction.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should invest in structured transparency when deploying AI agents: clearly communicate what agents are designed to do, where they may fall short, and how human oversight is maintained. Building trust through honesty about limitations accelerates adoption far more than overselling capabilities.
