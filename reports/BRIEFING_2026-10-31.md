## Best Newsletter Candidate
**EqualAI CEO: Your AI assistant is acting in your name. Who signed off?** (Fortune). This has the deepest org psych angle of the batch: it's not just about AI tools but about diffused accountability — the classic organizational failure where "oversight was assumed rather than assigned." That's a rich frame for a 500+ word piece: Pete can walk through how this mirrors delegation failures that predate AI (unclear decision rights, no audit trail, no escalation path) and then apply the piece's four commitments — specific permission, assigned responsibility, user-perspective testing, and failure reporting — as a governance checklist CHROs and COOs can use right now as they roll out AI agents. It connects a timely tech story to timeless organizational design principles, which is exactly the kind of bridge that makes for a strong newsletter.
