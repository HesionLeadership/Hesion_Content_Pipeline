## Summary
Sarah Yager, OpenAI's first human rights and responsible deployment lead, discusses her role overseeing military AI applications amid the company's DoW contract, expressing concerns about AI in targeting, autonomous weapons, and the lack of diversity among decision-makers shaping AI policy.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story illustrates the organizational challenge of embedding an ethics/compliance function within a mission-driven tech company under commercial and government pressure, raising questions about whether such roles have genuine authority or serve as symbolic 'branding exercises,' and how insular leadership demographics can shape organizational blind spots.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure that ethics, compliance, or responsible-use roles are given real authority (e.g., direct access to engineers and product decisions) rather than symbolic positioning, and should actively diversify decision-making bodies to avoid groupthink and blind spots in high-stakes technology deployment.
