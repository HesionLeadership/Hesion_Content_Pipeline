# Marketing and Finance Teams Fight Over Budgets. A Shared View of Spending Can Fix That

## Summary
Marketing and finance teams frequently clash over budget allocation, with finance often playing the role of gatekeeper. The article argues that bringing finance into marketing planning early creates a shared view of spending that enables smarter investment and faster growth.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Cross-functional conflict between departments with competing goals (revenue growth vs. cost control) is a classic organizational tension rooted in functional silos, role identity, and misaligned incentive structures. Early collaboration reduces intergroup bias and fosters shared mental models.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design processes that embed cross-functional collaboration at the planning stage rather than the approval stage—shifting finance from a reactive gatekeeper role to a proactive strategic partner reduces friction, builds trust, and aligns departments around shared outcomes.
