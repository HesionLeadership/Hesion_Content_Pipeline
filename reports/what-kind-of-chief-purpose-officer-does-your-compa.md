# What Kind of Chief Purpose Officer Does Your Company Need?

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Tue, 01 Sep 2026 15:21:42 +0000

## Summary
Research based on interviews with 56 Chief Purpose Officers (CPOs) across industries from 2022-2025 explores the different types of purpose leadership roles companies need, examining how these leaders navigate strategic practices and organizational integration of corporate purpose.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The emergence of the CPO role reflects organizational efforts to institutionalize purpose-driven culture, raising questions about role clarity, strategic alignment, and whether dedicated purpose roles enhance or fragment organizational identity and employee meaning-making.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should carefully assess what type of purpose leadership fits their organization's maturity and strategic needs—whether purpose requires a dedicated executive role, integration into existing leadership functions, or a hybrid approach—to avoid creating symbolic positions that lack real influence.
