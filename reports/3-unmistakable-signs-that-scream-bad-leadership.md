# 3 Unmistakable Signs That Scream ‘Bad Leadership’

## Summary
The article identifies three unmistakable signs of bad leadership: (1) team members stop sharing ideas or speaking up, (2) high performers quietly start leaving, and (3) people only do the bare minimum. These behavioral signals in teams reflect deeper leadership failures.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly maps to established constructs in organizational psychology—psychological safety (silence), turnover intention among top talent, and disengagement/quiet quitting as responses to poor leadership. Leader behavior is a primary driver of team climate, and these three signals are well-documented downstream effects of destructive or ineffective leadership.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat declining voice behavior, high-performer attrition, and widespread disengagement as diagnostic indicators of leadership quality rather than individual employee problems. Implement pulse surveys measuring psychological safety, track voluntary turnover by manager, and monitor engagement metrics at the team level to identify leadership issues before they become systemic.
