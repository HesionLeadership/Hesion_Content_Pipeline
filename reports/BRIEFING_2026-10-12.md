## Top Story
**With AI productivity, your team is burning out** (Fast Company) (article not read) is the Top Story. It names a problem CHROs are about to face at scale: AI makes output look healthy even as people quietly drown in oversight and correction work. This directly challenges how organizations measure performance and matters because it reframes burnout detection as a metrics design problem, not just a wellness problem.

## Best Newsletter Candidate
**With AI productivity, your team is burning out** (Fast Company) (article not read) has the deepest org psych runway for a 500+ word piece. There's a lot to unpack: why traditional burnout signals (slowing output) fail in AI-augmented teams, how invisible labor like reviewing and correcting AI work gets unevenly distributed and unrewarded, and the trap of reallocating AI-saved time into more output instead of recovery. Pete could build a framework here—pairing quantitative productivity data with qualitative wellbeing checks, and naming/rewarding oversight work as real work—that's genuinely new advice for CHROs, not recycled burnout talking points.
