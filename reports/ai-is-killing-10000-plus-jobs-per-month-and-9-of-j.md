# AI is killing 10,000-plus jobs per month and 9% of jobs are at risk, analysts say

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Fri, 23 Oct 2026 10:24:44 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/10/23/ai-is-killing-10000-plus-jobs-per-month-and-9-of-jobs-are-at-risk/](https://fortune.com/2026/10/23/ai-is-killing-10000-plus-jobs-per-month-and-9-of-jobs-are-at-risk/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
AI is causing net job losses in the U.S. exceeding 10,000 roles per month, concentrated in accounting, advertising, office admin, and legal services, even as construction jobs rise due to data center buildouts. Goldman Sachs estimates 9% of workers could be temporarily displaced during the AI transition, based on a projected 15% productivity increase from AI adoption.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Large-scale workforce displacement from AI adoption raises questions about how organizations manage employee anxiety, reskilling, and trust during periods of technological disruption and uncertainty.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively build transparent AI transition plans—including reskilling pathways and clear communication about which roles are at risk—rather than letting displacement unfold reactively, which can erode trust and engagement even among employees not directly affected.
