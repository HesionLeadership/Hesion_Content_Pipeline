## Summary
Cisco deployed personalized AI agents to all 90,000 employees across the organization, representing a large-scale enterprise AI adoption initiative aimed at augmenting individual worker capabilities and productivity.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This represents a massive organizational change management challenge—deploying AI tools to an entire workforce simultaneously raises questions about technology acceptance, employee autonomy, skill displacement anxiety, and how organizations can foster adoption without triggering resistance or learned helplessness.

## Leadership Lesson
When rolling out transformative technology at scale, leaders must address both the technical deployment and the psychological readiness of the workforce. CHROs should anticipate varied adoption curves, invest in change management and upskilling, and monitor whether AI agents enhance or undermine employee agency and job crafting.
