# Don’t Set Up New Hires to Fail in Employee Orientation

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Mon, 21 Sep 2026 08:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/dr-gina-anderson/dont-set-up-new-hires-to-fail-in-employee-orientation/91407272](https://www.inc.com/dr-gina-anderson/dont-set-up-new-hires-to-fail-in-employee-orientation/91407272)

## Summary
The article identifies three common educational pitfalls in employee orientation that set new hires up to fail: information overload (cramming too much content into early sessions), lack of relevance (presenting generic material disconnected from the new hire's actual role), and passive learning formats (relying on lectures and slideshows rather than interactive or experiential approaches).

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Onboarding effectiveness is a well-studied area in organizational psychology, with research showing that structured, role-relevant socialization tactics significantly predict newcomer adjustment, job satisfaction, and retention. Poor orientation design undermines organizational socialization by failing to facilitate role clarity, self-efficacy, and social integration.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit their onboarding programs for cognitive overload, role irrelevance, and passive delivery—replacing them with spaced learning, role-specific content, and interactive experiences that accelerate newcomer productivity and reduce early turnover.
