# Goodyear Tire CEO Mark Stewart steps down as company continues turnaround plan

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Mon, 09 Nov 2026 14:56:47 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/goodyear-roger-wood-interim-ceo.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/09/goodyear-roger-wood-interim-ceo.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Goodyear Tire's CEO Mark Stewart stepped down effective immediately amid a stalled multiyear turnaround plan, with board member Roger Wood named interim CEO. The company missed its key goal of a 10% operating margin and reported a Q3 net loss, prompting a board-led strategic review and search for a permanent CEO.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the tension between short-tenure turnaround leadership and the time typically required for organizational transformation to show results, as well as board governance mechanisms (special committees, interim leadership) activated when performance targets are missed.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure turnaround mandates include realistic timelines and milestone checkpoints agreed upon with the board upfront, since misaligned expectations between a new CEO's transformation roadmap and board patience can trigger premature leadership churn that further destabilizes the change effort.
