# Morning Briefing — October 02, 2026

## This Week's Themes
Leadership psychology under pressure is the dominant thread this week — anxiety derailing transformation (MIT Sloan), misdiagnosed stress (HBR), and burnout cascading through DeepMind's R&D unit all point to the same root issue: leaders and systems failing to accurately read and respond to psychological strain. A second cluster centers on AI's organizational side effects — skill atrophy in beginners (Fast Company), accountability-masking behaviors (HBR), and the broader question of how automation reshapes professional judgment and trust. A third theme is structural fragility: single-point-of-failure risk (Fast Company), co-founder relationship tension (Fast Company), and the illusion of alignment on teams (Inc) all show how hidden organizational debt compounds silently until it breaks something visible. Airbnb's HR reinvention is the lone "build" story amid a week mostly about diagnosing and preventing breakdown. Net theme: organizations are good at launching change and tech adoption, bad at building the psychological and structural infrastructure to sustain it.
