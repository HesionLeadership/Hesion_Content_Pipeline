# Gender Equality to Take 120 Years Even as Gap Narrows, WEF Says

**Source:** Bloomberg  
**Published:** Wed, 16 Sep 2026 11:55:55 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-16/wef-says-gender-equality-to-take-120-years-video](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-16/wef-says-gender-equality-to-take-120-years-video)

## Summary
The WEF's annual gender gap report finds global gender parity is still 120 years away. WEF Managing Director Saadia Zahidi notes that during global crises, organizations tend to revert to traditional (male-dominated) leadership patterns, and this regression is already appearing in the AI sector.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Crisis-driven regression to traditional leadership archetypes reflects the 'think manager, think male' paradigm, where organizational stress triggers reliance on stereotypically masculine leadership models, undermining diversity gains. The observation about AI replicating these patterns raises questions about how algorithmic bias may institutionalize gender inequality in talent management systems.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should actively monitor whether crisis periods or major technological shifts (like AI adoption) are eroding diversity progress in leadership pipelines. Build structural safeguards—such as diverse succession slates, bias-audited AI hiring tools, and accountability metrics—that hold during periods of organizational stress, not just stability.

## Original Story Summary
WEF Managing Director Saadia Zahidi speaks to Francine Lacqua after their latest annual report on gender found that global gender parity remains more than a lifetime away for women, with current progress meaning it will still take 120 years to reach full equality.
