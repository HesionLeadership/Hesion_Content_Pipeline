**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
The article argues that AI investment outside the tech sector hasn't boosted profits because companies haven't redesigned their core operating models—workflows, approvals, and governance still assume human-paced work even where AI has taken over tasks. The piece lists four questions to gauge AI readiness: Cost per outcome, Adoption, Sequencing, and Model discipline.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates a classic socio-technical systems problem: introducing new technology without redesigning the surrounding work structure (roles, approvals, governance) traps gains at the individual level and prevents them from scaling to the organization.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat AI adoption as an operating-model redesign led directly by the CEO—not a delegated IT project—requiring redefined roles, approval authorities, and real-time governance before expecting enterprise-wide productivity gains.
