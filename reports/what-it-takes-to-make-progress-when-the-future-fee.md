## Summary
An HBR conversation with psychologist Martin Seligman explores how leaders can cultivate a sense of agency in their teams when the future feels uncertain and out of control, focusing on building positive work cultures through psychological empowerment.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Seligman's work on learned helplessness and its inverse—agency and learned optimism—directly applies to how organizational cultures either empower or paralyze employees during periods of uncertainty. Fostering agency at the team level is a core mechanism for sustaining engagement and adaptive performance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should intentionally design work environments that restore employees' sense of control and agency during turbulent times—through autonomy-supportive management practices, prospective thinking exercises, and reframing uncertainty as opportunity rather than threat.
