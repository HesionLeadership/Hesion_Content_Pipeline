# Stop Prompting AI. Start Directing It

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Wed, 05 Aug 2026 11:00:54 +0000  
**URL:** [https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/stop-prompting-ai-start-directing-it/](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/stop-prompting-ai-start-directing-it/)

## Summary
MIT Sloan Review article argues that leaders should shift from 'prompting' AI (giving simple queries) to 'directing' it, drawing on research identifying four pathways by which AI generates surprising insights during analytical work and examining how organizations function as algorithmic assemblages where AI systems reshape decision-making and workflows.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The shift from prompting to directing AI reflects a fundamental change in human-AI collaboration dynamics within organizations, requiring leaders to develop new cognitive frameworks and managerial competencies for orchestrating algorithmic systems rather than merely using them as tools.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should invest in building organizational capability around AI direction—not just AI literacy—by training leaders to think of AI as a collaborative partner requiring structured oversight, goal-setting, and iterative guidance, much like managing a high-performing but unpredictable team member.
