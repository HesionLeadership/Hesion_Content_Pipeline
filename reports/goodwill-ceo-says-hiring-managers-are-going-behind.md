# Goodwill CEO says hiring managers are going behind their bosses’ backs and looking for workers with college degrees, not skills

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sat, 21 Nov 2026 14:26:21 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/article/goodwill-ceo-hiring-managers-going-behind-bosses-backs-looking-for-workers-with-college-degrees/](https://fortune.com/article/goodwill-ceo-hiring-managers-going-behind-bosses-backs-looking-for-workers-with-college-degrees/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Goodwill CEO Steve Preston says that despite employers publicly dropping degree requirements in favor of skills-based hiring, many hiring managers continue to favor college-educated candidates, leaving non-degree holders—especially Gen Z—struggling to find jobs.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates a classic implementation gap between espoused organizational policy and enacted practice, driven by similarity-attraction bias (homophily) among hiring managers who favor candidates who resemble themselves educationally and culturally.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs must go beyond policy announcements and actively audit hiring manager behavior, train interviewers on bias mitigation, and build structured, skills-based assessment tools to ensure stated DEI and skills-first commitments actually translate into hiring outcomes.
