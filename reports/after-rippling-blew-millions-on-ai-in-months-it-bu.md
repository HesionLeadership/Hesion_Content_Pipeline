# After Rippling blew millions on AI in months, it built an employee ROI tool

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** Fri, 07 Aug 2026 21:30:11 +0000  
**URL:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/after-rippling-blew-millions-on-ai-in-months-it-built-an-employee-roi-tool/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/07/after-rippling-blew-millions-on-ai-in-months-it-built-an-employee-roi-tool/)

## Summary
After Rippling discovered it had spent millions on AI tools in just a few months without clear visibility into the spending, the company built and launched AI Spend Console, a product that tracks individual and team-level AI usage and spending across an organization.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story highlights the organizational challenge of resource accountability and visibility when adopting new technologies rapidly. When spending is decentralized and unmonitored, organizations face a principal-agent problem where individual usage patterns can diverge significantly from organizational priorities.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders must build transparency and accountability mechanisms into technology adoption from the start—not retroactively. CHROs and COOs should ensure that empowering employees with new tools (like AI) is paired with usage monitoring frameworks, not to restrict innovation but to align individual behavior with organizational resource strategy.
