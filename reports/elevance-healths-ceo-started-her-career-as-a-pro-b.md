## Summary
Gail Boudreaux, CEO of Elevance Health ($199B revenue), is the highest-ranking woman on the Global 500 and attributes her leadership development to her background as a collegiate and professional basketball player.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The transfer of competencies from team sports to executive leadership—including resilience, competitive drive, strategic thinking under pressure, and collaborative teamwork—represents a well-studied pathway in leadership development. Athletic backgrounds may cultivate transformational leadership traits such as goal orientation, discipline, and the ability to motivate diverse teams.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider how formative experiences outside traditional business pathways—such as competitive athletics—develop transferable leadership competencies like team coordination, performance under pressure, and resilience. Leadership development programs can be enriched by incorporating experiential learning that mirrors the intensity and collaborative demands of team sports.
