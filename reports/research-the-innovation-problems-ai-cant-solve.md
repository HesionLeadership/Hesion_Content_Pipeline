# Research: The Innovation Problems AI Can’t Solve

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-14T12:05:50Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/research-the-innovation-problems-ai-cant-solve](https://hbr.org/2026/08/research-the-innovation-problems-ai-cant-solve)

## Summary
Generative AI accelerates innovation processes but introduces critical problems it cannot solve: it narrows creative thinking by anchoring teams to AI-generated outputs, reinforces existing biases in data and assumptions, and creates psychological distance between innovation teams and their end customers, reducing empathetic understanding of real user needs.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
AI tools can create cognitive anchoring effects and conformity pressures within innovation teams, reducing the diversity of thought and deep customer empathy that drive breakthrough innovation. This reflects well-established research on groupthink, cognitive biases, and the importance of divergent thinking in creative problem-solving.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders overseeing innovation functions should implement structured safeguards—such as requiring human-led customer immersion before AI-assisted ideation, deliberately seeking disconfirming evidence, and training teams to recognize anchoring bias from AI outputs—to ensure AI augments rather than replaces the critical human judgment and empathy that fuel meaningful innovation.
