# High Performers, Take a Breath

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Wed, 02 Sep 2026 08:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/shawna-seldon/high-performers-take-a-breath/91398718](https://www.inc.com/shawna-seldon/high-performers-take-a-breath/91398718)

## Summary
The article argues that high-performing executives invest heavily in learning strategy, finance, and leadership but neglect self-regulation skills. A growing body of research suggests that learning to regulate emotions, stress responses, and physiological states (such as through breathwork) is critical for sustained executive performance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Self-regulation is a foundational competency in emotional intelligence and leadership effectiveness research. Leaders who cannot manage their own stress and emotional reactivity are more prone to poor decision-making, interpersonal conflict, and burnout, which cascades through organizational culture.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should integrate evidence-based self-regulation practices—such as breathwork, mindfulness, and stress management—into executive development programs, treating emotional and physiological self-management as a core leadership competency rather than a personal wellness add-on.
