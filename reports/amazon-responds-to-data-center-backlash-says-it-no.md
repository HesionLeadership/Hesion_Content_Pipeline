# Amazon responds to data center backlash, says it no longer uses NDAs

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** Sat, 03 Oct 2026 18:43:57 +0000

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Amazon AWS CEO Matt Garman announced the company no longer uses NDAs with government agencies on data center projects, as part of a broader blog post pushing back against community backlash over water usage, electricity costs, pollution, and lack of community benefits from data centers.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates a crisis of institutional trust, where corporate transparency moves (like ending NDAs) may be insufficient to overcome accumulated public skepticism rooted in perceived procedural unfairness (secrecy, lack of community input before permits were secured).

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that transparency announced after trust has eroded is often met with skepticism rather than relief; procedural justice (involving stakeholders before decisions are finalized) is more effective at building trust than post-hoc disclosure or defensive messaging.
