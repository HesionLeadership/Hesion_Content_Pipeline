# ASML’s CEO is putting his paycheck on the line for employee morale. It could help retain talent that’s nearly impossible to replace

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sat, 31 Oct 2026 10:42:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/10/31/asml-ceo-christophe-fouquet-executive-pay-employee-satisfaction/](https://fortune.com/2026/10/31/asml-ceo-christophe-fouquet-executive-pay-employee-satisfaction/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
ASML CEO Christophe Fouquet announced that executive pay, including his own, will now be tied to a new Workplace Experience Index derived from employee survey questions on trust, psychological safety, and management support. The move follows a drop in employee engagement from 78% to 73.5% after a turbulent year that included 1,700 layoffs and union pushback.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how organizational restructuring and perceived unfairness in executive compensation can erode psychological safety and trust, and how formally linking leadership incentives to engagement metrics can serve as a structural accountability mechanism to realign leadership behavior with employee experience.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider building employee sentiment metrics (trust in leadership, psychological safety, managerial support) directly into executive incentive structures, especially during or after restructuring, to signal accountability and counteract the erosion of trust that layoffs and change typically cause.
