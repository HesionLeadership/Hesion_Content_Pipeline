# How AI Creates a Capability Mirage

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Mon, 14 Sep 2026 11:00:15 +0000  
**URL:** [https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/how-ai-creates-a-capability-mirage/](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/how-ai-creates-a-capability-mirage/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
The article argues that generative AI tools create a 'capability mirage' in organizations—polished AI-generated outputs mask whether individuals actually possess the underlying skills, making it harder for leaders and teams to accurately assess competence. This erosion of accurate skill assessment damages interpersonal trust within teams.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This touches on signaling theory and trust formation in teams: when observable outputs no longer reliably signal underlying competence, team members lose the calibration cues needed to build accurate mental models of each other's abilities, undermining psychological safety and trust-based collaboration.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should shift performance evaluation away from output-only assessment toward process-based and competency-based evaluation (e.g., live problem-solving, explaining reasoning, stress-testing work), and should explicitly discuss AI-usage norms so that skill development and trust calibration aren't silently eroded by tool-assisted polish.

## Strength Score
**7/10** — The piece raises a genuine organizational psychology issue—how technology-mediated ambiguity in competence signals can erode trust and skill development—though it's fairly conceptual/speculative rather than grounded in specific empirical findings or case studies, and the 'mirage' framing is more evocative than rigorously evidenced.
