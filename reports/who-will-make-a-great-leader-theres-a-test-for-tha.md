## Summary
Harvard economist David Deming discusses his research developing science-based assessments for 'soft skills' like teamwork, management, and leadership, including findings that the Reading the Mind in the Eyes Test predicts team performance, and that people who most want to be managers actually perform worse than randomly assigned managers.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story directly engages organizational psychology by empirically testing how emotion-recognition ability and allocative decision-making skill predict real-world team and managerial performance, challenging self-selection as a proxy for leadership readiness.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should be skeptical of using self-nomination or expressed desire for promotion as a signal of managerial potential, and should instead consider validated assessments of social perception and resource-allocation skill when making promotion decisions.
