# 'What you see is what you pay' - why some US restaurants are banning tips

**Source:** BBC Business  
**Published:** Mon, 24 Aug 2026 23:04:17 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c4g36yze692o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c4g36yze692o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## Summary
Some US restaurants are eliminating tipping culture in favor of transparent, all-inclusive pricing, arguing that tips create unfair pay disparities between front-of-house and back-of-house staff and introduce income unpredictability for workers.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The tipping debate reflects core organizational justice concerns—specifically distributive and procedural justice—where compensation systems perceived as arbitrary or inequitable undermine employee morale, create status hierarchies, and contribute to turnover, particularly among kitchen staff who receive no tips despite essential contributions.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders redesigning compensation structures should consider how pay transparency and equity across roles affect team cohesion, retention, and perceptions of fairness; moving from variable, customer-determined pay to standardized wages can reduce intergroup conflict and signal organizational commitment to all employees, not just customer-facing ones.
