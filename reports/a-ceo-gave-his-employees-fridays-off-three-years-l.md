# A CEO gave his employees Fridays off. Three years later, revenue is up about 160%

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sun, 08 Nov 2026 10:00:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/08/ceo-four-day-workweek-fridays-off-brick-media-revenue/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/08/ceo-four-day-workweek-fridays-off-brick-media-revenue/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Brick Media CEO Jake Kurtz moved employees to a permanent four-day, 36-hour workweek in 2023 without cutting pay, and the company has since seen revenue grow roughly 160% over three years alongside sharply reduced voluntary turnover and workforce growth from 15 to 25 employees.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how reduced work hours combined with retained workforce stability can increase tacit knowledge efficiency (fewer onboarding/communication costs, faster informal problem-solving) and strengthen intrinsic motivation and organizational commitment tied to work-life balance rather than compensation alone.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat retention and workload management as core efficiency levers, not just culture perks: capping client/task loads per employee and investing in schedule flexibility can lower turnover costs and improve institutional knowledge retention, which compounds into measurable business performance over time.
