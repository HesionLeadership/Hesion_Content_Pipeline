# Ex-Disney CEO Bob Chapek says he raised concerns with the board 'weekly' during Iger power battle

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Mon, 28 Sep 2026 16:02:17 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/disney-bob-chapek-bob-iger-power-battle-board.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/disney-bob-chapek-bob-iger-power-battle-board.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Former Disney CEO Bob Chapek says he raised concerns weekly with Disney's board about then-Executive Chairman Bob Iger allegedly undermining him during a power struggle, but the board dismissed his concerns as 'Bob being Bob.' Chapek was ousted in 2022 and replaced by Iger.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the failure of governance structures to manage co-leadership arrangements and role ambiguity, where an emeritus/transitional executive retains informal power that undermines the formal successor, creating a dysfunctional dual-authority structure.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and boards must clearly define authority boundaries and enforce accountability when transitioning leadership, especially in 'chairman emeritus' or advisory arrangements; failing to act on repeated, credible signals of sabotage or role conflict erodes trust in governance and destabilizes the new leader's authority.
