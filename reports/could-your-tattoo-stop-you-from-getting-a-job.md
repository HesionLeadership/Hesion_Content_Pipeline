The story examines how tattoos continue to affect hiring decisions and workplace acceptance in the UK, despite a quarter of the population having at least one tattoo, including the new prime minister. ## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights appearance-based discrimination and implicit bias in hiring and promotion decisions, raising questions about how organizations define 'professionalism' and whether such norms systematically exclude qualified candidates based on irrelevant personal characteristics.

## Leadership Lesson

Defining 'professional appearance' too narrowly can signal an exclusionary culture that repels younger talent demographics.
