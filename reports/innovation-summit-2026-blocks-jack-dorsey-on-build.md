## Summary
Jack Dorsey discusses reorganizing Block around functional disciplines and now around 'intelligence,' flattening hierarchy into three roles (individual contributor, directly responsible individual, and coach) to speed decision-making using AI-ingestible organizational data.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how organizational structure functions as an information-processing technology, and how flattening hierarchy and redefining managerial roles (from command-and-control to coaching) can increase employee agency and decision speed, though it also raises questions about alignment, credibility costs, and psychological impact of disruptive restructuring.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that AI-enabled transparency can reduce reliance on hierarchical communication, but success depends on redefining manager roles as coaches who build judgment and craft mastery, and on tolerating short-term credibility loss when driving necessary structural change.
