# Mavi bets on the AI boom creating demand for a new kind of accountant

**Published:** Mon, 28 Sep 2026 15:26:46 +0000

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Mavi, an AI-powered talent marketplace co-founded by Molly Liu and Aman Puri, has emerged from stealth with $4M in seed funding to connect U.S. companies with AI-proficient global accounting and finance talent, arguing that AI is shrinking entry-level roles while increasing demand for judgment-heavy mid-to-senior talent.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights a talent pipeline disruption problem: as AI automates entry-level accounting work, the traditional pathway for developing mid-level expertise erodes, while remaining senior staff face intensified cognitive and judgment demands ('do more with less').

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively redesign career ladders and skill-development pathways now, since automating away entry-level roles can hollow out the pipeline that produces future senior judgment-based talent—requiring new models for building expertise outside traditional apprenticeship structures.
