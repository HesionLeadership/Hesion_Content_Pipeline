# Morning Briefing — October 29, 2026

## This Week's Themes
The dominant theme this week is the gap between AI adoption and organizational readiness: **How readiness debt is affecting organizations** (Fast Company) (article not read), **AI Is Thriving at Capital One** (MIT Sloan Review), and **AI is everywhere except in the GDP numbers — here is what triggers the break** (Fortune) all argue that infrastructure, role clarity, and workflow redesign—not tool deployment—determine whether AI actually pays off. A closely related thread is trust and judgment under AI-driven pressure: **Your Company’s Fraud Defenses Aren’t Ready for AI Threats** (HBR), **NSA official turned founder: AI multiplies attackers’ expertise while defenders are still short on talent** (Fortune), and **AI delivers ‘zero financial payoff’ to most companies, Apollo economist says** (TechCrunch) all point to human judgment and verification as the scarce resource AI can't replace. A third theme is workforce anxiety and trust during disruption, seen in **Leaders Need to Grapple with Employee Ambivalence on AI** (HBR), **AI is killing 10,000-plus jobs per month and 9% of jobs are at risk, analysts say** (Fortune), and **American Express is cutting 5% of its workforce to 'prepare for AI'** (CNBC Business). Finally, several stories explore how leader behavior shapes culture at a micro level, including **Careful, managers. Your mood affects your whole team’s performance** (Fast Company) and **What Interruptions Say About Your Organizational Culture** (MIT Sloan Review).

## Top Story
**Your Company’s Fraud Defenses Aren’t Ready for AI Threats** (HBR) is the clear pick—it's the only fully verified story today and it lands a sharp point: organizations routinely substitute trust in a brand or name for direct verification, and AI is about to make that shortcut far easier to exploit. For CHROs and COOs, this reframes fraud defense as an organizational design problem—specifically, the handoff points between finance, procurement, HR, and operations where "trust" quietly replaces verification.

## Best LinkedIn Post Candidate
