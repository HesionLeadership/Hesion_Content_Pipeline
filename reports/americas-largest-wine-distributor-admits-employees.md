# America’s Largest Wine Distributor Admits Employees Bribed Retailers. Now It Will Pay $12.5 Million

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Mon, 14 Sep 2026 09:39:01 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/lucia-auerbach/americas-largest-wine-distributor-admits-employees-bribed-retailers-pay-12-million-dollars/91404450](https://www.inc.com/lucia-auerbach/americas-largest-wine-distributor-admits-employees-bribed-retailers-pay-12-million-dollars/91404450)

## Summary
Southern Glazer's Wine and Spirits, the largest U.S. wine and spirits distributor, admitted that employees bribed retailers with cash, luxury goods, and trips, then concealed the payments through false invoices. The company will pay $12.5 million to resolve federal charges.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case illustrates how systemic ethical failures can become embedded in organizational culture when compliance controls are weak and employees face pressure to meet sales targets, leading to normalized deviance where bribery becomes routine business practice.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs must build robust compliance cultures that go beyond written policies—including strong whistleblower protections, regular auditing of expense and invoice processes, and ensuring that performance incentives don't inadvertently reward unethical behavior. When misconduct is concealed through false documentation, it signals a systemic failure in internal controls and ethical leadership, not just individual bad actors.
