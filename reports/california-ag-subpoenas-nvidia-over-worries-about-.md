## Summary
California's AG has subpoenaed Nvidia to investigate whether former CEO Jensen Huang's alleged relationship with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset led to leaks of advanced GPU technology or confidential information, and whether Nvidia misled investors about these risks. The investigation follows Huang's resignation after revelations of multiple affairs with company employees, alongside parallel SEC and congressional probes.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case highlights classic power-abuse dynamics—a founder-CEO engaging in relationships with subordinates and an external contact with potential national security implications—compounded by apparent board oversight gaps and organizational over-dependence on a single charismatic leader (seen in employee/investor pressure to reinstate him despite the scandal).

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat founder/executive relationships with direct reports or external parties with conflict-of-interest potential as material governance risks, not personal matters—requiring clear disclosure policies, board-level oversight, and succession plans that reduce single-leader dependency before a crisis forces the issue.
