# Anthropic’s S-1 shows $16.3 billion in revenue and an $80.7 billion net loss

## Summary
Anthropic's IPO prospectus reveals $16.3 billion in revenue and an $80.7 billion net loss (mostly accounting charges) for H1 2026, with improving operating margins and positive cash flow. The filing's risk factors include warnings that employees who joined for the company's safety mission could become disillusioned and leave if they feel Anthropic isn't living up to its values, referencing a recent employee resignation over AI safety concerns.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The filing explicitly identifies mission-values alignment as a material business risk, highlighting how organizations built on strong ideological or ethical missions face unique vulnerability to employee disengagement and attrition when perceived value-drift occurs.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs at mission-driven organizations should treat psychological contract violations (perceived gaps between stated values and actual practice) as a tangible retention and morale risk, not just a PR concern—proactively auditing whether operational decisions remain congruent with the mission that attracted talent in the first place.
