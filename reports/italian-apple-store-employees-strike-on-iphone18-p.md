## Summary
Employees at Apple's Italian retail stores went on strike on the launch day of the iPhone 18 Pro, timing their labor action to maximize visibility and disrupt a high-profile product release.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Strategic strike timing on a flagship launch day reflects employee leverage tactics and signals deeper issues around worker satisfaction, compensation, or labor relations within Apple's retail operations in Italy.

## Leadership Lesson
Retail and frontline workforce grievances don't disappear during peak business moments—they amplify. CHROs should proactively address labor relations concerns before high-stakes events, as employees will strategically use maximum-impact moments to make their voices heard.
