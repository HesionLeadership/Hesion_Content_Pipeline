# A $5 Billion AI Startup’s New Rule for Employees: Writing Should Take Longer Than Reading

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Wed, 12 Aug 2026 15:25:12 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/saleah-blancaflor/a-5-billion-ai-startups-new-rule-for-employees-writing-should-take-longer-than-reading/91389824](https://www.inc.com/saleah-blancaflor/a-5-billion-ai-startups-new-rule-for-employees-writing-should-take-longer-than-reading/91389824)

## Summary
Clay, a $5 billion AI startup, has established an internal rule that employees should spend more time writing communications than recipients spend reading them. The policy emphasizes thoughtful, clear communication and reflects the company's belief that AI tools should enhance thinking quality rather than just increase output speed.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This policy reflects research on cognitive load theory and communication quality in organizations—shifting the burden of clarity to the sender reduces collective information processing costs and signals a culture that values deliberate thinking over rapid output.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders should establish explicit norms around communication quality, especially as AI tools make it easier to generate low-effort, high-volume content. Setting standards that prioritize reader experience over writer convenience can reduce organizational noise, improve decision-making quality, and prevent the 'AI slop' problem where easy content generation degrades collective attention.
