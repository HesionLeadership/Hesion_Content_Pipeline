# Experience Doesn’t Automatically Create Confidence. New Research Finds How to Bridge the Gap

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Mon, 14 Sep 2026 08:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/scott-hutcheson/experience-confidence-research/91399944](https://www.inc.com/scott-hutcheson/experience-confidence-research/91399944)

## Summary
New research suggests that leadership confidence doesn't stem from accumulating experience alone or positive thinking, but rather from leaders' ability to make meaning of their experiences through structured reflection and sensemaking processes.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This aligns with research on reflective practice and experiential learning theory—experience alone is insufficient for development; leaders must engage in deliberate meaning-making to convert experience into self-efficacy and confident action.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design leadership development programs that go beyond exposure-based learning (stretch assignments, rotations) and incorporate structured reflection, coaching, and sensemaking practices that help leaders extract confidence-building insights from their experiences.
