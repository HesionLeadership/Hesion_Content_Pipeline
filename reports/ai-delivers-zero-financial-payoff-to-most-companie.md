# AI delivers ‘zero financial payoff’ to most companies, Apollo economist says

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** Sun, 25 Oct 2026 16:57:32 +0000  
**URL:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/25/ai-delivers-zero-financial-payoff-to-most-companies-according-to-apollo-economist/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/25/ai-delivers-zero-financial-payoff-to-most-companies-according-to-apollo-economist/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Apollo chief economist Torsten Slok finds that AI investment has produced no measurable improvement in profit margins or revenue per worker for S&P 500 companies outside the major tech firms, arguing that business judgment, trust, and culture—not raw computation—drive financial performance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The piece implicitly argues that organizational performance depends on human judgment, accountability, and cultural factors like trust and fairness, which AI cannot replicate—highlighting the gap between technological capability and the social/psychological infrastructure needed to convert it into value.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that AI tools alone won't drive productivity gains without parallel investment in decision-making processes, accountability structures, and cultural alignment; technology adoption must be paired with organizational design changes, not treated as a standalone solution.
