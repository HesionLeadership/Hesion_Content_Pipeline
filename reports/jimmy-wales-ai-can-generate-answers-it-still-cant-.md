# Jimmy Wales: ‘AI can generate answers. It still can’t earn trust’

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sun, 16 Aug 2026 07:30:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/08/16/jimmy-wales-ai-can-generate-answers-it-still-cant-earn-trust/](https://fortune.com/2026/08/16/jimmy-wales-ai-can-generate-answers-it-still-cant-earn-trust/)

## Summary
Jimmy Wales discusses how Wikipedia's model of community-driven trust and transparency offers lessons that AI cannot replicate. He argues that while AI can generate answers, the human editorial process and institutional credibility behind Wikipedia are what earn trust.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights how trust in organizations is built through transparent processes, community governance, and collective accountability rather than through technological capability alone—a core principle in organizational trust theory.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders should recognize that trust is not a product of output quality alone but of the processes, transparency, and human accountability structures behind that output. CHROs and COOs should invest in governance frameworks and community-based decision-making to build durable institutional trust, especially as AI tools proliferate.
