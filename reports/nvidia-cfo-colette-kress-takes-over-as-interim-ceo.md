## Summary
Nvidia named CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO after founder Jensen Huang resigned following an admitted affair that raised national security concerns; the company had no publicly disclosed succession plan, and the board may seek a technologist as permanent successor, with Ian Buck mentioned as a potential internal candidate.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the risks of founder-centric leadership without formal succession planning, and how organizations lean on trusted, long-tenured executives (like a 13-year CFO) to provide stability and credibility during abrupt, reputation-driven leadership crises.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and boards should build and maintain CEO succession plans—even for high-performing founder-led companies—so that leadership transitions triggered by scandal or unexpected departure don't leave the organization without a clear, vetted path forward.
