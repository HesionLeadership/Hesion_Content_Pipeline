## Summary
Inc presents 29 small cultural rituals that help organizational culture take root. While the full list isn't available from the summary alone, these typically include practices like starting meetings with personal check-ins, celebrating small wins publicly, onboarding rituals, peer recognition moments, naming meeting-free days, sharing customer stories, rotating meeting facilitation, weekly gratitude practices, founder/leader office hours, failure debriefs without blame, new hire welcome traditions, team retrospectives, and other micro-habits that reinforce values through repeated behavior.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Organizational culture is sustained not through mission statements but through repeated micro-rituals that signal belonging, psychological safety, and shared identity. Research on organizational socialization and sensemaking shows that small, consistent behavioral cues shape norms far more effectively than top-down declarations.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should invest in designing and protecting small, repeatable cultural rituals—such as structured recognition moments, onboarding traditions, and reflective team practices—rather than relying solely on formal culture initiatives. These micro-practices create the behavioral infrastructure that makes stated values real and observable.
