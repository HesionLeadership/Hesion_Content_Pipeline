# After 78 Years on Air, This Baltimore TV Station Cut Its Anchors. What Replaced Them Has Critics Worried

## Summary
Baltimore TV station WMAR, after 78 years on air, eliminated its local news anchors as part of sweeping layoffs by parent company Scripps, replacing them with AI-generated or automated content. Former and current employees are pushing back against the decision.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case illustrates the organizational trauma and resistance that emerges when companies replace human workers with automation, particularly in roles with high public visibility and identity attachment. It also raises questions about how leadership communicates and manages workforce reduction decisions that fundamentally alter organizational identity.

## Leadership Lesson
When implementing automation-driven workforce reductions, leaders must anticipate the psychological impact on remaining employees, institutional identity loss, and stakeholder backlash. CHROs should develop transparent change management strategies that address survivor syndrome, protect employer brand, and honestly assess whether cost savings justify the erosion of trust and organizational culture.
