# 'I've had to Botox my CV': Are AI recruitment tools affecting women's careers?

**Source:** BBC Business  
**Published:** Thu, 06 Aug 2026 09:42:17 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cyvl8y141e4o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cyvl8y141e4o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## Summary
AI recruitment tools may be systematically disadvantaging women, particularly those returning to the workforce after career breaks, by penalizing resume gaps and filtering out candidates based on criteria that disproportionately affect women. Women report having to 'Botox' their CVs—smoothing over gaps and downplaying breaks—to get past algorithmic screening.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story highlights how algorithmic bias in selection systems can perpetuate and amplify existing gender inequities in hiring, raising fundamental questions about procedural justice and adverse impact in technologically mediated personnel selection.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs must audit AI recruitment tools for adverse impact against protected groups, particularly women returners, and ensure that automated screening criteria do not encode structural biases—such as penalizing career gaps—that undermine diversity goals and violate principles of fair selection.
