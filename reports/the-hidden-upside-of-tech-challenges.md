# The Hidden Upside of Tech Challenges

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Wed, 21 Oct 2026 11:00:48 +0000  
**URL:** [https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/the-hidden-upside-of-tech-challenges/](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/the-hidden-upside-of-tech-challenges/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
New research distinguishes between draining 'technology hindrances' and stimulating 'technology challenges,' showing that tech stress isn't uniformly negative—when hindrances are mitigated, new tech challenges can boost employee learning, motivation, and engagement. The researchers propose a three-part approach for managers to minimize hindrances while cultivating constructive tech challenges.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The article applies the challenge-hindrance stressor framework from occupational health psychology, showing that employees' cognitive appraisal of a stressor (as threat vs. opportunity) determines whether technostress harms or enhances performance, satisfaction, and retention.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should first systematically remove hindrance stressors (tool complexity, surveillance, notification overload) before rolling out new technology, then intentionally frame and scaffold new tech adoption as growth opportunities—through training, autonomy, and clear purpose—rather than letting employees experience unmanaged ambivalence.
