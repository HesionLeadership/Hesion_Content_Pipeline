## Summary
Security experts warn that AI industry executives, founders, and employees with access to sensitive information are increasingly vulnerable to honey-trap schemes, exacerbated by AI tools that make crafting fake personas and backstories cheap and easy. The piece follows allegations that Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang fell for such a scheme, and notes that company culture—particularly whether employees feel safe questioning leadership or unusual requests—is a key defense factor.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights how 'executive exceptionalism' and deference to authority create blind spots that make leaders and insiders more exploitable, while psychologically safe cultures that permit questioning authority figures serve as a organizational defense mechanism against manipulation.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build security awareness into culture by explicitly removing executive exemptions from scrutiny, training staff to recognize social engineering patterns, and fostering psychological safety so employees feel empowered to flag unusual requests—including those involving the CEO—without fear of repercussion.
