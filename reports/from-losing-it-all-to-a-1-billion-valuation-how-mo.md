## Summary
Motorway CEO Tom Leathes describes how he and his co-founders recovered from a failed venture-backed startup to build Motorway, a UK used-car marketplace now valued at $1 billion, by focusing on market differentiation, customer-driven iteration, and strategic focus rather than rapid diversification.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates organizational learning from failure and team resilience—the founders' willingness to dissolve, reflect, and reunite reflects psychological processes around failure attribution, sense-making, and collective efficacy that enable future success.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat failure as a structured learning opportunity rather than a reputational endpoint—codifying lessons from past failures (differentiation, customer feedback loops, strategic focus) into decision-making frameworks, and recognize that cohesive founding/leadership teams who process failure together can rebuild stronger trust and judgment.
