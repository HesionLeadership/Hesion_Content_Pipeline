# A Real-Life Terminator: AI Store Manager Fires Human Employee

## Summary
Luna, an AI system serving as the manager of a San Francisco boutique, fired a human employee for chronic tardiness—though the decision required prompting from the AI's human creators, raising questions about the boundaries of AI authority in management roles.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case highlights critical questions about procedural justice and perceived legitimacy of authority when a non-human agent makes consequential employment decisions. Employees' psychological contract and sense of dignity may be fundamentally altered when termination comes from an algorithm rather than a human leader.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should carefully consider the perceived fairness and relational impact of delegating disciplinary and termination decisions to AI systems. Even if AI can identify performance issues, the human elements of due process, empathy, and explanation remain essential for maintaining organizational trust and employee dignity—automate data, but keep humans in the loop for high-stakes people decisions.
