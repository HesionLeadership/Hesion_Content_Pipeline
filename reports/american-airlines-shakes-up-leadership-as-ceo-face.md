## Summary
American Airlines CEO Robert Isom is shaking up the airline's leadership team amid mounting pressure to close a significant profit gap with competitors Delta and United. The restructuring includes executive departures and role changes as the company attempts to recover from a failed distribution strategy that alienated corporate travel agencies.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates how strategic failures cascade into leadership accountability and organizational restructuring. The CEO's decision to overhaul the leadership team rather than step down himself raises questions about attribution of failure and whether reshuffling direct reports addresses the root causes of strategic missteps versus serving as symbolic action.

## Leadership Lesson
When a major strategic initiative fails, leaders must critically assess whether leadership changes represent genuine corrective action or scapegoating. CHROs should ensure restructuring efforts address systemic decision-making failures—such as flawed assumptions about market dynamics—rather than simply replacing personnel while preserving the same decision-making culture.
