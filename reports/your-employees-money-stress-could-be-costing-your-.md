## Summary
The article argues that employees' personal financial stress carries over into the workplace, negatively affecting productivity, engagement, and retention. It advocates for employer-sponsored financial wellness programs as a strategic business investment.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Financial stress is a significant source of cognitive load and distraction that undermines employee performance and well-being—consistent with conservation of resources theory and research on stress spillover from personal to work domains.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that employee financial wellness is not a personal matter outside the organization's scope—offering financial literacy programs, emergency savings support, and financial planning resources can reduce absenteeism, presenteeism, and turnover while improving engagement.
