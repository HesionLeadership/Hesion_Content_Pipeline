## Summary
Despite OpenAI offering unprecedented stock-based compensation averaging $1.5 million per employee, the company still experienced high-profile defections to rivals like Meta. The story examines what factors beyond compensation drive retention of top talent in competitive industries.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the well-established finding that extrinsic rewards alone are insufficient for retaining high-performers; intrinsic motivators such as autonomy, purpose, growth opportunities, and organizational culture play critical roles in employee retention decisions.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that compensation is a necessary but insufficient retention lever—leaders must invest in meaningful work design, career development pathways, psychological safety, and organizational identity to retain key talent, especially in hyper-competitive talent markets.
