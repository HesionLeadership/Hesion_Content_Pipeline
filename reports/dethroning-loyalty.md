# Dethroning Loyalty

## Summary
The article from MIT Sloan Review examines the problematic nature of loyalty as a leadership value, arguing that as economic uncertainty empowers more authoritarian leadership styles, organizations need to critically reassess how loyalty demands can become toxic and undermine healthy organizational dynamics.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Demands for loyalty in organizations can suppress dissent, enable authoritarian leadership, and create psychologically unsafe environments where employees prioritize allegiance over ethical behavior and honest feedback. This dynamic is especially pronounced during economic downturns when power asymmetries between leaders and followers increase.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should distinguish between earned commitment (built through trust and fairness) and demanded loyalty (enforced through fear and power), and actively design cultures that reward candor and constructive dissent rather than blind allegiance—especially during periods of organizational stress or market uncertainty.
