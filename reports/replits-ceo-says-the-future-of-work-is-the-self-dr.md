# Replit’s CEO says the future of work is the ‘self-driving company’

**Published:** Fri, 16 Oct 2026 12:55:09 +0000  
**URL:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/16/replits-ceo-says-the-future-of-work-is-the-self-driving-company/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/16/replits-ceo-says-the-future-of-work-is-the-self-driving-company/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Replit CEO Amjad Masad describes his vision of a 'self-driving company' where AI agents handle routine work (like initial support ticket triaging) while humans focus on judgment, taste, and architecture decisions. He argues this shift makes work more creative and less boring rather than simply eliminating jobs.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates job redesign and task reallocation theory—as AI absorbs routine cognitive labor, human roles shift toward judgment-intensive and creative work, potentially increasing job enrichment but also requiring new skill development and changing what 'contribution' means at work.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively redesign job roles and performance metrics around judgment, strategic thinking, and creative problem-solving as AI absorbs routine tasks—waiting for this shift to happen organically risks disorientation and skills mismatches among employees unprepared for the change.
