## Summary
Jordan Belfort, convicted felon and 'Wolf of Wall Street,' has launched an AI voice startup called Tone with investor Shervin Pishevar, applying his sales 'tonality' techniques to AI-generated voices for sales calls and audiobook narration. The interview explores the company's technology, customer base, and addresses skepticism about trusting a platform built by founders with controversial pasts.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates the 'halo effect' and charismatic leadership dynamics: despite fraud convictions and documented harm to victims, Belfort's personal magnetism continues to attract investors and customers, echoing the same psychological mechanisms that drew people to Adam Neumann despite red flags.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs evaluating founder-led ventures or high-charisma leaders should separate personal magnetism from track record and build structured due-diligence processes (reference checks, ethics reviews, third-party verification) that aren't swayed by persuasive communication style alone.
