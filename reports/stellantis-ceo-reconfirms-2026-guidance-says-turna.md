# Stellantis CEO reconfirms 2026 guidance, says turnaround plan continues as automaker's shares hit new low

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Wed, 30 Sep 2026 20:15:48 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/stellantis-guidance-turnaround-plan.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/stellantis-guidance-turnaround-plan.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Stellantis CEO Antonio Filosa reaffirmed the company's 2026 financial guidance and 2028 cash flow targets despite shares hitting an all-time low (down ~60% this year), as the automaker continues a $70 billion turnaround plan built around sharper brand management, new investments, and regional empowerment rather than breaking up its 14-brand portfolio. An RBC analyst suggested a company break-up remains a plausible longer-term scenario despite leadership's public commitment to keeping it together.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the tension between leader conviction/consistency signaling (reaffirming targets to maintain stakeholder confidence) and the psychological risk of escalation of commitment to a chosen strategic path despite mounting external evidence (falling stock, analyst skepticism) suggesting a different course.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that reaffirming strategic commitment can build short-term credibility, but leaders must pair conviction with visible, measurable proof points (not just restated targets) to avoid being perceived as doubling down defensively rather than adapting intelligently.
