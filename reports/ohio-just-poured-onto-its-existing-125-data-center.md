## Summary
Ohio voters in 14+ municipalities approved bans or restrictions on data center development in Tuesday's election, adding to 125 existing local moratoriums and reflecting broad bipartisan opposition tied to environmental, grid, and transparency concerns. Experts suggest this could signal a national trend pushing ratepayer protections and grid safeguards onto state policy agendas.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how corporate expansion without community engagement or transparent communication breeds distrust and backlash, echoing organizational psychology findings on procedural justice—stakeholders accept unfavorable outcomes more readily when they perceive fair process and voice, which was notably absent here.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs leading large infrastructure or expansion projects should proactively engage local stakeholders, communicate transparently about costs/benefits, and build in perceived fairness and voice early—failing to do so, as seen with data center developers operating 'in secrecy,' invites organized resistance and reputational damage even when the underlying investment could be beneficial.
