# Nearly 1 in 3 Workers Admit Sabotaging Their Company’s AI—Here’s Why

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Sat, 01 Aug 2026 15:31:48 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/kevin-haynes/nearly-1-in-3-workers-admit-sabotaging-their-companys-ai-heres-why/91383505](https://www.inc.com/kevin-haynes/nearly-1-in-3-workers-admit-sabotaging-their-companys-ai-heres-why/91383505)

## Summary
Nearly one in three workers admit to sabotaging their company's AI adoption efforts, including faking AI usage, deliberately resisting new tools, and actively undermining implementation. Employee fears about job displacement, reduced compensation, and loss of professional identity are driving this resistance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects classic technology resistance dynamics amplified by existential threat perception—when employees view AI as threatening their job security and professional worth, they engage in counterproductive work behaviors as a form of self-preservation and protest. The gap between organizational mandates and employee psychological readiness creates a fertile ground for covert resistance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs must treat AI adoption as a change management challenge, not just a technology rollout. Leaders should proactively address employee fears through transparent communication about how roles will evolve rather than be eliminated, involve employees in co-designing AI integration, and create psychological safety so concerns can be voiced openly rather than expressed through sabotage.
