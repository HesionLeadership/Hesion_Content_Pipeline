## Summary
A former SK Hynix employee was sentenced to jail for leaking proprietary semiconductor technology information to a Chinese company, highlighting the ongoing risk of trade secret theft in the competitive chip industry.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case underscores the critical importance of insider threat management, employee loyalty, and organizational safeguards around intellectual property. It raises questions about what organizational conditions—compensation dissatisfaction, disengagement, or inadequate security culture—may contribute to employees betraying their employer's trust.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should invest in robust trade secret protection programs that combine technical controls with cultural interventions—including competitive retention strategies, exit interview intelligence, non-compete enforcement, and fostering a strong psychological contract so employees feel valued enough not to seek outside opportunities for monetizing proprietary knowledge.
