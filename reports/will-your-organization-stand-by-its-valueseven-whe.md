# Will Your Organization Stand By Its Values—Even When It’s Hard?

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-09-16T12:15:09Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/09/will-your-organization-stand-by-its-values-even-when-its-hard](https://hbr.org/2026/09/will-your-organization-stand-by-its-values-even-when-its-hard)

## Summary
The article examines how the true test of organizational values occurs when upholding them carries tangible costs—such as financial loss, public backlash, or internal friction—and argues that leaders must commit to values-based decision-making even under pressure.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses the gap between espoused values and enacted values in organizations, a well-documented phenomenon in organizational behavior research. When leaders abandon stated values under pressure, it erodes psychological safety, trust, and employee engagement.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build accountability structures and decision-making frameworks that explicitly reference organizational values during high-stakes situations, ensuring values are stress-tested rather than aspirational. Leaders who visibly uphold values at personal or organizational cost build deeper trust and long-term cultural resilience.
