## Summary
FIFPRO Europe's chief argues that FIFA needs structural and organizational reform rather than a debate over who should lead, emphasizing systemic issues over personality-driven leadership discussions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects a classic tension in organizational change management: the tendency to focus on leadership succession rather than addressing deeper structural and governance deficiencies. Research suggests that systemic reforms often yield more sustainable improvements than leadership changes alone.

## Leadership Lesson
When an organization faces chronic dysfunction, CHROs and COOs should resist the temptation to treat leadership replacement as a silver bullet. Instead, prioritize structural governance reforms, accountability mechanisms, and stakeholder engagement processes that outlast any single leader's tenure.
