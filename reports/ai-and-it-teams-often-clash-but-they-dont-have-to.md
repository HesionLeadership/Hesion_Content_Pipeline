## Summary
The article examines how AI and IT teams commonly clash within organizations and profiles three companies that successfully resolved these conflicts through structural and cultural interventions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story directly addresses intergroup conflict and cross-functional collaboration challenges that arise when new technological capabilities (AI) disrupt established team boundaries, roles, and resource allocation—a classic organizational design and group dynamics issue.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively design governance structures, shared goals, and communication channels between AI and IT teams before conflicts escalate, treating the integration as an organizational design challenge rather than a purely technical one.
