## Summary
The article examines how AI and IT teams frequently clash within organizations and profiles how three companies resolved these common conflicts through structural, cultural, and governance approaches.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a classic intergroup conflict and cross-functional collaboration challenge, where differing priorities, professional identities, and resource competition between AI and IT teams create organizational friction that must be managed through deliberate structural and relational interventions.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively design governance structures, shared goals, and communication norms between AI and IT teams rather than waiting for conflicts to emerge—treating cross-functional tension as a structural design problem, not just an interpersonal one.
