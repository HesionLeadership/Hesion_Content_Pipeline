# AI Is Thriving at Capital One

## Summary
Capital One, a long-time leader in data analytics, is now applying AI across software development, fraud reduction, risk/compliance auditing, and customer service, with leaders emphasizing long-term transformation over short-term ROI and heavy investment in training and data infrastructure to support adoption.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how successful technology adoption depends on organizational readiness factors—training, role clarity, and data/process infrastructure—rather than the technology itself, reflecting principles of sociotechnical systems theory and change management.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that AI transformation succeeds when leadership invests heavily in employee training and capability-building alongside tool deployment, rather than simply distributing new technology and expecting adoption; pairing technical rollout with workforce development ('tradecraft') is critical to realizing productivity gains.
