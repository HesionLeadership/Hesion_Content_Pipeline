# Workers are ‘job hugging’ in a stagnant labor market, but growing resentment means they could bail as soon as the next Great Resignation comes

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Mon, 18 Aug 2025 17:59:45 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/what-is-job-hugging-next-great-resignation/](https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/what-is-job-hugging-next-great-resignation/)

## Summary
Workers are increasingly 'job hugging'—staying in their current roles due to a stagnant labor market and economic uncertainty—despite growing resentment toward their employers. This pent-up dissatisfaction suggests a potential mass exodus when labor market conditions improve, echoing the Great Resignation pattern.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the critical distinction between voluntary and involuntary retention: employees who stay due to lack of alternatives (continuance commitment) rather than genuine attachment (affective commitment) represent a ticking organizational risk. The concept of 'job embeddedness' without satisfaction creates latent turnover intention that manifests explosively when constraints lift.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should not mistake low turnover rates for high engagement—conduct stay interviews and pulse surveys to identify 'trapped' employees whose resentment is building. Proactively investing in engagement, development, and addressing grievances now is far cheaper than facing a talent exodus when the market shifts.
