# Wealthy parents pay $75,000 for Alpha’s two-hour AI school day. Half of its K-8 students missed the math target

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sat, 14 Nov 2026 12:10:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/14/alpha-school-ai-students-tuition-learning-goals/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/14/alpha-school-ai-students-tuition-learning-goals/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Alpha School, a $75,000/year AI-driven private school network with no traditional teachers, built its pitch around students learning twice as fast using an AI tutor called TimeBack—but internal data show only 42% of students met that goal, with roughly half missing math targets. The network is now pushing into public schools, where it lacks the prize incentives used at private campuses and can't filter out struggling students.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a case study in the gap between leadership's public narrative and internal performance data, plus the risks of scaling a model built on specific incentive structures (prizes, selective student populations) to environments where those structures don't exist.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should be wary of scaling any performance model validated under narrow, favorable conditions (small cohorts, strong incentives, ability to filter out low performers) into broader, less controlled environments without first validating that the mechanism—not just the marketing—holds; track and disclose internal metrics as rigorously as external claims before publicizing success.
