## Summary
Nvidia's stock plunged as much as 14% after CEO Jensen Huang abruptly stepped down, with CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO; the news rattled global tech markets, with mixed effects on Asian and European chip-related stocks.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Sudden, unexplained leadership transitions at highly visible companies can trigger acute investor and market anxiety, reflecting how closely organizational stability is tied to perceptions of leader indispensability and succession readiness.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat CEO succession planning as a continuous risk-management function, not a reactive event—clear communication protocols and a credible, visible bench of successors can mitigate market and employee shock when leadership changes unexpectedly.
