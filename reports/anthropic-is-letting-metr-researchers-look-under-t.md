# Anthropic is letting METR researchers look under the hood with employee-level access

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** Thu, 15 Oct 2026 19:14:26 +0000  
**URL:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/anthropic-is-letting-metr-researchers-look-under-the-hood-with-employee-level-access/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/anthropic-is-letting-metr-researchers-look-under-the-hood-with-employee-level-access/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Anthropic is granting six researchers from nonprofit METR employee-level access—including laptops, office space, and internal tools—to independently evaluate its AI models and safety practices, with METR free to publish findings without Anthropic's veto (subject to narrow redaction categories). This is the top tier of a three-tier evaluator access system, following a promise by CEO Dario Amodei and a broader industry push toward independent AI oversight.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a case study in designed organizational transparency and accountability structures—deliberately ceding control to external watchdogs mirrors governance mechanisms (like embedded regulators) used to counteract self-serving bias and blind spots in high-stakes, fast-moving organizations.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs managing high-risk or high-trust domains (safety, compliance, ethics) should consider formalized external audit access with real publication independence, not just advisory reviews, since symbolic oversight without teeth does little to build genuine accountability or stakeholder trust.
