# Apple is reportedly cutting hundreds of jobs from Siri, Vision Pro teams

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** Fri, 21 Aug 2026 20:58:07 +0000  
**URL:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/21/apple-is-reportedly-cutting-hundreds-of-jobs-from-siri-vision-pro-teams/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/21/apple-is-reportedly-cutting-hundreds-of-jobs-from-siri-vision-pro-teams/)

## Summary
Apple is cutting hundreds of jobs from its Siri and Vision Pro teams as the company shifts strategic focus away from certain initiatives, acknowledging that some roles are being impacted by this organizational realignment.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Strategic pivots that result in large-scale layoffs within specific teams create survivor syndrome, erode psychological safety, and signal to remaining employees that their projects may also be deprioritized—potentially undermining innovation and risk-taking.

## Leadership Lesson
When reallocating resources away from flagship initiatives, leaders must proactively manage the narrative and provide transparent communication about the strategic rationale, offer robust transition support for affected employees, and reinforce commitment to remaining teams to prevent talent flight and morale collapse.
