# The economist behind the ‘China shock’ says AI is a paradoxical  ‘skill-disequalizer’ for Gen Z workers

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sat, 10 Oct 2026 12:49:27 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/10/10/david-autor-china-shock-nber-google-paper-illusion-of-competence-gen-z/](https://fortune.com/2026/10/10/david-autor-china-shock-nber-google-paper-illusion-of-competence-gen-z/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
A new study by economist David Autor finds that AI drafting tools improved patent lawyers' output quality for both junior and senior lawyers, but only senior lawyers showed actual skill improvement when tested without AI assistance—juniors showed no average gain, suggesting AI may act as a 'skill-disequalizer' rather than an equalizer.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The study illustrates how tool-assisted performance gains can mask (or even prevent) underlying skill development, particularly for novices who lack the foundational mental models needed to critically evaluate AI output—raising concerns about an 'illusion of competence' and erosion of apprenticeship-based learning.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should not assume AI tools automatically accelerate junior employee development; they must deliberately preserve unassisted practice, structured mentoring, and periodic skill audits so AI augments rather than replaces the apprenticeship pipeline that builds future senior talent.
