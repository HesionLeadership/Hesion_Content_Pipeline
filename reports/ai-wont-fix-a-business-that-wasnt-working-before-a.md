## Summary
The article argues that AI adoption cannot fix fundamental organizational problems — technology fails when layered on top of unclear processes, weak accountability, and teams lacking clear performance standards. Leaders must first address foundational operational and cultural issues before expecting AI to deliver results.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly reflects the sociotechnical systems perspective: technology implementations fail when organizations neglect the human and process dimensions. It also connects to research on role clarity and accountability as prerequisites for performance improvement through any intervention.

## Leadership Lesson
Before investing in AI or any transformative technology, CHROs and COOs should audit whether their organization has clear processes, defined performance standards, and robust accountability structures — without these foundations, technology adoption will amplify dysfunction rather than drive improvement.
