# Exclusive: Honeywell Technologies launches a ‘brain behind the building’ with Nvidia and Dell. Its CTO says humans will run it for now

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Wed, 14 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/10/14/honeywell-technologies-brain-behind-the-bulding-forge-cognition-dell-nvidia/](https://fortune.com/2026/10/14/honeywell-technologies-brain-behind-the-bulding-forge-cognition-dell-nvidia/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Honeywell launched Forge Cognition, an AI platform built with Nvidia and Dell that acts as a 'brain' for buildings, running in manual, semi-autonomous, or fully autonomous modes. Honeywell's CTO says the platform will stay human-controlled for the next 2-3 years, even though it can technically run autonomously, with Nvidia's headquarters serving as the first live deployment.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates deliberate trust calibration in human-automation collaboration: Honeywell is intentionally limiting system autonomy so human operators can verify AI decisions, build confidence in the technology, and avoid the skepticism or resistance that often accompanies rapid automation rollouts.

## Leadership Lesson
When deploying AI or automation that affects critical operations, leaders should sequence autonomy gradually and keep humans 'in the loop' long enough to build trust and capture tacit expertise—rather than rushing to full automation, which can trigger resistance, errors, and loss of institutional knowledge. CHROs/COOs should treat AI rollout as a change-management and trust-building process, not just a technical deployment.
