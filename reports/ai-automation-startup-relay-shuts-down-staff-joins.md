# AI automation startup Relay shuts down, staff joins Google’s Chrome team

**Source:** TechCrunch

**URL:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/ai-automation-startup-relay-shuts-down-staff-joins-googles-chrome-team/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/ai-automation-startup-relay-shuts-down-staff-joins-googles-chrome-team/)

## Summary
AI automation startup Relay has shut down operations, with its entire staff being acqui-hired by Google to join the Chrome team. Founder Jacob Bank hints at ambitious plans to integrate AI capabilities into Chrome.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Acqui-hires represent a unique organizational integration challenge—absorbing an intact team with its own culture, norms, and identity into a much larger organization. The dynamics of team cohesion preservation versus assimilation into Google's Chrome division raise important questions about identity transition and knowledge transfer.

## Leadership Lesson
When executing acqui-hires, CHROs should consider whether keeping the acquired team intact preserves valuable group dynamics and psychological safety, or whether rapid integration into existing teams better serves long-term performance. Deliberate onboarding strategies that honor the acquired team's identity while aligning them to new organizational goals are critical.
