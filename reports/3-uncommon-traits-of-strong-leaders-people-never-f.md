# 3 Uncommon Traits of Strong Leaders People Never Forget

## Summary
The article identifies 3 uncommon traits of strong, memorable leaders: they admit what they don't know (intellectual humility), they make people feel safe to speak up (psychological safety), and they give credit generously rather than claiming it for themselves (credit sharing). These 'soft' traits are presented as serious competitive advantages for building stronger teams.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This maps directly to well-established org psych constructs: leader humility, psychological safety (Edmondson), and prosocial leadership behavior. Research consistently shows these traits predict team performance, innovation, and retention.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should evaluate and develop leaders not just on decisiveness and results, but on humility, safety-building, and credit-sharing behaviors—these are measurable competencies that drive team engagement and reduce turnover, not merely 'nice-to-haves.'
