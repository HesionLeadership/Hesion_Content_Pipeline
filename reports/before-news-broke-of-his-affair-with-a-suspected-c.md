## Summary
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's affair with a woman U.S. intelligence suspects is a Chinese intelligence asset has reignited scrutiny of his advocacy for expanding Nvidia's China business, despite U.S. export controls. Nvidia's board has launched an independent review into whether trade secrets or national security were compromised, while Huang remains CEO and faces a congressional briefing request.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case highlights how board governance structures respond (or fail to respond quickly) when a CEO's personal conduct creates potential conflicts of interest with the company's strategic and national-security-sensitive business dealings.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and boards should ensure conflict-of-interest and personal conduct disclosure policies apply robustly to top executives, especially those with significant influence over geopolitically sensitive business decisions, and that independent oversight mechanisms are triggered proactively rather than reactively after media exposure.
