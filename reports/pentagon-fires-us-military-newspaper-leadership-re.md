## Summary
The Pentagon has fired the leadership of Stars and Stripes, the independent US military newspaper, in a move that raises questions about editorial independence and institutional control over military media.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Removing leadership of an organization designed to operate with editorial independence from its parent institution highlights tensions between organizational autonomy and hierarchical control, and raises concerns about the chilling effect on voice behavior and psychological safety among remaining staff.

## Leadership Lesson
When leadership dismissals appear to threaten institutional independence, they can erode trust and silence dissent across the broader organization. CHROs should ensure that governance actions—especially those involving semi-autonomous units—are transparent and procedurally fair to preserve organizational voice and credibility.
