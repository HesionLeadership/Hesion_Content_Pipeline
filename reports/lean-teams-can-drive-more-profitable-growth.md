# Lean Teams Can Drive More Profitable Growth

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Wed, 02 Sep 2026 08:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/inc-video/lean-teams-can-drive-more-profitable-growth/91399226](https://www.inc.com/inc-video/lean-teams-can-drive-more-profitable-growth/91399226)

## Summary
Inc. 5000 honoree Natasha Miller describes how pandemic-era layoffs led her to restructure her company around a smaller core team supplemented by trusted freelancers, resulting in a more flexible, efficient, and profitable business model.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates how organizational downsizing, when followed by intentional restructuring rather than simply doing more with less, can lead to improved organizational design. The shift to a core-periphery workforce model raises questions about team cohesion, psychological contracts with non-permanent workers, and how lean structures affect employee engagement and workload.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should view workforce reductions not just as cost-cutting but as opportunities to redesign organizational structure—building a flexible core-plus-freelancer model that maintains capability while improving agility. The key is investing in relationships with external talent so they function as trusted extensions of the team rather than disposable labor.
