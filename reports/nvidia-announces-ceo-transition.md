# NVIDIA Announces CEO Transition

## Summary
NVIDIA announced that Jensen Huang has resigned as President, CEO, and Board member effective immediately, with CFO Colette Kress appointed Interim CEO while the Board searches for a permanent successor. The Board also named Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever Chair, and both leaders emphasized continuity in operations, strategy, and customer commitments.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a high-stakes leadership transition case involving a founder-CEO's sudden departure after 33 years, highlighting how organizations manage succession uncertainty, signal stability to stakeholders, and leverage internal leadership continuity (CFO-to-interim-CEO) to reduce disruption.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure succession plans include a credible, pre-vetted internal bridge leader (as Kress represents here) to maintain stakeholder confidence and operational continuity during unplanned CEO transitions, while also separating board governance (new Chair role) from CEO authority to strengthen oversight.
