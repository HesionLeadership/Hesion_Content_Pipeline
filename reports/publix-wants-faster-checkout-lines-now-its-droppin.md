# Publix Wants Faster Checkout Lines. Now It’s Dropping the Rule Meant to Speed Them Up

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Thu, 20 Aug 2026 15:00:21 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/amaya-nichole/publix-wants-faster-checkout-lines-dropping-rule-meant-to-speed-them-up/91394190](https://www.inc.com/amaya-nichole/publix-wants-faster-checkout-lines-dropping-rule-meant-to-speed-them-up/91394190)

## Summary
Publix is eliminating its strict express lane item-limit policy, instead empowering frontline employees to use their judgment to manage checkout flow and direct customers to lanes based on real-time conditions rather than rigid rules.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This represents a shift from bureaucratic control to employee empowerment and discretion at the frontline level—trusting workers to make real-time operational decisions rather than relying on rigid policies that may not account for situational variability.

## Leadership Lesson
When rigid rules designed to optimize performance actually create friction or fail to account for complexity, leaders should consider empowering frontline employees with decision-making authority. This requires investing in employee judgment and training rather than defaulting to prescriptive policies—a key insight for COOs managing operational workflows.
