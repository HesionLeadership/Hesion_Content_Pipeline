## Summary
Jensen Huang, who resigned as Nvidia CEO last week following revelations of affairs with an employee and a woman linked to Chinese intelligence, said he would return as CEO if the board asked him to, citing support from employees, investors, and partners.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the tension between founder-CEO indispensability and governance accountability—boards often face pressure to reinstate powerful leaders despite serious ethical and security-related misconduct, especially when the leader is seen as critical to company performance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and boards should establish clear, pre-defined misconduct thresholds and succession protocols before a crisis hits, so leadership continuity decisions aren't made reactively under public/investor pressure that can undermine governance integrity.
