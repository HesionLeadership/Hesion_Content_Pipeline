## Summary
Gap Inc. shares rose after the company announced a leadership change at its Old Navy division and raised its profit forecast, signaling renewed investor confidence in the company's strategic direction under new brand leadership.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Leadership succession at a key business unit, combined with improved financial guidance, illustrates how strategic leadership changes can signal organizational turnaround and restore stakeholder confidence. The market's positive reaction reflects the perceived importance of leader-organization fit in driving brand performance.

## Leadership Lesson
When a brand or division is underperforming, timely leadership changes paired with clear strategic communication can catalyze both internal momentum and external confidence. CHROs should ensure succession planning frameworks are robust enough to enable decisive leadership transitions that align new leaders with the organization's turnaround needs.
