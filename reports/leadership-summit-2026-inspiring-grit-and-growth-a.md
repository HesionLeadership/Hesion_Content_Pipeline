## Summary
Angela Duckworth discusses how the concept of grit applies to leadership and organizational resilience in an era of rapid AI-driven change, as part of HBR's Leadership Summit 2026.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Grit—the intersection of passion and perseverance—has well-established roots in organizational psychology research, and its application to how leaders and employees navigate technological disruption (particularly AI) raises important questions about adaptability, growth mindset, and sustained motivation in changing work environments.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider how to cultivate grit alongside adaptability in their talent strategies—perseverance matters, but in an AI-disrupted landscape, leaders must also foster learning agility and psychological flexibility so that grit doesn't become rigid attachment to obsolete approaches.
