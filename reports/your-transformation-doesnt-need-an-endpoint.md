## Summary
HBR's Insider newsletter explores why organizational transformation should be viewed as a continuous learning journey rather than a project with a fixed endpoint, how to detect toxic leaders during the interview process, and related organizational topics.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Framing transformation as an ongoing process rather than a finite project aligns with research on organizational learning and adaptive change, suggesting that companies with a continuous improvement mindset outperform those seeking a definitive 'end state.' Detecting toxic leadership during hiring connects to research on destructive leadership and selection psychology.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design transformation initiatives as iterative learning cycles rather than fixed-milestone projects, and should invest in structured interview protocols specifically designed to surface toxic leadership traits before making senior hires.
