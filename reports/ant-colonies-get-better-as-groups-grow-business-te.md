## Summary
Drawing from research on ant colonies that become more efficient as they grow, the article proposes three business practices for cross-functional teams: (1) establish clear role specialization within teams, (2) use simple, consistent communication protocols, and (3) design decentralized decision-making structures that allow teams to self-organize.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The article leverages collective intelligence and swarm behavior research as a biomimetic model for team scaling, touching on how role clarity, communication norms, and distributed authority can prevent coordination losses as teams grow—directly relevant to Steiner's model of process losses in groups.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that scaling teams effectively requires deliberate structural design—defining specialized roles, simplifying communication channels, and empowering local decision-making—rather than simply adding headcount and expecting proportional output gains.
