# AI Makes Building Easy. Choosing What to Build Is Harder.

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-12T12:15:11Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/ai-makes-building-easy-choosing-what-to-build-is-harder](https://hbr.org/2026/08/ai-makes-building-easy-choosing-what-to-build-is-harder)

## Summary
As AI tools become widely accessible and commoditized, the competitive advantage for teams shifts from technical capability to deeply understanding the right problems to solve. Leaders must invest in problem framing, domain expertise, and user understanding rather than just AI implementation skills.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the organizational psychology concept of problem construction—teams that excel at sense-making and problem definition outperform those focused solely on solution execution, especially when tools are democratized and technical barriers are lowered.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should prioritize hiring and developing people with strong problem-framing skills, domain expertise, and customer empathy rather than over-indexing on technical AI proficiency. Build organizational capabilities around sense-making, cross-functional collaboration, and deep contextual understanding to maintain competitive advantage.
