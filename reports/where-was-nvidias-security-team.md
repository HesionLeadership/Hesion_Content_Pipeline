## Summary
Nvidia's longtime chief security officer stepped down in August, just before CEO Jensen Huang's departure amid scrutiny of extramarital relationships—one with an executive at Megaspeed, a company under federal investigation for alleged chip smuggling to China. The piece examines what Nvidia's security team may have known and whether organizational silos between security, HR, and legal left blind spots around insider threats and foreign intelligence risks.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights how organizational silos—where security teams are narrowly scoped to cyber/physical threats while HR and legal handle executive conduct—can create dangerous blind spots around insider threats, especially when executive behavior intersects with national security concerns.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure security, HR, legal, and compliance functions have structured channels to share concerns about executive conduct, particularly when behavior intersects with conflicts of interest or foreign business relationships; narrow definitions of 'security' that exclude insider threat and relationship-based risks leave organizations exposed.
