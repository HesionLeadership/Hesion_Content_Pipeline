# David Ellison names Ynon Kreiz co-CEO of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
David Ellison named outgoing Mattel CEO Ynon Kreiz as co-CEO of the combined Paramount-Warner Bros. Discovery entity, with Ellison focusing on long-term strategy and creative vision while Kreiz handles day-to-day operations and business integration. The $110 billion merger is set to close October 6 following settlement of an antitrust lawsuit.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a textbook case of a co-CEO structure with explicitly divided responsibilities (strategic/creative vs. operational), raising questions about role clarity, power-sharing dynamics, and potential conflict in shared leadership arrangements during a complex post-merger integration.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs overseeing post-merger integrations should pay close attention to how co-CEO or shared-leadership roles are formally defined and communicated, since ambiguous boundaries between 'strategic vision' and 'operational management' are a common source of executive conflict and organizational confusion if not reinforced by clear governance structures and decision rights.
