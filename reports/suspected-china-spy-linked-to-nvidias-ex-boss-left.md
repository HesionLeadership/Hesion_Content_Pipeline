# Suspected China spy linked to Nvidia's ex-boss left in 2025, Megaspeed says

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Alice Huang, a suspected China spy linked to Nvidia's former CEO Jensen Huang (who resigned amid reports of affairs), left her executive role at Singapore-based Megaspeed in August 2025. Megaspeed, the largest buyer of Nvidia chips in Southeast Asia, is under investigation for potentially providing backdoor access to restricted AI chips for Chinese companies, and US officials are now scrutinizing export control oversight failures.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case highlights how executive personal relationships and conflicts of interest can become serious organizational and national security risks, while also exposing gaps in corporate compliance structures and cross-agency governmental oversight that failed to flag risks despite internal recommendations.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure robust conflict-of-interest disclosure policies for senior executives, especially those with access to sensitive technology, trade secrets, or regulated exports, and build governance systems that don't rely solely on individual leaders' judgment or personal relationships.
