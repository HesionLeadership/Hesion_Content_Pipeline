# Bosses are convinced Gen Z fakes Wi-Fi outages and sick days to sneak off in summer—but 1 in 2 senior managers are guilty of the same thing

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Tue, 25 Aug 2026 07:03:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/08/25/gen-z-and-millennials-managers-ditching-desks-and-taking-a-secret-vacation-behind-bosses-back-fake-sick/](https://fortune.com/2026/08/25/gen-z-and-millennials-managers-ditching-desks-and-taking-a-secret-vacation-behind-bosses-back-fake-sick/)

## Summary
Research reveals that while bosses accuse Gen Z workers of faking Wi-Fi outages and sick days to enjoy summer weather, approximately 50% of senior managers engage in the same behavior—calling in sick or sneaking out of work during warm weather.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights a significant hypocrisy gap in workplace accountability and points to broader issues of generational attribution bias, where leaders disproportionately blame younger workers for behaviors that are actually widespread across all organizational levels. It also raises questions about whether rigid attendance norms are misaligned with employee needs across the board.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should address summer absenteeism systemically rather than scapegoating specific generations—consider implementing flexible summer schedules or compressed workweeks that legitimize time off, reducing the need for deception at all levels and building a culture of trust and transparency.
