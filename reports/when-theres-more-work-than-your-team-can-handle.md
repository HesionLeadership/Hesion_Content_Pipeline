# When There’s More Work Than Your Team Can Handle

**Source:** HBR

## Summary
HBR explores strategies for leaders facing workload that exceeds their team's capacity, arguing that leaders cannot simply absorb extra work themselves or push overextended teams harder. The article addresses how to prioritize, communicate constraints, and make strategic decisions about what work to deprioritize or decline.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses role overload theory and the consequences of chronic work demands exceeding resources, a core topic in occupational health psychology. Leaders who fail to manage workload boundaries risk burnout cascades, reduced team performance, and learned helplessness across their units.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build organizational norms that empower managers to transparently reprioritize and push back on excessive demands rather than defaulting to heroic absorption—systematic workload governance prevents burnout and preserves sustainable performance.
