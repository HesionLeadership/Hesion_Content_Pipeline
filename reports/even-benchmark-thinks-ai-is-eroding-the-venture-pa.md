# Even Benchmark thinks AI is eroding the venture pact against backing rivals

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Benchmark partners acknowledged at TechCrunch Disrupt that the longstanding VC norm against backing competing startups is eroding under AI-driven market pressures, citing a recent conflict where Factory fired board observer Chris Degnan over alleged unethical dealings with rival Cognition, who then hired him as CRO.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates how institutional norms built on trust and reputation (rather than formal rules) erode when environmental pressures—here, rapid market expansion and blurring competitive boundaries—make adherence increasingly costly, forcing organizations to substitute transparency processes for norm-based trust.

## Leadership Lesson
When industry or market dynamics make long-standing informal trust norms unsustainable, leaders should proactively replace reliance on reputation with explicit transparency protocols and conflict-disclosure processes—waiting for a crisis to reveal the gap erodes stakeholder trust faster than acknowledging the norm shift directly.
