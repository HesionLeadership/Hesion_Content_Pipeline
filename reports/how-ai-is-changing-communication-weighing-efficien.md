## Summary
An HBR IdeaCast episode features Stanford professor Matt Abrahams and Zapier CEO Wade Foster discussing how AI is reshaping leadership communication—helping leaders synthesize information and structure ideas, while risking authenticity, empathy, and a 'comprehension gap' if overused.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The discussion touches on authenticity, accountability, and judgment in leader communication, and how overreliance on AI tools can erode the human elements (empathy, voice, critical thinking) that build trust and credibility with employees and stakeholders.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should establish clear organizational norms for AI use in communication—specifying when AI is a thought partner versus final author—and model transparent, judgment-driven AI usage to preserve trust, accountability, and authentic leadership voice.
