# Factory CEO just accused his VC board adviser of spying for Cognition

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Factory CEO Matan Grinberg fired board adviser Chris Degnan, alleging he shared confidential information with rival Cognition while serving on Factory's board; Degnan disputes this, claiming he resigned upon accepting a CRO role at Cognition and never shared confidential data. The dispute escalated publicly on X, drawing in VCs Vinod Khosla and Keith Rabois, who took opposing sides.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case highlights the fragility of trust-based governance structures in startups, where board advisers often hold dual loyalties (to the startup, to their VC firm, and increasingly to competing portfolio companies), creating structural conflicts of interest that erode psychological safety and information security.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should formalize conflict-of-interest protocols and confidentiality agreements for board advisers and investors with stakes in competitors, rather than relying on informal trust; proactive disclosure requirements and clear recusal processes should be built into governance structures before trust is tested.
