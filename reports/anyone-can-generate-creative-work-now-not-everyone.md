# Anyone Can Generate Creative Work Now. Not Everyone Can Judge It

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Sat, 19 Sep 2026 05:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/nicole-ramirez/anyone-can-generate-creative-work-now-not-everyone-can-judge-it/91407457](https://www.inc.com/nicole-ramirez/anyone-can-generate-creative-work-now-not-everyone-can-judge-it/91407457)

## Summary
Vistaprint's global executive creative director argues that while AI tools have democratized the ability to generate creative output, the critical differentiator is human judgment—skilled teams who can evaluate, curate, and decide what actually ships to customers.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
As AI lowers the barrier to content creation, the organizational competency shifts from production skills to evaluative judgment and taste, fundamentally redefining what 'creative expertise' means in teams and how organizations should structure creative roles and hiring criteria.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders should invest in developing their teams' critical evaluation and decision-making capabilities rather than solely focusing on technical production skills. As generative tools proliferate, the competitive advantage lies in cultivating organizational judgment—knowing what's good enough to ship—which requires experience, standards, and psychological safety to reject AI-generated mediocrity.
