## Summary
Sprinkles founder Candace Nelson transformed customer complaints on TikTok into a strategic advantage by using social media as a real-time focus group, product lab, and customer research tool, fundamentally changing how the business operates.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the power of customer voice integration and organizational learning—leaders who treat external feedback (even negative) as data rather than threat can accelerate adaptive decision-making and foster a culture of responsiveness.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build organizational systems that rapidly channel external feedback into product and process decisions, treating customer complaints as free R&D rather than reputational threats. This requires cultivating psychological safety internally so teams aren't defensive about criticism.
