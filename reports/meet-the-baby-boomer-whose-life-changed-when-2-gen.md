# Meet the Baby Boomer whose life changed when 2 Gen Z AI founders walked into her plumbing business. She became their first COO

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Wed, 28 Oct 2026 13:00:00 +0000

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Two Gen Z AI founders (Broccoli AI) went door-to-door to contractor shops seeking a business operator to build with, and found Marcelle Flowers, a 62-year-old plumbing company owner, who became their first customer, then adviser, then COO; the company has since raised $52M and grown to 150 employees serving 1,500+ contractor businesses.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how pairing complementary expertise—young technical founders with an experienced domain operator—can accelerate product-market fit, and how authentic relationship-building (curiosity over sales pitches) fosters trust that transactional approaches often fail to achieve.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that technical innovation often needs domain-expert 'translators' who understand customer pain points intimately; actively recruiting experienced operators into leadership roles—even from outside the tech industry—can de-risk product development and build authentic market trust faster than conventional sales or hiring strategies.
