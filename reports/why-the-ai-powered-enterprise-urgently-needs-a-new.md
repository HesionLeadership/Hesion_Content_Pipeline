## Summary
EY-sponsored content arguing that AI-powered enterprises require a fundamentally new leadership mindset, shifting from traditional command-and-control approaches to ones that embrace AI integration, continuous learning, and cross-functional collaboration.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The adoption of transformative technology like AI demands adaptive leadership and a growth mindset at the organizational level, requiring leaders to manage ambiguity, foster psychological safety for experimentation, and redesign work systems around human-AI collaboration.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively develop leadership competency models that include AI literacy, comfort with ambiguity, and the ability to redesign roles and workflows around human-AI teaming—rather than treating AI adoption as purely a technology initiative.
