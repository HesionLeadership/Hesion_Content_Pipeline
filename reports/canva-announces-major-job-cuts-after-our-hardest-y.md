# Canva announces major job cuts after 'our hardest year yet'

**Source:** BBC Business  
**Published:** Wed, 04 Nov 2026 00:09:35 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw77pg7neydmo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cw77pg7neydmo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Canva is cutting 22% of its workforce (1,271 people) through layoffs, relocations, or role changes, citing its 'hardest year yet' amid slowing growth and rising AI-related costs. The company is also centralizing product teams in Sydney and requiring hybrid workers to return to office 2-3 days per week starting next year.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how organizations manage large-scale restructuring communication during technological disruption, including the psychological impact on both departing employees and 'survivors' who remain amid centralization and RTO mandates.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that bundling layoffs, relocation demands, and RTO mandates into a single disruptive event compounds employee anxiety and erodes trust; sequencing major changes and providing clear rationale for each decision separately can reduce perceived organizational injustice and support retention of key talent.
