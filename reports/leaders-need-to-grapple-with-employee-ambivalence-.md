## Organizational Psychology Angle
Employee ambivalence toward organizational change (like AI adoption) often stems from competing fears (job loss) and hopes (efficiency gains), and leaders who suppress or dismiss this ambivalence rather than acknowledging it risk lower trust and engagement.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should create structured, low-risk spaces for employees to experiment with AI tools and openly voice concerns, while transparently communicating how productivity gains will be reinvested (e.g., in new roles or reduced workload) rather than solely used for cost-cutting.
