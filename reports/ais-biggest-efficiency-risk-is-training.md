# AI’s Biggest Efficiency Risk is Training

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Wed, 02 Sep 2026 08:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/jon-shanahan/ais-biggest-efficiency-risk-is-training/91398758](https://www.inc.com/jon-shanahan/ais-biggest-efficiency-risk-is-training/91398758)

## Summary
New data reveals that nearly half of employees are left to figure out AI tools on their own, with inadequate training creating significant efficiency risks for employers. The story highlights that organizations investing in AI technology without corresponding workforce development may be undermining their own productivity goals.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects a classic technology adoption gap where organizations invest heavily in tools but underinvest in human capital development, creating employee frustration, skill anxiety, and reduced self-efficacy. The sink-or-swim approach to AI training can erode psychological safety and widen competency gaps across the workforce.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat AI upskilling as a strategic organizational change initiative—not an afterthought. Structured training programs, clear learning pathways, and dedicated support reduce adoption resistance and ensure ROI on technology investments. Leaders must pair every major technology rollout with a commensurate investment in employee development.
