## This Week's Themes

A third theme is leadership communication and identity under uncertainty, visible in **ConnectOne Bank CEO: what Kevin Warsh's 'quieter Fed' can teach CEOs about communicating uncertainty** (Fortune) and **Research: When Being Authentic Undermines Your Leadership** (HBR).
