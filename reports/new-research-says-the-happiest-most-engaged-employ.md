## Summary
New research suggests that the happiest and most engaged employees share a common trait related to how they navigate career decisions under uncertainty, rather than factors like compensation or perks.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This connects to tolerance for ambiguity and decisiveness in career development—employees who can act on incomplete information and embrace uncertainty tend to report higher engagement and satisfaction, linking to self-determination theory and proactive career behaviors.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should foster organizational cultures where employees feel psychologically safe making career moves without perfect information. Providing mentorship, internal mobility programs, and normalizing iterative career exploration can boost engagement by reducing decision paralysis.
