## Summary
Berkshire Hathaway shares have fully recovered and hit new all-time highs 18 months after Warren Buffett's surprise announcement that he would step down as CEO, with new CEO Greg Abel now seen as capable of running the company and taking advantage of its cash reserves amid a market rotation away from AI/tech stocks.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how market/stakeholder confidence in a successor CEO is rebuilt gradually through demonstrated performance over time, rather than through initial announcements—reflecting research on how trust in new leadership following a founder's departure is earned incrementally rather than granted immediately.

## Leadership Lesson
For CHROs/COOs managing CEO succession, expect an initial 'confidence gap' or discount period even with well-prepared successors; sustained, visible execution of strategy (not just reassurance from analysts or press) is what ultimately restores stakeholder trust and closes that gap.
