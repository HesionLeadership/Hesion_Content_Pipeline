## Summary
WWT CEO Jim Kavanaugh describes his evolution from monitoring employee parking lot presence as a proxy for work ethic to embracing hybrid work and trusting employees based on outputs rather than hours logged. Workplace experts emphasize that RTO mandates should be driven by clear purpose (e.g., mentorship, creativity) rather than blanket policies.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates the shift from input-based monitoring (surveillance, presenteeism) to trust-based, output-oriented management, and how perceived distrust from mandatory RTO policies can undermine autonomy and psychological safety.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit RTO and flexibility policies against specific organizational goals (mentorship, collaboration, creativity) rather than defaulting to uniform attendance mandates, and should shift performance evaluation toward measurable outputs rather than visible inputs like hours or physical presence.
