# This CEO Borrows Leadership Strategy From Dungeons & Dragons: Set Guardrails, Not Routes

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Fri, 04 Sep 2026 07:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/dan-schawbel/ceos-dungeons-dragons-leadership/91401026](https://www.inc.com/dan-schawbel/ceos-dungeons-dragons-leadership/91401026)

## Summary
Designit CEO Keri Dawson draws parallels between being a Dungeons & Dragons Dungeon Master and leading an organization, arguing that leaders should set guardrails rather than prescriptive routes, allowing teams the autonomy to navigate challenges creatively within defined boundaries.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This maps directly to self-determination theory and autonomy-supportive leadership — providing structural boundaries while granting employees freedom within those constraints enhances intrinsic motivation, creativity, and engagement.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design governance frameworks that establish clear boundaries and non-negotiables (guardrails) while resisting the urge to micromanage the path to outcomes. This approach fosters psychological ownership, adaptive problem-solving, and higher team engagement — particularly in knowledge-work environments where creative autonomy drives performance.
