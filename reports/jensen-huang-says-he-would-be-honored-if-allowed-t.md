## Summary
Jensen Huang publicly stated he would be 'honored' to return as Nvidia CEO if the board decides it's in the company's best interest, following his resignation over personal misconduct allegations including affairs with a direct report and a figure suspected of ties to Chinese intelligence. Over 10,000 employees and prominent tech figures signed an open letter demanding due process, while Nvidia's board reportedly has no plans to reinstate him amid congressional scrutiny over chip export compliance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case highlights tensions between founder-centric organizational identity, procedural justice in executive discipline, and stakeholder pressure campaigns that can undermine board authority during crisis governance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should establish clear, pre-defined protocols for executive misconduct investigations that balance transparency and due process, since ambiguous processes invite stakeholder backlash and can pressure boards into decisions driven by employee/investor sentiment rather than independent governance.
