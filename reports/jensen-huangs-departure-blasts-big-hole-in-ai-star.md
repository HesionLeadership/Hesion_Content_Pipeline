# Jensen Huang’s departure blasts big hole in AI startup funding, VCs and founders say

**Published:** Fri, 13 Nov 2026 22:43:00 +0000

## Summary
Following Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia CEO after 33 years, investors and founders fear a major funding gap in AI startups, since Huang personally drove many of Nvidia's billions in startup investments. Sources doubt interim CEO Colette Kress will maintain the same aggressive, founder-driven dealmaking pace, which could reduce valuations and startup funding across the ecosystem.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates 'key person dependency risk'—where critical organizational functions (in this case, investment decision-making) become so centralized around one charismatic leader's judgment and relationships that the organization's capacity to execute core strategy is destabilized by their departure.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit how much institutional capability (deal flow, decision authority, external relationships) is embedded in a single executive rather than distributed through systems, delegated authority, or documented decision frameworks; over-reliance on founder intuition without succession infrastructure creates organizational fragility that surfaces abruptly at leadership transitions.
