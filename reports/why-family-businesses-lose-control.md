# Why Family Businesses Lose Control

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-11T12:15:54Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/why-family-businesses-lose-control](https://hbr.org/2026/08/why-family-businesses-lose-control)

## Summary
Family businesses often lose control not because of poor leadership at the executive level, but because family owners are unprepared for the responsibilities of ownership, including governance, decision-making, and stewardship across generations.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the critical distinction between leadership capability and ownership competence—a governance gap where family dynamics, role ambiguity, and lack of structured succession planning erode organizational control over time.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs in family-owned or founder-led enterprises should invest in ownership education and governance structures that develop next-generation owners as competent stewards, not just groom operational leaders. Treating ownership as a role requiring deliberate development—parallel to executive development—prevents organizational drift and loss of strategic control.
