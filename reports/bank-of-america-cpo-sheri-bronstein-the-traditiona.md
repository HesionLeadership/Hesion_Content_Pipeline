# Bank of America CPO Sheri Bronstein: ‘The traditional definition of a working parent is too narrow’

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Thu, 19 Nov 2026 12:00:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/19/bank-of-america-chro-sheri-bronstein-the-traditional-definition-of-a-working-parent-is-too-narrow/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/19/bank-of-america-chro-sheri-bronstein-the-traditional-definition-of-a-working-parent-is-too-narrow/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Bank of America CPO Sheri Bronstein argues that the definition of 'working parent' is too narrow, since many employees are simultaneously caregiving for children, aging parents, or dealing with health/life issues (the 'sandwich generation'). The bank has expanded benefits (parental leave, backup care for children and adults, adoption/fertility reimbursement) and emphasizes manager empathy and awareness as key to making those benefits effective, helping it rise from No. 33 to No. 10 on Fortune's 2026 Best Workplaces for Parents list.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how perceived organizational support and voice (employees feeling heard and able to shape policy) drive engagement and trust, consistent with research on perceived organizational support (POS) and psychological safety in disclosing personal/caregiving needs.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should ensure benefits are paired with manager training on empathy and active listening, since policy awareness and psychological safety determine whether employees actually use available support—benefits alone don't change behavior without trusted, responsive leadership.
