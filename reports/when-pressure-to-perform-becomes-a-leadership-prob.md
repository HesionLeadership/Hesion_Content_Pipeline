## Summary
The article explores how the pressure to perform can become counterproductive for leaders, transforming from a motivator into a leadership liability when it drives unsustainable behaviors, erodes trust, and undermines team well-being.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Chronic performance pressure can trigger maladaptive leadership behaviors such as micromanagement, emotional reactivity, and short-term thinking, which degrade psychological safety and team performance — a well-documented phenomenon in stress and leadership research.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build systems that help leaders recognize when performance pressure is shifting from productive challenge to destructive threat — through coaching, stress monitoring, and creating cultures where leaders can be transparent about pressure without stigma.
