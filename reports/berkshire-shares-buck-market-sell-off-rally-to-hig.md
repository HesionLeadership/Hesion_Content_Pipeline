## Summary
Berkshire Hathaway shares have recovered to near all-time highs more than a year after Warren Buffett announced his plan to step down as CEO, with new CEO Greg Abel deploying cash into buybacks and investments including a $930 million stake in Italian tech company Bending Spoons. The article also details Berkshire's top stock holdings and cash position as of mid-2026.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how markets and stakeholders initially discount a company's value after a legendary founder-CEO announces departure (the 'Buffett premium' dissolving), but confidence can be rebuilt over time as the successor demonstrates competent capital allocation and strategic continuity.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs managing founder-to-successor transitions should recognize that stakeholder confidence erodes immediately upon a charismatic leader's departure announcement, but can be systematically rebuilt through visible, competent decision-making by the new leader (e.g., capital deployment, strategic investments) rather than rhetoric alone—patience and demonstrated judgment matter more than quick wins.
