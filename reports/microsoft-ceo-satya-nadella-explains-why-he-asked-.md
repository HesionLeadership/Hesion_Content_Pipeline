## Summary
Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella explained that the company offered buyouts to 60,000 employees to give them 'agency' in choosing whether to adapt to an AI-first reorganization where workers increasingly manage AI agents rather than doing direct work themselves, or to leave voluntarily instead of facing layoffs.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Nadella frames a voluntary exit program as a way to preserve employee agency and autonomy during a disruptive structural change, acknowledging that AI-driven role transformation creates anxiety and that forced restructuring without choice undermines psychological ownership of one's career path.

## Leadership Lesson
When undertaking major structural or technological transformation, offering employees a genuine choice (exit vs. adapt) rather than imposing unilateral role changes or layoffs can reduce anxiety and preserve a sense of control, but CHROs should scrutinize whether 'voluntary' buyouts under implicit pressure to adopt unfamiliar skillsets truly deliver autonomy or merely soften the optics of workforce reduction.
