# Converse responds to outrage over the Instagram ad for its Chuck 70 X sneakers

**Source:** Fast Company  
**Published:** 2026-09-21T13:14:52  
**URL:** [https://www.fastcompany.com/91610281/converse-responds-outrage-over-instagram-ad-chuck-70-x-sneakers](https://www.fastcompany.com/91610281/converse-responds-outrage-over-instagram-ad-chuck-70-x-sneakers)

## Summary
Converse removed an Instagram ad featuring K-pop singer Karina after critics said the image evoked KKK imagery and lynching. The company apologized but declined to explain how the ad cleared its internal vetting process, raising questions about systemic failures in corporate ad approval pipelines.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case highlights a recurring organizational failure in diversity-sensitive review processes—specifically, how homogeneous or insufficiently diverse review teams can create blind spots that allow racially insensitive content through multiple approval stages. The repeated nature of such failures across major brands suggests structural deficiencies in organizational learning.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure that content review processes include genuinely diverse perspectives at every stage of approval, and that organizations conduct post-incident root cause analyses that lead to systemic changes—not just apologies. The pattern of repeated industry failures indicates that many companies treat these incidents as PR problems rather than organizational design problems.
