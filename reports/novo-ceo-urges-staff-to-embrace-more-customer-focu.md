## Summary
Novo Nordisk's CEO is pushing employees to adopt a more customer-focused organizational culture, signaling a strategic cultural shift at the Danish pharmaceutical giant known for its blockbuster weight-loss and diabetes drugs.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects a classic top-down culture change initiative where senior leadership attempts to realign organizational identity and employee behavior around external stakeholders. The success of such efforts depends heavily on whether the espoused values are reinforced through systems, incentives, and middle-management buy-in rather than just CEO rhetoric.

## Leadership Lesson
When a CEO calls for cultural transformation, CHROs and COOs must ensure the message is operationalized through hiring practices, performance metrics, reward systems, and leadership development—otherwise it remains aspirational rather than behavioral. Culture change fails when it stays at the level of speeches without structural reinforcement.
