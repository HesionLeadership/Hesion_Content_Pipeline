## Summary
AI startup Photon held a mock funeral for mobile apps at a developer event, marking its thesis that AI agents working through messaging apps (iMessage, WhatsApp) will replace traditional apps. The company raised $4.5M in seed funding after signing 40,000+ developers and growing revenue 10x in four months, and plans to expand into agent-to-agent communication.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Photon's funeral stunt is an example of symbolic leadership and sensegiving—using ritual and narrative to crystallize a disruptive strategic vision and rally developer/stakeholder belief around an uncertain paradigm shift.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders can use bold symbolic acts and storytelling to create shared meaning around ambiguous strategic bets, helping stakeholders internalize and commit to a new direction before the market fully validates it.
