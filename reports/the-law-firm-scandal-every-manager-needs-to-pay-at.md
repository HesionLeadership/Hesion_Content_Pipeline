## Summary
A law firm scandal involving a viral video of a partner in a power-imbalanced relationship with a subordinate on a park bench has raised urgent questions about workplace boundary enforcement. The story argues that every manager and executive needs to treat relationship boundaries and power dynamics as a core risk management priority.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Power imbalances in hierarchical organizations create conditions where boundary violations can occur with impunity, and failure to enforce policies around supervisor-subordinate relationships exposes organizations to legal, reputational, and cultural harm.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively establish and enforce clear policies on relationships involving power differentials, ensure robust reporting channels, and treat boundary management as a strategic risk issue—not just an HR formality—before a viral moment forces their hand.
