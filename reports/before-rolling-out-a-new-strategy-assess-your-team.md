# Before Rolling Out a New Strategy, Assess Your Team’s Readiness

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-12T12:05:03Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/before-rolling-out-a-new-strategy-assess-your-teams-readiness](https://hbr.org/2026/08/before-rolling-out-a-new-strategy-assess-your-teams-readiness)

## Summary
Before rolling out a new strategy, leaders should assess three conditions of team readiness that will determine whether the plan can be successfully executed: the team's capability, alignment, and commitment.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses change readiness and implementation science—research consistently shows that strategy execution fails not due to the strategy itself but due to insufficient attention to team-level psychological and structural readiness factors including shared mental models, collective efficacy, and goal alignment.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build structured readiness assessments into their strategic planning process, evaluating whether teams have the skills (capability), shared understanding of priorities (alignment), and motivational buy-in (commitment) before launching new initiatives—rather than assuming readiness exists.
