## Summary
The US has confirmed an ongoing investigation into Singapore-based Megaspeed for potentially violating export controls by helping route advanced Nvidia chips to China, a probe that intensified after Nvidia's then-CEO Jensen Huang's extramarital affairs (one allegedly with a Megaspeed executive suspected of spying for China) came to light and he resigned. The episode has also exposed tension between the US Treasury and Commerce Departments over who failed to properly oversee chip export oversight.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how a senior executive's personal conduct can cascade into major organizational, reputational, and national security crises, while also revealing interagency blame-shifting and accountability diffusion when oversight fails.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure conflict-of-interest and personal conduct policies extend to senior executives whose relationships or disclosures could create material business or security risk, and should build cross-functional accountability structures so that oversight failures don't get deflected between departments after the fact.
