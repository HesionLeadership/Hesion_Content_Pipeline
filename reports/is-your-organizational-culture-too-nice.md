# Is Your Organizational Culture Too Nice?

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-19T12:15:08Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/is-your-organizational-culture-too-nice](https://hbr.org/2026/08/is-your-organizational-culture-too-nice)

## Summary
The article examines how organizational cultures that prioritize niceness and harmony over honest feedback and candor can undermine performance, accountability, and innovation. Leaders who avoid difficult conversations create environments where mediocrity is tolerated and high performers become disengaged.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses the tension between psychological safety and accountability—organizations need both candor and trust, and an overemphasis on relational harmony without constructive conflict leads to groupthink, poor decision-making, and erosion of performance standards.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit whether their culture conflates 'niceness' with psychological safety. Build systems that normalize constructive dissent and direct feedback—such as structured feedback rituals, candor norms, and leader modeling of productive conflict—so that accountability and care coexist rather than compete.
