# TikTok Is Laying Off 250 Employees in Its Nashville Office

**Source:** NYT Business RSS  
**Published:** Thu, 06 Aug 2026 00:24:06 +0000  
**URL:** [https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/05/technology/tiktok-layoffs-nashville.html](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/08/05/technology/tiktok-layoffs-nashville.html)

## Summary
TikTok is laying off approximately 250 employees at its Nashville office, which housed members of its content moderation team. The layoffs reflect the company's ongoing workforce restructuring efforts.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Content moderation teams face uniquely high psychological demands—exposure to disturbing content, emotional labor, and now job insecurity. Layoffs in this specific function raise questions about how organizations manage the psychological toll on remaining workers who must absorb increased workloads in already high-stress roles.

## Leadership Lesson
When conducting layoffs in psychologically demanding roles like content moderation, CHROs should pay special attention to survivor syndrome and workload redistribution. Remaining employees face compounded stress from both job insecurity and the inherent emotional burden of the work, requiring proactive mental health support and realistic capacity planning.
