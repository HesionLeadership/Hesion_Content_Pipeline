## Summary
Former Apple retail executive and JCPenney CEO Ron Johnson discusses how his transformation approach succeeded at Apple (creating the Apple Store) but failed at JCPenney, emphasizing the importance of trusting instinct over data in innovation, and reflecting on how he moved too fast at JCPenney without accounting for employee investment in existing ways of working.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how organizational change readiness, employee buy-in, and stakeholder investment in existing systems can determine whether an identical leadership playbook succeeds or fails across different organizational cultures.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that transformation strategies are not universally transferable—leaders must assess an organization's cultural readiness, existing employee investment in current practices, and pace of change before applying a previously successful playbook, and should prioritize listening and observation over speed when entering a new organizational context.
