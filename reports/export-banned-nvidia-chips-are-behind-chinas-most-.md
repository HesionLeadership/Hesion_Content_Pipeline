## Summary
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang left the company after admitting an affair with Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, a firm implicated in smuggling export-banned Nvidia chips into China; new research suggests top Chinese AI models were trained using these banned chips, raising questions about Nvidia's compliance oversight and corporate liability.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how executive personal relationships with customers or business partners can create undisclosed conflicts of interest that compromise organizational oversight, due diligence, and compliance—especially when leaders personally vouch for partners' legitimacy (as Huang did for Megaspeed's data centers).

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat executive personal relationships with major customers, vendors, or partners as material conflict-of-interest risks requiring disclosure and independent verification—especially in regulated industries where leaders' personal credibility can substitute for actual compliance diligence.
