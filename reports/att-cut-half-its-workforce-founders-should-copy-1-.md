## Summary
AT&T cut half its workforce during a major strategic transformation, with the article advising founders to copy AT&T's bold strategic pivot into new markets but avoid its failure to build a supportive company culture during massive organizational change.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Large-scale workforce reductions without attention to culture and change management undermine strategic execution. The disconnect between strategic ambition and organizational culture illustrates how poor leadership during transformation erodes employee trust, engagement, and ultimately business outcomes.

## Leadership Lesson
When undertaking major strategic pivots that involve significant restructuring, CHROs and COOs must invest equally in cultural transformation as in strategic planning—downsizing without a clear people strategy destroys the organizational capability needed to execute the new vision.
