# Why Great Turnarounds Start with Culture, Not Strategy

## Summary
Verizon CEO Dan Schulman discusses how successful corporate turnarounds must begin with cultural transformation rather than strategic planning, particularly as the telecom giant navigates AI disruption and industry change.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The culture-first approach to organizational turnarounds aligns with research on organizational culture as a foundational enabler of strategic execution—without shifting mindsets, values, and norms, new strategies fail to gain traction. This reflects the well-established principle that culture eats strategy for breakfast.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs leading transformation efforts should prioritize cultural diagnostics and alignment before rolling out strategic initiatives; investing in shared values, psychological buy-in, and behavioral norms creates the organizational readiness necessary for strategy to succeed.
