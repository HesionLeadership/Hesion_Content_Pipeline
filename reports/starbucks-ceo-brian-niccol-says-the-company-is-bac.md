# Starbucks CEO Brian Niccol Says the Company Is Back. His Turnaround Comes Down to 1 Leadership Principle

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Thu, 17 Sep 2026 05:30:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/marcel-schwantes/starbucks-ceo-brian-niccol-turnaround-leadership-principle/91406613](https://www.inc.com/marcel-schwantes/starbucks-ceo-brian-niccol-turnaround-leadership-principle/91406613)

## Summary
Starbucks CEO Brian Niccol is leading a turnaround by focusing on getting back to fundamentals—simplifying the menu, improving the in-store experience, and refocusing on core coffee craftsmanship rather than pursuing new innovations or expansions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Niccol's approach reflects the 'back to basics' leadership strategy, where organizational renewal comes not from radical transformation but from re-anchoring the company in its core identity and operational excellence. This aligns with research on organizational identity and how mission drift undermines performance.

## Leadership Lesson
When organizations lose their way through complexity creep and strategic drift, leaders should resist the temptation to add more initiatives and instead audit what made the organization successful in the first place. CHROs and COOs should evaluate whether operational complexity is eroding employee clarity, customer experience, and cultural coherence.
