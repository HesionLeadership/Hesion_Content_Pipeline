## Summary
The article explores how leaders and teams must cope with relentless AI disruption, where the tools and models they invest in are rapidly leapfrogged by newer alternatives, creating a state of perpetual strategic instability and decision-making uncertainty.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Continuous technological disruption creates chronic ambiguity and decision fatigue for leaders and teams, threatening psychological safety, team morale, and the ability to commit to strategic directions. This maps directly to research on organizational adaptation under turbulence and tolerance of ambiguity.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build organizational cultures that treat technology investments as modular and provisional rather than fixed commitments, while developing team resilience and decision-making frameworks that prevent analysis paralysis in the face of constant change. Leaders must normalize iteration and reduce the psychological cost of pivoting.
