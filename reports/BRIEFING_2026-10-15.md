**The Defining Challenge of Responsible AI Is Designing Systems That Preserve Human Judgment When and Where It Counts** (MIT Sloan Review) is the most substantive piece today — an expert panel debate grounded in data (65% agree, 19% disagree) rather than opinion. It matters because it reframes "human-in-the-loop" from a compliance checkbox to a design discipline: organizations must deliberately identify where judgment matters most and build skill-preserving friction into those points, or risk accountability theater without actual capability behind it.
