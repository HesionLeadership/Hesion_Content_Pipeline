## Summary
California's attorney general has issued an investigative subpoena to Nvidia, examining whether the company or departed CEO Jensen Huang misled investors in connection with a scandal involving Huang's relationship with a suspected Chinese spy and potential diversion of advanced chips to China. Nvidia's board has declined to reinstate Huang despite public campaigning by tech figures like Elon Musk for his return.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case highlights board governance under crisis conditions—how a board maintains independence and makes accountability-driven decisions about reinstating a powerful founder-CEO despite external pressure and personal loyalty appeals.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure board succession and crisis-response protocols are insulated from founder charisma or external lobbying, reinforcing that disclosure integrity and conflict-of-interest boundaries apply even to long-tenured, high-status leaders.
