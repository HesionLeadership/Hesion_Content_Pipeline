# ‘Nothing’s Going to Feel Good Now’ for Employees Uncomfortable With Change, says Cisco CEO

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Mon, 21 Sep 2026 06:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/stephanie-mehta/nothings-going-to-feel-good-now-for-employees-uncomfortable-with-change-says-cisco-ceo/91407814](https://www.inc.com/stephanie-mehta/nothings-going-to-feel-good-now-for-employees-uncomfortable-with-change-says-cisco-ceo/91407814)

## Summary
Cisco CEO Chuck Robbins discusses his leadership philosophy regarding organizational change, stating that employees who are uncomfortable with change will find that 'nothing's going to feel good now' as the company undergoes significant transformation, including AI-driven shifts. He reflects on his tenure as Business Roundtable chair and the necessity of pushing through discomfort during periods of rapid change.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses employee resistance to change and how leaders frame and communicate organizational transformation. Robbins' blunt messaging raises questions about whether such direct communication reduces ambiguity or increases threat perception among employees navigating uncertainty.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that while candid communication about inevitable change can reduce false hope and ambiguity, it must be paired with psychological support and clear pathways forward—otherwise, blunt messaging risks amplifying anxiety and disengagement rather than building adaptive capacity.
