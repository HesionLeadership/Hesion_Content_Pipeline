# Algorithms Trap Us in the Familiar. Can They Also Spark Breakthroughs?

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Thu, 20 Aug 2026 11:00:25 +0000  
**URL:** [https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/algorithms-trap-us-in-the-familiar-can-they-also-spark-breakthroughs/](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/algorithms-trap-us-in-the-familiar-can-they-also-spark-breakthroughs/)

## Summary
Algorithmic tools designed to democratize knowledge access may be silently narrowing organizations' creative potential by suppressing the value of expertise. Research reveals that the hidden architecture of these tools traps organizations in familiar patterns, undermining the unique contributions that domain experts bring to innovation.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights a critical tension between technology-driven efficiency and organizational creativity: algorithmic systems can create cognitive homogenization across teams, diminishing the role of expert knowledge diversity that drives breakthrough innovation.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should critically audit how algorithmic and AI-driven tools shape information flow and decision-making within their organizations. Leaders must design systems that amplify rather than flatten expertise, ensuring that technology tools supplement expert judgment rather than replace the cognitive diversity that fuels innovation.
