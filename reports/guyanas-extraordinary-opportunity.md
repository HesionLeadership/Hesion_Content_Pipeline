## Summary
Bloomberg explores Guyana's nation-building challenges as the country manages an extraordinary oil boom, examining the organizational and institutional capacity issues behind rapid economic transformation.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Rapid resource-driven growth creates enormous organizational scaling challenges, requiring a small nation to build institutional capacity, governance structures, and human capital pipelines far faster than typical developmental trajectories allow.

## Leadership Lesson
When organizations experience hypergrowth (whether a startup scaling or a nation industrializing), leaders must prioritize building institutional infrastructure, talent development systems, and governance frameworks proactively—capacity gaps left unaddressed during boom periods become existential risks.
