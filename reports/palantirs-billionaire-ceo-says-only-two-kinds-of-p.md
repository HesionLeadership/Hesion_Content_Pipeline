# Palantir’s billionaire CEO says only two kinds of people will succeed in the AI era: trade workers—‘or you’re neurodivergent’

## Summary
Palantir CEO Alex Karp claims only vocational workers and neurodivergent individuals will thrive in the AI era, and the company has built fellowship programs targeting both groups, including a non-college pathway (Meritocracy Fellowship) and a Neurodivergent Fellowship. The piece contrasts this with Microsoft and Anthropic leaders who argue liberal arts and human-centric skills (EQ, critical thinking) will remain valuable.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story touches on cognitive diversity as a talent strategy and alternative credentialing models, raising questions about how organizations identify, recruit, and structure roles for neurodivergent employees versus relying on traditional degree-based hiring filters.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should consider whether their hiring criteria (degree requirements, standardized interview formats) inadvertently screen out high-potential neurodivergent or non-traditional candidates, and whether structured fellowship/apprenticeship pipelines could surface overlooked talent—but should validate such programs with outcome data rather than anecdote or CEO rhetoric alone.
