## Summary
Supercell, a Finnish mobile gaming company known for its autonomous 'cell' structure and five global hit games, restructured in 2023 into two divisions—New Games (retaining flat, autonomous teams) and Live Games (adding hierarchy to manage mature franchises)—after five years without a new hit, prompting CEO Ilkka Paananen to question whether the company could still attract top talent under more traditional management.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates the tension between organizational structures optimized for creative autonomy versus those optimized for operational scaling and franchise management, highlighting how success itself can create structural inertia that impedes adaptation.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that organizational structures are not permanent competitive advantages—what attracts and enables talent at one stage (radical autonomy for innovation) may need to coexist with more structured, accountable models at another stage (scaling mature products), requiring differentiated management approaches within the same company rather than a one-size-fits-all culture.
