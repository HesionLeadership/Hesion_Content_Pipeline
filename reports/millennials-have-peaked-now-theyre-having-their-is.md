## Summary
Millennials, now largely in middle management, are experiencing a collective existential crisis as they question whether their careers and lives have met expectations. The article explores the disillusionment of a generation that was promised fulfillment through work but now faces economic uncertainty and a 'is this it?' malaise.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects a midcareer meaning crisis among millennials in middle management roles, touching on themes of career plateaus, psychological contract violations, and the gap between expected and realized career outcomes.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively address midcareer disengagement among millennial middle managers by investing in career development pathways, purpose-driven work redesign, and meaningful growth opportunities beyond traditional promotion ladders—before this cohort's disillusionment drives turnover or quiet quitting.
