## Summary
As AI shrinks team sizes into small 'Tiny Teams with Tokens,' coordination among teams becomes the critical bottleneck; Cisco's Outshift incubation engine addresses this with three systems: Tiny Teams (T3s) for productivity, a shared memory engine (TOME) for context, and cross-functional Coordination Leads (CLs) for resolving priorities and conflicts.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The piece illustrates how reducing team size via AI augmentation does not eliminate coordination costs but relocates them to inter-team boundaries, requiring new structures for shared mental models, decision rights, and conflict resolution rather than relying on individual team cohesion alone.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that AI-driven productivity gains at the individual/team level will stall without parallel investment in cross-team coordination mechanisms—shared memory systems and clearly empowered cross-functional coordination roles—otherwise decision quality and organizational coherence will degrade even as output metrics look healthy.
