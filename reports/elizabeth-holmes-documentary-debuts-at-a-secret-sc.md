## Summary
A documentary about convicted Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes, titled 'You Can See Everything,' debuted at a secret screening at the Telluride Film Festival. The film, created by Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim, features interviews with Holmes about her rise and fall.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The Elizabeth Holmes/Theranos case remains a landmark study in how charismatic leadership, organizational fraud, and toxic corporate culture can persist when boards, investors, and employees fail to exercise critical oversight. The documentary format may offer new psychological insights into the self-narratives of leaders who engage in corporate deception.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build organizational systems that challenge charismatic authority with robust accountability structures—including independent board oversight, whistleblower protections, and cultures where questioning leadership is normalized rather than punished.
