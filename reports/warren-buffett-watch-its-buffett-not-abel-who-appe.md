# Warren Buffett Watch: It's Buffett, not Abel, who appears to be calling the shots on stocks

**Source:** CNBC CEO  
**Published:** Sat, 22 Aug 2026 12:06:37 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/22/warren-buffett-not-greg-abel-appears-to-still-be-calling-the-shots-on-stocks.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/22/warren-buffett-not-greg-abel-appears-to-still-be-calling-the-shots-on-stocks.html)

## Summary
New Berkshire Hathaway CEO Greg Abel appears to be deferring all equity investment decisions to predecessor Warren Buffett and portfolio manager Ted Weschler, raising questions about the true locus of decision-making authority in the post-succession era.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the classic challenge of CEO succession where the predecessor retains informal authority, creating ambiguity around decision rights and potentially undermining the new leader's ability to establish autonomous leadership identity.

## Leadership Lesson
When planning leadership transitions, boards and outgoing leaders must establish clear decision-making boundaries. CHROs and COOs should ensure succession plans explicitly delineate which decisions transfer immediately versus gradually, as lingering predecessor influence can erode the successor's credibility and organizational clarity.

## Strength Score
**8/10** — This is a textbook case of succession dynamics, shadow leadership, and decision-making authority transfer in a high-profile organization. It directly maps to well-studied org psych phenomena around CEO succession, role ambiguity, and the predecessor's continued influence on organizational governance.
