# Why the AI age calls for ‘founder mode’

**Published:** Wed, 07 Oct 2026 14:40:13 +0000

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Fortune's editor discusses a wave of 'founder mode' leadership—hands-on, conviction-driven, agile—as the response CEOs need to AI disruption, featuring interviews with Sam Altman (OpenAI's AI risk and IPO plans) and Mary Barra (GM), plus examples like Nikesh Arora (Palo Alto Networks), David Cote (Vertiv), Andrew Forrest (Fortescue), and Travis Kalanick's new venture Atoms.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The piece frames 'founder mode' as a shift away from bureaucratic, consensus-driven technocratic leadership toward high-agency, close-to-the-work decision-making—raising classic tensions between centralized control and distributed empowerment that org psychology has long studied.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that 'founder mode' can boost speed and conviction but risks concentrating decision rights, bypassing checks, and eroding psychological safety if not paired with structures for dissent and distributed expertise—especially when wielded by leaders without founder legitimacy.
