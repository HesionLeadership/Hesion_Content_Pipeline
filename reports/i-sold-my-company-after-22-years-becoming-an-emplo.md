# I Sold My Company After 22 Years. Becoming an Employee Was the Hard Part

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Sat, 12 Sep 2026 05:00:00 -0400

## Summary
A founder who sold their company after 22 years describes the psychological and behavioral challenges of transitioning from owner/CEO to employee within the same organization, likening it to learning to drive on the other side of the road.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story highlights the identity disruption and role transition challenges founders face post-acquisition, where deeply ingrained autonomy and decision-making authority must be recalibrated to fit a subordinate role—a profound shift in psychological ownership and self-concept.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs managing post-acquisition integrations should design structured role-transition support for founders who stay on, including clear expectation-setting, identity coaching, and gradual authority handoffs, as founder retention failures often stem from unaddressed psychological ownership conflicts rather than compensation or title issues.
