# Flock reportedly tries to shrink workforce with employee buyouts

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** Sat, 19 Sep 2026 20:39:13 +0000

## Summary
Flock Safety, a surveillance technology company, is offering employee buyouts to reduce its workforce, indicating that without voluntary departures, layoffs would be 'almost certainly' necessary.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Voluntary buyout programs represent a softer approach to workforce reduction that can preserve organizational trust and psychological safety compared to involuntary layoffs, though they introduce survivor syndrome risks and can lead to adverse selection where top performers leave first.

## Leadership Lesson
When downsizing is inevitable, offering buyouts before layoffs signals respect for employee agency and can mitigate the procedural justice concerns that erode morale and engagement—but CHROs must carefully design packages to retain critical talent and manage the uncertainty that buyout announcements create across the organization.
