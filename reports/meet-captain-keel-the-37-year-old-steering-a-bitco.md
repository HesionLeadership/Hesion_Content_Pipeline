## Summary
Ben Gagnon, now 37, led Bitfarms through boardroom turmoil and a hostile takeover attempt by Riot Platforms, then engineered its complete transformation from Bitcoin mining into an AI/HPC energy infrastructure company, rebranding it as Keel Infrastructure in April 2026.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates organizational identity transformation under crisis conditions—a leader must simultaneously defend the organization's boundaries (fending off a hostile takeover), stabilize internal conflict (board turnover, lawsuits), and pivot organizational identity and strategy, all while maintaining stakeholder trust.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs/COOs should note that successful strategic pivots often require sequencing crisis stabilization before transformation—Gagnon first resolved the takeover fight and governance conflict before pursuing the business model pivot, suggesting that psychological and structural stability must precede major strategic change efforts.
