# Gartner Says Companies May Rehire 30 Percent of Workers Replaced by AI. Here’s the Leadership Lesson

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Wed, 16 Sep 2026 05:30:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/marcel-schwantes/gartner-companies-rehire-workers-replaced-ai-leadership-lesson/91405844](https://www.inc.com/marcel-schwantes/gartner-companies-rehire-workers-replaced-ai-leadership-lesson/91405844)

## Summary
Gartner predicts that by 2028, companies will rehire 30% of workers previously replaced by AI after discovering that AI implementation costs, quality issues, and lost institutional knowledge outweigh the savings. The leadership lesson centers on avoiding reactive, herd-mentality decisions about workforce automation and instead taking a measured approach to AI adoption.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects the well-documented phenomenon of organizational bandwagon effects and escalation of commitment in reverse—companies rushing to adopt AI without adequate workforce planning may suffer from loss of tacit knowledge, institutional memory, and the human judgment that AI cannot replicate, leading to costly course corrections.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should resist pressure to pursue aggressive AI-driven headcount reductions without first conducting rigorous task-level analyses of which roles genuinely benefit from automation versus those where human expertise, contextual judgment, and institutional knowledge are irreplaceable. Building a deliberate augmentation-over-replacement strategy preserves organizational capability while still capturing AI's efficiency gains.
