# David Ellison just brought in a co-CEO to run his new empire: Meet Ynon Kreiz

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
David Ellison has named Ynon Kreiz, outgoing Mattel CEO, as co-CEO of the newly merged Paramount Skydance and Warner Bros. Discovery entity, with Ellison focusing on strategy/vision and Kreiz overseeing daily operations and integration of the two media giants.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates a co-CEO/shared leadership structure where roles are explicitly divided (visionary founder vs. operational integrator), raising classic questions about role clarity, power-sharing, and whether a leader skilled at cost-cutting turnarounds can also sustain long-term strategic innovation.

## Leadership Lesson
When installing co-leadership structures during high-stakes mergers, CHROs/COOs should clearly define decision rights and accountability boundaries upfront, and should scrutinize whether a leader's prior turnaround success (cost-cutting) translates to the current challenge (innovation-driven integration), since operational excellence and strategic/innovation leadership require different skill sets.
