# CEOs are still betting on the leadership trait AI can’t replace

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Wed, 16 Sep 2026 09:44:04 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/09/16/ceos-leadership-trait-ai-cant-replace/](https://fortune.com/2026/09/16/ceos-leadership-trait-ai-cant-replace/)

## Summary
CEOs continue to prioritize emotional intelligence as the leadership trait that AI cannot replicate, emphasizing human-centered skills like empathy, relationship-building, and trust as essential differentiators in an increasingly automated business landscape.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
As AI automates technical and analytical tasks, the value of emotional intelligence in leadership rises—aligning with research showing that EI predicts leadership effectiveness, team performance, and organizational commitment beyond cognitive ability alone.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should double down on developing emotional intelligence competencies in their leadership pipelines, treating empathy, interpersonal skill, and relational trust as strategic capabilities rather than soft skills, especially as AI handles more cognitive and analytical work.
