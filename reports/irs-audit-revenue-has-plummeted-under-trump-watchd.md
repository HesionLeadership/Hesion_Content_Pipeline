# IRS Audit Revenue Has Plummeted Under Trump, Watchdog Finds

**Published:** Mon, 31 Aug 2026 22:12:33 +0000

## Summary
The IRS has lost roughly a quarter of its workforce since President Trump took office, leading to a significant drop in audit revenue. The massive staffing reduction has hampered the agency's core operational capacity.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Rapid, large-scale workforce reductions can devastate organizational capability far beyond simple headcount—institutional knowledge loss, survivor syndrome among remaining employees, and degraded core functions create compounding operational failures.

## Leadership Lesson
When implementing workforce reductions, leaders must conduct capability-impact analyses before cutting. Losing 25% of staff without strategic planning for knowledge transfer and workload redistribution can cripple an organization's ability to fulfill its mission—a cautionary tale for any COO overseeing restructuring.
