## Summary
The article explores the convergence of AI and product management, arguing that organizations need both AI-focused product managers and product-minded AI leaders, with technical product managers positioning themselves as candidates for the emerging Chief AI Officer role.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects the organizational challenge of role ambiguity and boundary spanning when new technologies create hybrid positions that don't fit neatly into existing hierarchies. Organizations must navigate identity tensions between technical and strategic functions as AI reshapes job architectures.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively define the competency frameworks and reporting structures for emerging AI leadership roles rather than letting them evolve organically, which risks turf wars and role conflict between engineering, product, and strategy functions.
