# Forget Commuter Benefits: Employees Are Demanding This $21-a-Day Perk Instead

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Mon, 31 Aug 2026 11:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/chloe-rosenberg/forget-commuter-benefits-employees-are-demanding-this-21-a-day-perk-instead/91398342](https://www.inc.com/chloe-rosenberg/forget-commuter-benefits-employees-are-demanding-this-21-a-day-perk-instead/91398342)

## Summary
A growing number of companies are offering meal stipends of up to $500 per month (roughly $21 per day) to entice hybrid employees back to the office, replacing traditional commuter benefits like train passes and parking stipends as the most in-demand workplace perk.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Food-based perks tap into both extrinsic motivation and social belonging—shared meals create informal bonding opportunities that strengthen team cohesion, which is particularly critical in hybrid environments where in-person interaction is scarce and intentional.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should evaluate whether their benefits packages reflect the actual needs of a hybrid workforce rather than legacy commuting assumptions; strategically designed meal benefits can serve dual purposes as both retention tools and catalysts for in-office collaboration and social connection.
