# McDonald's CEO expects high inflation, lackluster traffic are here to stay

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Wed, 23 Sep 2026 20:23:52 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/mcdonalds-investor-day-ceo-chris-kempczinksi-inflation.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/mcdonalds-investor-day-ceo-chris-kempczinksi-inflation.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
McDonald's CEO Chris Kempczinski said persistent inflation and weak customer traffic are the new normal for the restaurant industry, not a temporary rough patch, and urged his team to stop calling it a 'difficult environment' and just call it 'the environment.' The company is focusing on gaining market share from competitors while being cautious about further price increases after acknowledging it raised prices too fast post-pandemic.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Kempczinski's reframing directive—shifting language from 'difficult environment' to 'the environment'—is a deliberate sensemaking intervention aimed at ending organizational denial and normalizing sustained adversity as the baseline for strategic planning.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that how leadership frames prolonged challenges internally shapes whether teams treat them as exceptions to endure or realities to strategize around; explicitly retiring language that implies a return to 'normal' can accelerate adaptive planning and reduce wishful thinking in execution.
