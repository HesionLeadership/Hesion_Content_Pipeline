## Summary
An advice column addressing a manager's dilemma about how to discuss an employee's clothing choices with her when she technically adheres to the company dress code but clients are making comments about her appearance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the tension between formal policy compliance and informal workplace norms, raising issues of appearance-based discrimination, managerial feedback delivery, and the psychological impact of subjective standards on employee dignity and belonging.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders must distinguish between enforceable dress code standards and subjective appearance preferences. If client feedback is driving the conversation, managers should examine whether the feedback reflects legitimate business concerns or bias—and ensure any conversation preserves the employee's psychological safety and sense of fairness.
