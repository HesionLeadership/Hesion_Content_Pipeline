# Employees Want Financial Stability Perks Over Wellbeing Benefits, Study Finds

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Fri, 28 Aug 2026 13:24:09 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/employees-want-financial-stability-perks-over-wellbeing-benefits-study-finds/91397671](https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/employees-want-financial-stability-perks-over-wellbeing-benefits-study-finds/91397671)

## Summary
Gartner research shows 55% of employees experience severe financial anxiety, leading to a significant shift in employee preferences away from traditional wellbeing benefits (like meditation apps and gym memberships) toward financial stability perks such as medical coverage, job security, and income-focused benefits.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects Maslow's hierarchy of needs in action within the workplace—when employees' foundational needs around financial security feel threatened, higher-order wellness perks lose perceived value. It also highlights the gap between what organizations offer (signaling care) and what employees actually need (substantive support), which can erode perceived organizational support.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit their benefits portfolios against actual employee needs rather than trending wellness fads. Prioritizing financial stability perks—emergency savings programs, competitive medical coverage, transparent job security communication—will yield stronger engagement and retention than surface-level wellbeing initiatives that fail to address root anxieties.
