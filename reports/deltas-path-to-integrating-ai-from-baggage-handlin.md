# Delta’s path to integrating AI, from baggage handling to customer service

**Source:** Fast Company  
**Published:** 2026-09-02T11:00:00  
**URL:** [https://www.fastcompany.com/91596459/amala-duggirala-delta-pacesetters-2026](https://www.fastcompany.com/91596459/amala-duggirala-delta-pacesetters-2026)

## Summary
Delta's new Chief Digital & Technology Officer Amala Duggirala is leading the airline's AI integration strategy, arguing that 'technology is the easy part and culture is the hard part.' Her approach emphasizes operationalizing AI rather than experimenting, training leadership immersively before broad rollouts, reimagining processes rather than just automating tasks, and cultivating 'relentless learners' — building on her prior success at USAA where she trained 38,000 employees in AI awareness.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Duggirala's philosophy directly addresses the technology adoption paradox: organizations fail not because of technical limitations but because of cultural resistance and insufficient change management. Her emphasis on leadership training before employee rollout reflects research on top-down modeling of new behaviors and the critical role of organizational learning culture in successful transformation.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat AI adoption as a culture change initiative, not a technology project. This means investing in leadership immersion and workforce reskilling before broad deployment, structurally unifying digital and technology functions under one leader with C-suite access, and framing AI as process reimagination rather than task automation to overcome 'wait and see' resistance.

<p class="wp-block-paragraph">At the tech industry event CES in January 2025, Delta&#8217;s CEO, Ed Bastian, <a href="https://news.delta.com/delta-soars-centennial-year-game-changing-innovations-ces-2025">unveiled</a> Delta Concierge, the airline&#8217;s generative <a href="https://www.fastcompany.com/section/artificial-intelligence" title="AI">AI</a> travel assistant. Its full rollout has happened under the watch of the new chief digital &amp; technology officer, Amala Duggirala. As of August 2026, the tool <a href="https://www.futuretravelexperience.com/2026/08/delta-rolls-out-ai-powered-concierge-to-all-skymiles-members-for-personalised-travel-support/">reaches</a> 100% of SkyMiles members to assist flyers with disruption support, self-service cancellations, and bag tracking.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Days into her Delta tenure, Amala Duggirala laid out her AI philosophy in a January 2026 essay on Medium, <a href="https://medium.com/@amaladuggirala/headline-technology-is-the-easy-part-and-culture-is-the-hard-part-dc1842def522">&#8220;Technology is the Easy Part, and Culture is the Hard Part.&#8221;</a> Her core argument: With generative artificial intelligence, the technology isn&#8217;t the hard part. It’s adoption. &#8220;The technology is the vehicle, but culture is the engine,&#8221; she wrote, warning that the tempting &#8220;wait and see&#8221; approach is dangerous and risks leaving organizations behind.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Duggirala urges leaders to follow her lead at the airline: Operationalize generative AI rather than treating it merely as an experiment; train leadership immersively before rolling tools out widely; move from automating tasks to reimagining processes entirely; and cultivate &#8220;relentless learners.&#8221; &#8220;We&#8217;ve moved past &#8216;if AI&#8217; to &#8216;how do we get ROI now,'&#8221; she <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7392336181088247808/">wrote</a> on LinkedIn.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Duggirala <a href="https://news.delta.com/delta-cio-rahul-samant-retire-after-leading-technology-transformation">joined</a> Delta in January 2026 in a <a href="https://www.ciodive.com/news/delta-cio-rahul-samant-retires/809162/">new role</a> uniting the airline&#8217;s enterprise technology and digital product functions under one leader for the first time, helping to build on AI successes from before her tenure, like Baggage AI, a real-time vehicle routing and dispatch optimizer pilot at the Atlanta airport that the airline says resulted in a 30% improvement in bag transfers. She <a href="https://news.delta.com/amala-duggirala-evp-and-chief-digital-and-technology-officer">reports</a> directly to Bastian, the CEO, and sits on the company&#8217;s leadership committee. In announcing her <a href="https://www.fastcompany.com/section/hiring" title="Hiring">hiring</a>, Delta said Duggirala is <a href="https://news.delta.com/amala-duggirala-evp-and-chief-digital-and-technology-officer">deeply committed</a> to &#8220;equipping Delta teams with advanced, agentic, and data-informed solutions.&#8221;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">She arrived at Delta on the strength of her success as executive VP and CIO at <a href="https://news.delta.com/amala-duggirala-evp-and-chief-digital-and-technology-officer">USAA</a>, where she made AI adoption an enterprise-wide effort, emphasizing workforce training and reskilling alongside technology deployment. During her tenure, USAA trained roughly 38,000 employees in AI awareness and grew to more than 200 AI products. </p>
