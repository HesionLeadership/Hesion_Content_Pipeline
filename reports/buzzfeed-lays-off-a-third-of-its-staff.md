# BuzzFeed Lays Off a Third of Its Staff

**Source:** NYT Business RSS  
**Published:** Tue, 28 Jul 2026 01:11:25 +0000  
**URL:** [https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/27/business/buzzfeed-huffpost-layoffs-byron-allen.html](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/27/business/buzzfeed-huffpost-layoffs-byron-allen.html)

## Summary
BuzzFeed's new owner Byron Allen is laying off roughly a third of its workforce (~180 employees) as part of efforts to reduce the company's financial losses following his acquisition.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Large-scale layoffs following an ownership transition represent a critical juncture for survivor syndrome and organizational identity disruption. Employees who remain face uncertainty about the new leader's vision, eroding psychological safety and engagement during a period that demands both.

## Leadership Lesson
When new ownership necessitates deep cuts, CHROs and COOs must proactively manage survivor morale and clearly communicate a forward-looking strategy. Cutting a third of staff without a compelling narrative about the company's future risks losing top talent voluntarily and creating a downward spiral of disengagement.
