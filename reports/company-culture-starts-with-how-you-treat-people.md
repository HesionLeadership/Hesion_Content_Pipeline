## Summary
Inc. 5000 honoree Natasha Miller shares how she initially underestimated the importance of company culture, but after deliberately investing in people, core values, and cultural development, her company became stronger and better positioned for sustained growth.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story reflects the well-established link between organizational culture investment and firm performance, illustrating how founder-led cultural transformation—shifting from task-focus to people-focus—can serve as a competitive advantage and growth enabler.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat culture as a strategic investment rather than a byproduct of operations. Deliberately defining core values, investing in employee experience, and modeling people-first leadership can unlock scalable growth and improve retention.
