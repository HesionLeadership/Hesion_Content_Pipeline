# The problem is not the AI code, but nobody knows anything anymore

**Source:** Hacker News  
**Published:** Mon, 28 Sep 2026 16:11:42 +0000  
**URL:** [https://www.ssp.sh/brain/the-problem-is-not-the-ai-code-but-nobody-knows-anything-anymore/](https://www.ssp.sh/brain/the-problem-is-not-the-ai-code-but-nobody-knows-anything-anymore/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
The article argues that the real problem with AI coding tools isn't code quality but the erosion of organizational knowledge, as engineers and teams increasingly rely on AI (e.g., Claude) without understanding system architecture or the reasoning behind decisions. It cites examples of engineers working long hours just to prompt AI and ship code without comprehension, raising concerns about maintainability and loss of institutional knowledge, especially among junior hires.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates a breakdown in organizational learning and sensemaking, where tacit knowledge, shared mental models, and collective understanding of 'why' decisions were made are being displaced by tool-mediated shortcuts, leading to deskilling and loss of psychological ownership over work.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure that speed-driven AI adoption doesn't outpace knowledge transfer and onboarding practices; leaders must protect time for comprehension, documentation, and mentorship (especially for junior talent) to prevent institutional knowledge decay and maintain long-term system maintainability.
