## Summary
Shake Shack founder Danny Meyer discusses the principles he follows when scaling organizational culture, including hiring for emotional skills, prioritizing employees over customers, and maintaining cultural consistency during rapid growth.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Meyer's approach highlights the tension between scaling operations and preserving organizational culture—a core challenge in organizational psychology. His emphasis on hiring for interpersonal qualities over technical skills aligns with research on person-organization fit and its impact on service quality and retention.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design hiring systems that assess cultural fit and emotional intelligence alongside technical competence, and invest in leadership development that reinforces core values as the organization scales—because culture erodes fastest during periods of rapid growth if it isn't intentionally protected.
