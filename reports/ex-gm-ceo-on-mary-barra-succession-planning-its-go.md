**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Former GM CEO Dan Akerson discusses the difficulty GM's board faces in succession planning for CEO Mary Barra, noting the company is reportedly working with an executive search firm to evaluate both internal candidates (Sterling Anderson, Paul Jacobson, Mark Reuss) and outside prospects amid industry disruption from AI, EVs, and autonomous vehicles.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates executive succession planning as a structured, longitudinal organizational process involving CEO input, board evaluation, and insider-vs-outsider risk calculus, reflecting known tensions between continuity (internal promotion) and adaptability (external hires) in leadership transitions.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat succession planning as a continuous, multi-year process with formal evaluation criteria (strategic vision, collaboration, adaptability) rather than a reactive event, and should weigh the documented risks of external hires against the benefits of established internal leadership pipelines.
