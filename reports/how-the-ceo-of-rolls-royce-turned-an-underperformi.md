## Summary
HBS professor Nitin Nohria discusses the case of Tufan Erginbilgiç, who became CEO of Rolls-Royce in 2023 and declared the company was on a 'burning platform' due to poor financial performance despite strong engineering culture. He began preparing for the role before his official start date, assembled his management team early, and used radical candor and data-driven urgency to confront a culture that prioritized engineering achievement over financial discipline.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how strong, identity-defining cultures (here, engineering excellence) can create blind spots that suppress attention to other critical performance dimensions, and how leaders can use data and direct confrontation to surface truths employees already sense but haven't acknowledged.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit whether a strong, source-of-pride culture is masking performance blind spots, and consider that incoming leaders who prepare and engage before their official start date can accelerate trust-building and organizational diagnosis—provided they pair urgency with respect for existing talent and credibility-building through data.
