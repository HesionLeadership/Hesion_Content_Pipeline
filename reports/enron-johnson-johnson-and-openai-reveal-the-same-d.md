## Summary
The article examines a common pattern across Enron, Johnson & Johnson, and OpenAI where successful organizations gradually drift from their founding values and ethical commitments through a process of incremental normalization, ultimately becoming unrecognizable from their original missions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story highlights the phenomenon of ethical erosion and normalization of deviance within organizations—where small, incremental departures from stated values compound over time until the organization's culture fundamentally shifts. It connects to research on how success and growth can create pressures that systematically override ethical guardrails and mission alignment.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should implement structural mechanisms for regular values audits and create independent channels for surfacing mission drift before it becomes entrenched. Leaders must recognize that organizational decay is rarely sudden—it's the accumulation of small compromises that feel rational in the moment, making proactive cultural monitoring and honest self-assessment essential leadership practices.
