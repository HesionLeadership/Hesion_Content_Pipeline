## Summary
Cameroon's 91-year-old President Paul Biya, who has not been seen publicly for 58 days, issued a decree reshuffling the country's military leadership, raising questions about his capacity to govern and the transparency of decision-making at the top.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story highlights the organizational risks of concentrated authority in an absent or incapacitated leader, including succession ambiguity, information asymmetry, and the use of symbolic actions (military reshuffles) to project control without visible engagement.

## Leadership Lesson
Organizations must establish clear succession planning and governance transparency mechanisms. When a leader's presence and capacity become uncertain, the absence of visible leadership erodes institutional trust and creates power vacuums that can destabilize the entire organization.
