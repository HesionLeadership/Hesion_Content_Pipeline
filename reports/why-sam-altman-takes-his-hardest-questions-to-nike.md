## Summary
Palo Alto Networks CEO Nikesh Arora is known for blunt, high-pressure directness—developed at Google—and serves as a trusted advisor to OpenAI's Sam Altman, who says direct, actionable feedback is rare and valuable. Arora justifies his exacting standards by comparing cybersecurity work to high-stakes fields like aerospace, where mistakes can be catastrophic.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates the tension between psychological safety and performance accountability—Arora's model suggests that brutal honesty and high expectations, rather than softened feedback, can build trust and effective mentorship when paired with clear high-stakes framing.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that candid, rigorous feedback—when consistently applied and clearly tied to legitimate high-stakes outcomes—can be more valued by high performers than diplomatic vagueness; however, this approach requires careful calibration to avoid fear-based cultures rather than accountability-based ones.
