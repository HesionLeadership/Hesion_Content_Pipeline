# Don’t Let AI Flatten Your Leadership Style

**Source:** HBR

## Summary
HBR article advising leaders on how to avoid letting AI tools homogenize their leadership communication, judgment, and executive presence. It warns that over-reliance on AI-generated content can erode authentic voice, flatten decision-making nuance, and diminish the distinctive qualities that define effective leadership.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This connects directly to authentic leadership theory—leaders who outsource their thinking and communication to AI risk losing the perceived authenticity and credibility that drive follower trust and engagement. It also touches on cognitive offloading and its impact on leader identity and sensemaking.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should establish guidelines for AI use that preserve leaders' authentic voice and critical judgment. Encourage leaders to use AI as a drafting tool rather than a thinking replacement, and build development programs that strengthen—not outsource—executive presence and decision-making capabilities.
