# I run an organization dedicated to collective grieving. I recognize a familiar dynamic with AI in the workplace

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sun, 13 Sep 2026 11:30:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/09/13/ai-workplace-grief-leadership/](https://fortune.com/2026/09/13/ai-workplace-grief-leadership/)

## Summary
The leader of a collective grieving organization draws parallels between grief processes and the unspoken emotional toll AI is having on workers, arguing that AI adoption is creating workplace grief over lost expertise, eroded trust, and threatened career identity.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
AI-driven workplace transformation triggers identity threat and ambiguous loss among employees whose skills, professional identity, and sense of competence are being devalued—mirroring grief responses that organizations rarely acknowledge or address.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively create space for employees to process the emotional and identity-related impacts of AI adoption, treating technological transitions not just as reskilling challenges but as organizational change events that require psychological support, transparent communication, and meaning-making opportunities.
