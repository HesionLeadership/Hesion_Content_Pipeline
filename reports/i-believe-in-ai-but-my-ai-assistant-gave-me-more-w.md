# I believe in AI. But my AI assistant gave me more work than it could finish

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Fri, 16 Oct 2026 12:15:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/10/16/ai-assistants-productivity-task-overload/](https://fortune.com/2026/10/16/ai-assistants-productivity-task-overload/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
A writer describes how her AI assistant generated a 156-item to-do list, illustrating how AI can create work faster than it finishes it. She outlines five changes to fix this: 1) Limit the daily list to roughly 25 meaningful entries reflecting actual capacity; 2) Group related tasks under one execution item instead of listing them separately; 3) Require proof attached to completion (e.g., live links, delivery records); 4) Demand specific blockers instead of vague 'unable to complete' messages; 5) Track outcome counts (pitches sent, replies needed, deliverables completed) rather than just listing possible actions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates the psychological distinction between activity and accomplishment—how systems (human or AI) can reward the appearance of productivity (planning, checklists) over actual task completion, a dynamic well-documented in goal-setting and performance measurement research.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design workflow and performance systems that measure completed outcomes and proof of delivery rather than volume of generated tasks or plans, since unchecked task-generation (by AI tools or process bureaucracy) can create the illusion of productivity while increasing cognitive load and reducing real throughput.
