# I helped grow a steel company from $750 million to $12 billion. CEOs should use AI before they grade employees on it

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sun, 15 Nov 2026 12:30:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/15/ceos-ai-performance-reviews-compensation-executives-geoff-woods/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/15/ceos-ai-performance-reviews-compensation-executives-geoff-woods/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
A former steel company executive argues that CEOs who mandate AI use among employees often fail to use AI themselves for strategic thinking, and shares how he used AI as a 'thought partner' (via a CRIT framework: Context, Role, Interview, Task) to help grow Jindal Steel's market cap from $750 million to $12 billion.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates a leadership credibility gap: leaders who mandate behavioral change without modeling it themselves (walking the talk) undermine adoption and trust, a dynamic related to leader-member exchange and perceived organizational hypocrisy.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure AI (or any mandated capability) adoption metrics measure quality of strategic application, not just usage frequency, and should require executives to model the same behaviors and standards they impose on employees before tying compensation to new skills.
