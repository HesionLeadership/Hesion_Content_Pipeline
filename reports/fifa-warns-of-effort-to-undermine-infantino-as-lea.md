## Summary
FIFA has warned of an organized effort to undermine President Gianni Infantino, signaling a deepening leadership crisis within the global soccer governing body. The situation reflects internal power struggles and governance challenges at the top of one of the world's most influential sports organizations.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story highlights classic dynamics of leadership legitimacy threats within large governing organizations—when institutional leaders frame internal dissent or external criticism as 'undermining,' it can signal either genuine coordinated opposition or defensive leadership seeking to consolidate power by delegitimizing challengers.

## Leadership Lesson
When facing organized opposition, leaders must distinguish between legitimate governance concerns and personal attacks. CHROs and COOs should ensure institutional mechanisms exist for constructive dissent so that criticism doesn't devolve into destabilizing power struggles—and so that leaders don't reflexively frame all opposition as illegitimate threats.
