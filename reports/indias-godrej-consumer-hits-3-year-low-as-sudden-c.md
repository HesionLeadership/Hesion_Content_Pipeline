## Summary
Godrej Consumer Products' shares fell to a 3-year low after the sudden and unexpected departure of CEO Sudhir Sitapati, raising investor concerns about leadership stability and the company's strategic direction.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Sudden CEO departures without clear succession planning erode stakeholder confidence and signal potential organizational instability. The market reaction reflects how leadership continuity is a critical factor in perceived organizational health and investor trust.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs must ensure robust succession planning and leadership pipeline development so that unexpected executive departures don't create a vacuum that threatens organizational stability and stakeholder confidence. Transparent communication during leadership transitions is essential to maintaining trust.
