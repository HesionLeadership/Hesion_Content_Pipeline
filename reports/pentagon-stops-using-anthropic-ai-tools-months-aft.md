# Pentagon stops using Anthropic AI tools months after blacklisting company, BBC told

**Source:** BBC Business  
**Published:** Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:13:21 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5j9x9pr0240o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c5j9x9pr0240o?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
The Pentagon says it has fully stopped using Anthropic's AI tools, months after designating the company a 'national security supply-chain risk' when it refused to remove safety guardrails for military use. Sources say Claude was still being used in intelligence and operations as recently as last week, embedded in Palantir's Maven system, illustrating how difficult it was to actually remove once integrated.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights a classic organizational decoupling problem: formal policy announcements (banning Anthropic) diverged sharply from actual practice on the ground, where deeply embedded tools continued to be used due to integration complexity and operational necessity.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that mandated policy changes—especially around technology vendors—often face significant implementation lag when tools are deeply embedded in workflows; leaders must assess technical/operational dependencies before setting public deadlines, or risk a visible gap between stated policy and actual practice.
