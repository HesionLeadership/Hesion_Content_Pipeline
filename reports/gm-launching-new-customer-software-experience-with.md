# GM launching new customer software experience with redesigned Chevy, GMC pickups

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Tue, 15 Sep 2026 11:00:01 GMT

## Summary
GM is launching a redesigned customer software experience with its new Chevy and GMC pickups, and the shift toward more digital vehicles has prompted the company to also fundamentally redesign its internal organizational processes and team structures.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
GM's decision to restructure organizational processes alongside a product transformation reflects the principle that technological change requires concurrent organizational redesign—aligning structure, workflows, and culture to support new capabilities rather than layering digital products onto legacy processes.

## Leadership Lesson
When undertaking major product or technology transformations, leaders must simultaneously redesign internal organizational processes and cross-functional collaboration models; failing to align the organization's operating model with its product strategy creates friction that undermines both innovation and execution.
