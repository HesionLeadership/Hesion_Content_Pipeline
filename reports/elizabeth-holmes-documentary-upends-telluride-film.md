## Organizational Psychology Angle
The Elizabeth Holmes/Theranos case remains a landmark study in how charismatic leadership, toxic organizational culture, and deceptive impression management can sustain massive fraud. The documentary raises questions about narcissistic leadership and how leaders construct compelling narratives that override organizational checks and balances.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build governance structures and psychological safety cultures where employees can challenge charismatic leaders without retaliation—Theranos failed because Holmes's persona suppressed dissent and critical questioning at every organizational level.
