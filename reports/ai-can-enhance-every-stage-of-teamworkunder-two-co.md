# AI Can Enhance Every Stage of Teamwork—Under Two Conditions

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-09-07T12:05:30Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/09/ai-can-enhance-every-stage-of-teamwork-under-two-conditions](https://hbr.org/2026/09/ai-can-enhance-every-stage-of-teamwork-under-two-conditions)

## Summary
A new HBR analysis finds that AI can enhance every stage of teamwork—before, during, and after meetings—but only under two conditions: teams must intentionally integrate AI into their workflows rather than using it ad hoc, and they must maintain human judgment and oversight throughout the process.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses team effectiveness and group decision-making processes, highlighting how technology adoption interacts with team coordination, information sharing, and collective intelligence—core topics in organizational psychology.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should develop structured protocols for AI integration into team workflows rather than leaving adoption to individual discretion. Establishing intentional, stage-specific AI usage guidelines—while preserving human oversight—can improve meeting quality, decision-making, and team outcomes at scale.
