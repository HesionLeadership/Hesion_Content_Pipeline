## Summary
The article explores how improved communication between engineering/design teams and shop floor/manufacturing teams can bridge perspective gaps and drive manufacturing efficiency. Better cross-functional dialogue is positioned as a key but often overlooked lever for operational performance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses cross-functional communication barriers and silo mentality in manufacturing organizations—a well-studied phenomenon in organizational psychology where differing team identities, mental models, and professional cultures create coordination failures that undermine performance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should invest in structured mechanisms (joint problem-solving sessions, cross-functional rotations, shared metrics) that bridge the cognitive and cultural divide between design/engineering and production teams. Improving inter-team communication is often a higher-ROI intervention than process or technology upgrades alone.
