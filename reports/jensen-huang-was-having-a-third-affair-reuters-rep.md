## Summary
Nvidia founder Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO and board member following revelations of multiple affairs with employees, including one with an accused spy; CFO Colette Kress is serving as interim CEO while no permanent successor has been named.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how power asymmetries between senior executives and subordinates can create conflicts of interest, erode governance, and expose organizations to reputational and even national security risk when personal relationships intersect with authority structures.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat executive-subordinate relationships as a governance risk, not just a personal matter—robust conflict-of-interest policies, clear reporting lines for concerns, and proactive succession planning are essential to limit organizational exposure when leadership misconduct surfaces.
