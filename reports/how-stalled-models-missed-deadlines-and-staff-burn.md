## Summary
Google DeepMind is experiencing internal turmoil characterized by stalled AI model development, missed deadlines, and widespread staff burnout, with tensions simmering among current and former employees for months.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story illustrates the organizational consequences of unsustainable performance pressure in high-stakes R&D environments, where chronic overwork, unclear progress milestones, and mounting expectations can erode morale and precipitate talent attrition. The simmering tensions suggest failures in psychological safety and leadership communication during periods of strategic uncertainty.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders managing high-pressure innovation teams must proactively monitor burnout signals, set realistic milestones, and maintain transparent communication about strategic direction—failure to do so risks losing top talent and compounding delivery failures. CHROs should implement structured well-being checkpoints and ensure middle managers are equipped to surface team distress before it becomes systemic.
