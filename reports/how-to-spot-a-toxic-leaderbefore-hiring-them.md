# How to Spot a Toxic Leader—Before Hiring Them

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-24T12:05:25Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/how-to-spot-a-toxic-leader-before-hiring-them](https://hbr.org/2026/08/how-to-spot-a-toxic-leader-before-hiring-them)

## Summary
HBR outlines six steps for hiring managers to identify toxic leadership traits before making a hire: (1) Define what 'toxic' means for your organization, (2) Use structured behavioral interviews, (3) Ask about past failures and conflict, (4) Check references with pointed questions, (5) Use validated psychometric assessments, and (6) Involve multiple evaluators in the process.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Toxic leadership is a well-documented construct in organizational psychology linked to increased turnover, reduced psychological safety, and organizational dysfunction. Pre-hire screening using structured interviews and psychometric tools aligns with decades of personnel selection research showing these methods outperform unstructured approaches in predicting counterproductive work behaviors.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should institutionalize structured, multi-rater hiring processes with validated assessments specifically designed to surface dark-side personality traits (e.g., narcissism, Machiavellianism) before toxic leaders enter the organization—prevention is far less costly than remediation.
