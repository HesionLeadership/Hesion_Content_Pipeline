# The Real Reasons Why Digital Projects Fail

## Summary
The article argues that digital project failures are often misdiagnosed as user resistance or poor training, when the real causes are distinct problems with data quality and system usability. Research on 564 physicians' use of electronic health records found these two factors affect cognitive load through separate mechanisms, requiring separate diagnosis and investment.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights attribution error in organizational diagnosis—leaders default to blaming 'user resistance to change' or poor adoption rather than conducting rigorous root-cause analysis, leading to misdirected corrective investments and recurring failure cycles.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should resist the reflex to label failed technology adoption as a change-management or training problem; instead, they should build diagnostic frameworks that separate data quality issues from system design issues before investing in fixes, since conflating the two leads to wasted remediation spend and repeated failures.
