## Summary
Six top business leaders share what they believe makes someone stand out at work. Key advice includes being curious and asking questions, showing resilience and adaptability, taking initiative beyond your role, demonstrating strong communication skills, building genuine relationships, and maintaining a consistent work ethic.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The advice from these leaders maps onto well-studied constructs in organizational psychology including proactive behavior, organizational citizenship behavior (OCB), and growth mindset. The emphasis on curiosity and initiative reflects research on proactive personality as a predictor of career success.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design talent identification and development systems that reward proactive behaviors, curiosity, and organizational citizenship—not just task performance. Selection and promotion criteria should explicitly include these differentiators rather than relying on traditional performance metrics alone.
