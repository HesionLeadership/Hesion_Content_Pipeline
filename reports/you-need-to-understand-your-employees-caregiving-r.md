# You Need to Understand Your Employees’ Caregiving Responsibilities

**Source:** HBR

## Summary
The article argues that managers and organizations need to understand employees' caregiving responsibilities—such as caring for children, aging parents, or family members with disabilities—because awareness leads to improved well-being, productivity, commitment, retention, and more meaningful family lives.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Work-family conflict and role overload are well-established constructs in organizational psychology; when organizations acknowledge and accommodate caregiving demands, employees experience reduced strain and greater organizational commitment, consistent with conservation of resources theory and perceived organizational support frameworks.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should implement systematic approaches to understand employees' caregiving obligations—through surveys, manager training, and flexible policies—because proactively supporting the whole employee reduces turnover, boosts engagement, and signals genuine organizational support that drives reciprocal commitment.
