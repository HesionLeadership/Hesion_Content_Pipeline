## Summary
Following the departure of founder/CEO Jensen Huang, Nvidia reported record quarterly revenue of $115.8 billion (up 103% year-over-year) and raised its full-year outlook, with interim CEO Colette Kress reassuring investors that business remains strong and customer/supplier relationships are intact.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how organizations manage leadership succession shocks—particularly abrupt, scandal-driven departures—by emphasizing institutional strength, stakeholder reassurance, and continuity messaging to maintain confidence among employees, investors, and partners.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should prepare succession communication plans in advance so that during sudden leadership crises, messaging can quickly emphasize organizational stability, existing talent depth, and operational continuity rather than relying solely on the departed leader's personal brand.
