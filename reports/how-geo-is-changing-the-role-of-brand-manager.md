# How GEO Is Changing the Role of Brand Manager

## Summary
As AI-powered generative engine optimization (GEO) reshapes how consumers discover and evaluate brands, the role of brand managers must evolve from optimizing for human attention and search engines to ensuring brands are accurately represented in AI-generated recommendations and summaries.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This shift requires brand managers to develop new mental models and competencies, representing a significant role transition that challenges professional identity and demands organizational learning at scale. It also raises questions about how marketing teams restructure authority and expertise when AI intermediates customer relationships.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively assess how AI disruption is redefining core roles within their organizations and invest in reskilling and role redesign before capability gaps emerge—treating AI-driven role evolution as a change management challenge, not just a technical one.
