# Could You Benefit from a Digital Twin?

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-09-04T12:15:23Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/09/could-you-benefit-from-a-digital-twin](https://hbr.org/2026/09/could-you-benefit-from-a-digital-twin)

## Summary
Digital twins—AI-generated clones of individuals—are moving beyond novelty in workplaces, being used to replicate employees' communication styles, knowledge, and decision-making patterns for tasks like meetings, training, and stakeholder engagement.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Digital twins raise fundamental questions about identity, trust, and authenticity in workplace relationships. Delegating interpersonal interactions to an AI clone may erode psychological safety and relational trust if colleagues feel they are not engaging with the real person.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders considering digital twin adoption should carefully evaluate which tasks benefit from AI delegation versus those that require genuine human presence. CHROs should establish clear norms around transparency—employees and stakeholders must know when they are interacting with a digital twin versus a real person to maintain trust and organizational integrity.
