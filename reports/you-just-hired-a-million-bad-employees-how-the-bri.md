# ‘You just hired a million bad employees’: How the brief tokenmaxxing era delivered the opposite of what it promised

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Fri, 24 Jul 2026 07:00:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/07/24/how-tokenmaxxing-era-delivered-the-opposite-promised/](https://fortune.com/2026/07/24/how-tokenmaxxing-era-delivered-the-opposite-promised/)

## Summary
Companies rapidly adopted AI token-based tools (tokenmaxxing) with spending skyrocketing from $20k to $1M monthly, but the unchecked deployment delivered poor results akin to hiring a million bad employees—generating volume without quality or strategic alignment.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The rush to deploy AI tools without governance mirrors classic organizational adoption failures where technology is treated as a substitute for human judgment rather than a complement, creating diffusion of responsibility and eroding quality standards across workflows.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat AI tool adoption like any workforce expansion—with clear role definition, performance metrics, and accountability structures. Unchecked enthusiasm for new technology without governance frameworks leads to waste and degraded output quality, just as rapid unstructured hiring does.
