# The Nixpkgs core team has disbanded

**Source:** Hacker News  
**Published:** Sat, 08 Aug 2026 01:12:45 +0000  
**URL:** [https://discourse.nixos.org/t/the-nixpkgs-core-team-has-disbanded/79413](https://discourse.nixos.org/t/the-nixpkgs-core-team-has-disbanded/79413)

## Summary
The Nixpkgs core team, responsible for governing the Nix package manager ecosystem, has officially disbanded. This follows extended internal governance conflicts and community tensions within the open-source project.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This represents a case study in governance failure within a volunteer-driven open-source organization, where unclear authority structures, role ambiguity, and unresolved interpersonal conflicts can lead to complete dissolution of leadership bodies rather than reform.

## Leadership Lesson
Volunteer and decentralized organizations need explicit governance charters, conflict resolution mechanisms, and succession planning. When leadership teams lack formal authority, clear decision-making processes, and psychological safety to address internal tensions, the result is often collapse rather than adaptation. CHROs should recognize that distributed authority models require even more—not less—structural scaffolding.
