# Netflix has dipped its toe into theatrical. Is it ready to jump in?

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Wed, 21 Oct 2026 18:32:12 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/netflix-theatrical-strategy.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/21/netflix-theatrical-strategy.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Netflix is expanding theatrical releases to 30 films in 2027 despite its streaming-first stance, while restructuring its C-suite by ousting content chief Bela Bajaria and film head Dan Lin in favor of former Disney executive Peter Rice, as it grapples with stagnant viewership and recent high-profile talent departures.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how organizational identity and strategic rigidity (streaming-only) can drive talent attrition when creative professionals value external validation and career capital—like theatrical releases—that the organization's stated values don't support.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that retaining top creative/technical talent sometimes requires loosening core strategic doctrine when it conflicts with what high performers need for career advancement and professional identity, even if that means incremental strategic drift from founding principles.
