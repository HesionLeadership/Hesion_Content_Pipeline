# Sesame Workshop CEO says you’ll never see an AI-powered Elmo toy: ‘We could have made a lot of money … but we’re just not going to do that’

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Tue, 17 Nov 2026 16:26:10 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/17/sesame-workshop-ceo-never-see-ai-powered-elmo-toy/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/17/sesame-workshop-ceo-never-see-ai-powered-elmo-toy/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Sesame Workshop CEO Sherrie Westin announced the nonprofit will not create AI-powered anthropomorphic toys (like Elmo) despite lucrative offers, citing concerns that such toys could interfere with the crucial connection between children and caregivers. The company uses AI selectively elsewhere, guided by its 'AI ABCs' framework emphasizing age-appropriate content, real learning, accessibility, personalization balance, and complementing (not replacing) caregiver relationships.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates values-based decision-making where leadership explicitly prioritizes mission/ethical guardrails over revenue opportunities, demonstrating how organizational identity and stated values can constrain strategic choices even under competitive and financial pressure.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs can learn that establishing clear ethical frameworks (like the 'AI ABCs') before pursuing new technology adoption helps leaders make consistent, defensible decisions when faced with lucrative but value-misaligned opportunities, protecting organizational identity and stakeholder trust.
