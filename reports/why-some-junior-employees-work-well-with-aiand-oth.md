## Summary
New HBR research explores why some junior employees effectively collaborate with AI tools while others struggle, highlighting individual differences in how entry-level workers adopt and leverage AI in their workflows.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Individual differences in technology adoption among junior employees likely reflect variations in learning orientation, self-efficacy, and cognitive flexibility—key constructs that predict how newcomers adapt to novel workplace tools and whether AI augments or undermines their skill development.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should avoid assuming AI tools will uniformly benefit all entry-level employees; instead, invest in differentiated onboarding and training that accounts for individual readiness, mindset, and prior experience, and create psychological safety for juniors to experiment with AI without fear of appearing incompetent.
