# Nvidia says it won't reinstate Huang as it reports soaring sales

## Summary
Nvidia's board has ruled out reinstating ousted co-founder Jensen Huang despite his request to return, even as the company reported sales doubling to $115.8bn. Interim CEO Colette Kress emphasized business continuity and leadership team stability, while the company conducts a search for a permanent replacement.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the tension between founder-dependency myths and organizational resilience—strong post-departure performance challenges the assumption that iconic leaders are irreplaceable, while also testing whether institutional systems (teams, processes, partnerships) can sustain momentum independent of a single charismatic figure.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should invest in distributed leadership capacity and succession planning well before a crisis hits; strong immediate post-departure performance often reflects pre-existing organizational depth and momentum rather than guaranteeing long-term strategic continuity, especially for future decisions requiring vision and judgment.
