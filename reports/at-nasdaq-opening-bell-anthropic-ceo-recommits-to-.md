## Summary
At Anthropic's Nasdaq IPO debut, CEO Dario Amodei publicly recommitted to pacing AI development responsibly, pledging the company will not bend to business or political pressure and will keep AI 'under human control.' Co-founder Daniela Amodei echoed this commitment to safety in a CNBC interview.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a case study in how leaders use high-visibility public moments (like an IPO) to make binding public commitments to organizational values, which can function as a psychological accountability mechanism against future value drift.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that public, specific commitments made during moments of success or scrutiny (IPOs, major funding rounds) can be leveraged intentionally to lock in espoused values and create external accountability—but leaders must also build internal structures (metrics, third-party audits, governance) to ensure these commitments survive contact with growth pressures.
