## Summary
The article argues that successful corporate reinvention (e.g., Ørsted's shift from fossil fuels to offshore wind, Microsoft's AI pivot) comes not from starting fresh or clinging to the core business, but from identifying and repurposing existing 'kernels of reinvention'—embedded organizational capabilities that can anchor new strategic directions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The piece touches on organizational identity and capability transfer during change—how leaders can reframe existing competencies and internal narratives to reduce resistance and stakeholder alienation during major strategic pivots.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit existing organizational capabilities, talent, and institutional knowledge before launching transformation initiatives, using these as psychological and operational anchors to build internal buy-in rather than triggering identity threat through wholesale reinvention.
