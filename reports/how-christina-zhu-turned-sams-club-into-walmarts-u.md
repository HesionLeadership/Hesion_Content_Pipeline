# How Christina Zhu turned Sam’s Club into Walmart’s unlikely growth engine in China

**Published:** Mon, 05 Oct 2026 20:22:32 +0000

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Christina Zhu, Walmart China's CEO since 2020, has driven Sam's Club to become 70% of Walmart's China revenue by combining a digital-first delivery model (30-minute grocery delivery) with an in-person warehouse shopping experience that has become a regional retail destination, even as China's broader consumer market slumps and other foreign brands struggle.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how leaders can navigate organizational identity tension—preserving a parent company's core standards (American-style service, quality control) while deeply localizing operations (portion sizes, delivery speed expectations, marketing)—to sustain legitimacy and competitive advantage in a foreign market.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs leading multinational operations should resist one-size-fits-all playbooks: empower local leadership with authority to redefine operational benchmarks (e.g., what 'convenience' means) while preserving non-negotiable brand/quality standards, since this dual fidelity—global consistency plus local responsiveness—can be a stronger differentiator than either extreme.
