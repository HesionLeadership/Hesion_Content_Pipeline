## Summary
Mattel named board member and current Condé Nast CEO Roger Lynch as its new CEO and chairman, succeeding Ynon Kreiz who departs after eight years to lead another public company.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This exemplifies board-insider succession planning, where a known quantity with institutional knowledge (via board tenure) is selected over an external unknown, reducing transition risk but raising questions about fresh perspective and change capacity.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and boards should weigh the tradeoffs of insider-board-member successors: while they offer continuity, cultural fit, and reduced onboarding risk, organizations must ensure succession planning still tests candidates against emerging strategic needs (e.g., digital transformation) rather than defaulting to familiarity.
