# Coffee giant Dutch Bros made all of its executives, including the CEO, do a two-week bootcamp where they learned how to pour drinks and run stores

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Dutch Bros requires all executives, including CEO Christine Barone, to complete a two-week 'broista' bootcamp learning to make drinks and run stores, as part of a broader 'growth from within' culture where leaders—including HQ marketing staff—gain frontline experience to maintain consistency and empathy as the company scales to 1,273 locations.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how shared frontline experience across hierarchical levels builds empathy, reduces status distance, and reinforces a strong, consistent organizational culture even as the company scales rapidly.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider requiring leaders at all levels—including those in corporate functions far from operations—to periodically experience frontline work, as this builds empathy, credibility, and alignment between policy decisions and ground-level realities, while also signaling a culture where feedback flows upward regardless of rank.
