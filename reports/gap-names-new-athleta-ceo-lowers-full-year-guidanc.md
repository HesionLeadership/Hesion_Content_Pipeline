# Gap names new Athleta CEO, lowers full-year guidance

**Published:** Thu, 19 Nov 2026 23:54:20 GMT

## Summary
Gap Inc. named Ashley Kechter (previously at Vuori, Fabletics, and originally Gap) as Athleta's new CEO following a 15% drop in comparable sales and the departure of former CEO Maggie Gauger to Lululemon. Gap also lowered full-year guidance despite strong performance at its namesake Gap brand (12 consecutive quarters of growth) while Athleta and Banana Republic struggled.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how organizational performance divergence across brand units within the same parent company often traces back to leadership consistency and execution discipline—Gap's cited '12 consecutive quarters' of growth versus Athleta's 'limited traction' and leadership churn highlights how stable leadership and strategic focus correlate with sustained performance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs/COOs should treat underperforming business units as diagnostic cases for leadership stability and strategic clarity—frequent CEO turnover (as at Athleta) often signals unresolved strategic drift, and bringing in 'boomerang' leaders with sector-specific expertise (Kechter's Fabletics/Vuori background) is a common turnaround tactic, but success depends on whether the new leader is empowered to make structural changes, not just conduct another review.
