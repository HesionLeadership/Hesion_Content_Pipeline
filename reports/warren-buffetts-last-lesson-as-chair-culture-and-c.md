# Warren Buffett’s last lesson as chair: Culture and capital require different guardians

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Mon, 21 Sep 2026 12:40:13 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/09/21/warren-buffett-last-lesson-as-chair-culture-capital-require-different-guardians-cfo/](https://fortune.com/2026/09/21/warren-buffett-last-lesson-as-chair-culture-capital-require-different-guardians-cfo/)

## Summary
Warren Buffett's final act as Berkshire Hathaway chair involves deliberately separating operating authority (given to new CEO Greg Abel) from cultural stewardship (entrusted to his son Howard Buffett as non-executive chair), recognizing that the skills needed to run a business differ from those needed to preserve its values and culture.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This succession design reflects the organizational psychology principle that organizational culture is a distinct construct from operational competence, and that preserving founder-era culture during leadership transitions requires intentional structural safeguards rather than assuming a single successor can embody both functions.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider that succession planning may require disaggregating roles rather than seeking a single 'replacement'—separating operational decision-making authority from cultural guardianship can protect organizational identity during transitions, especially in founder-led or values-driven organizations.
