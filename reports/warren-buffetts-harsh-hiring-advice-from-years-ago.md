## Summary
Warren Buffett's longstanding hiring advice—that integrity matters more than intelligence or energy—is revisited in the context of AI-powered workplaces where talented but unethical employees can cause amplified harm.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights the well-established principle that character and integrity are critical selection criteria, and that cognitive ability without moral character can be counterproductive, especially as technology amplifies individual impact within organizations.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should prioritize integrity-based assessments in hiring processes alongside competence measures, recognizing that in AI-augmented environments, the potential damage from high-ability, low-integrity employees scales dramatically. Invest in validated integrity testing, reference checks for ethical behavior, and cultural onboarding that reinforces organizational values.
