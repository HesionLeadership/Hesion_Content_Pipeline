# Stop Building Fences to Keep Your Employees

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Tue, 15 Sep 2026 08:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/chris-sund/stop-building-fences-to-keep-your-employees/91404078](https://www.inc.com/chris-sund/stop-building-fences-to-keep-your-employees/91404078)

## Summary
The article argues that leaders should stop building restrictive 'fences' (such as non-competes, golden handcuffs, or controlling policies) to retain top talent, and instead create environments where employees genuinely want to stay through purpose, growth, and trust.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses self-determination theory—when organizations rely on extrinsic constraints rather than fostering intrinsic motivation through autonomy, competence, and relatedness, they paradoxically increase turnover intention and reduce engagement.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit their retention strategies to distinguish between coercive retention mechanisms (restrictive covenants, deferred compensation traps) and genuine engagement drivers (meaningful work, career development, psychological safety). Retention built on barriers rather than belonging is fragile and breeds resentment.
