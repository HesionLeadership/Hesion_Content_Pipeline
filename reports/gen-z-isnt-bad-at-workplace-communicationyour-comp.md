# Gen Z isn’t bad at workplace communication—your company is bad at explaining its rules

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Mon, 28 Sep 2026 15:34:42 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/09/28/gen-z-workplace-communication-rules/](https://fortune.com/2026/09/28/gen-z-workplace-communication-rules/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
The article argues that generational conflicts at work, such as texting versus calling in about lateness or differing comfort with disclosing mental health struggles, stem not from Gen Z having worse communication skills but from unstated and mismatched assumptions about workplace norms, often shaped by pandemic-era remote work and reduced in-person socialization.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The piece illustrates attribution bias and implicit norm mismatch in intergenerational teams: both parties assume their communication style is 'obviously' correct or respectful, when in fact workplace norms are learned contextually rather than universally understood.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat communication norms (channels, deadlines, disclosure boundaries) as explicit policy to be trained and stated, not assumed as common sense, especially when onboarding generationally diverse or hybrid/remote workforces.
