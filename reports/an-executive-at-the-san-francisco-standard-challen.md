# An Executive at The San Francisco Standard Challenged an Alleged Double Standard. She Was Fired 5 Days Later

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Mon, 21 Sep 2026 08:48:38 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/kevin-haynes/executive-san-francisco-standard-challenged-alleged-double-standard-fired-5-days-later/91408245](https://www.inc.com/kevin-haynes/executive-san-francisco-standard-challenged-alleged-double-standard-fired-5-days-later/91408245)

## Summary
A female executive at The San Francisco Standard challenged what she perceived as a double standard in how the organization treated misconduct allegations against male versus female employees. She was fired five days after raising the concern and subsequently filed a lawsuit against the company and its CEO.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case illustrates the risks of retaliatory termination following internal dissent, highlighting failures in procedural justice and psychological safety. When employees who raise concerns about inequitable treatment are punished rather than heard, it signals a culture that suppresses voice and tolerates double standards.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs must ensure robust protections for employees who raise concerns about inconsistent policy enforcement—especially around discipline and misconduct. Retaliatory responses to internal challenges not only expose the organization to legal liability but destroy trust, chill future reporting, and signal that equity commitments are performative.
