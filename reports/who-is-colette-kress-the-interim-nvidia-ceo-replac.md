## Summary
Colette Kress, Nvidia's longtime CFO since 2013, has been appointed interim CEO by the board, replacing Jensen Huang. Kress will retain her CFO duties while serving as interim chief executive, having built deep trust with Huang over her tenure, including managing financial communications with Wall Street and navigating China-related sales restrictions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story illustrates the common succession pattern of promoting a trusted, long-tenured insider (especially a CFO) to interim CEO during leadership transitions, leveraging existing relational trust and institutional knowledge rather than risking instability with an external hire.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that succession planning benefits from cultivating deep bench strength among C-suite executives (especially CFOs) who have earned trust and demonstrated loyalty, as this reduces transition risk and maintains investor/employee confidence during leadership changes.
