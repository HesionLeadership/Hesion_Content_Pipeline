## Summary
Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang stepped down amid scandals involving affairs with employees and alleged national security concerns; OpenAI's Sam Altman and Greg Brockman spoke publicly about their personal relationship with Huang and expressed support, while Nvidia's board declined to reinstate him despite a 200,000-signature petition and his offer to return.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates the tension between charismatic/founder leadership and governance accountability—organizations must weigh public and peer loyalty to a high-status leader against misconduct that violates fiduciary and ethical boundaries.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure governance structures are resilient to founder-dependency and halo effects, so that misconduct investigations and succession decisions are insulated from personal loyalty, public pressure, or sunk-cost attachment to a charismatic leader.
