# Satlyt, founded by a former Google and SpaceX product manager, raises $8M to run AI on satellites

## Summary
Rama Afullo, a former Google and SpaceX engineer, raised $8M to found Satlyt, a startup building software that lets satellites run AI workloads and share computing resources in orbit, after both Google and SpaceX rejected his internal pitches for the idea.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates a common organizational pattern where large, established companies reject novel internal ideas that later prove viable externally, highlighting the gap between innovation sourcing and and organizational structures ill-suited to incubate speculative, non-core business bets.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should examine whether internal innovation pipelines (idea review boards, pitch processes) are structured to genuinely evaluate speculative ideas, or whether risk-aversion and short-term incentives cause talented employees to take validated ideas elsewhere, taking value and future competitive advantage with them.
