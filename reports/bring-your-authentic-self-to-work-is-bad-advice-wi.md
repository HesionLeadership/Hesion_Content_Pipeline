# ‘Bring Your Authentic Self to Work’ Is Bad Advice Without This 4-Part Leadership Test

## Summary
Brittany N. Cole, after experiencing grief while starting a new role at Pfizer, developed a 4-part leadership test for authentic self-expression at work. The framework helps leaders and employees decide what aspects of their personal lives to share in professional settings, moving beyond the vague 'bring your authentic self to work' mantra to a more intentional approach to workplace vulnerability.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses the tension between authentic self-expression and professional boundaries—a core challenge in psychological safety research. The framework operationalizes authenticity, which research shows can enhance trust and engagement but also carries risks when organizational culture doesn't support vulnerability.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that 'bring your authentic self to work' without structural support is an empty platitude. Leaders need frameworks and cultural conditions that help employees gauge when and how to share personal experiences—creating genuine psychological safety rather than performative openness that can backfire.
