# Eric Trump-backed robot soldier startup can't pay staff after founder borrowed $4.6 million from company to buy home

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Sat, 14 Nov 2026 12:00:01 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/foundation-payroll-ceo-loan.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/14/foundation-payroll-ceo-loan.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Foundation Future Industries, a military robotics startup backed by Eric Trump, missed payroll in October 2025 after its sole board member and CEO, Sankaet Pathak, had borrowed $4.6 million from the company to buy a luxury home. Pathak's previous fintech startup, Synapse, also collapsed in 2024, leaving thousands of customers locked out of $265 million in deposits.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates how concentrated, unchecked founder power (sole board member, majority shareholder) enables self-dealing and governance failures that directly harm employee trust and organizational stability, especially when a leader has a documented pattern of similar conduct at a prior company.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat founder-controlled governance structures (single board member, no independent oversight) as a major red flag, and should push for related-party transaction controls, independent board seats, and financial transparency before cash crises erode employee trust and retention.
