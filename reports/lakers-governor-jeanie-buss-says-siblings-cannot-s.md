# Lakers governor Jeanie Buss says siblings cannot sell family’s stake to Bob Iger, Joshua Kushner

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Mon, 17 Aug 2026 23:47:54 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/jeanie-buss-opposes-sale-family-stake.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/17/jeanie-buss-opposes-sale-family-stake.html)

## Summary
Jeanie Buss, controlling owner and governor of the Los Angeles Lakers, is blocking her siblings from selling the family's stake in the team to potential buyers Bob Iger and Joshua Kushner, with her lawyer declaring any such vote void.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a classic family business governance conflict where ownership control, decision rights, and sibling power dynamics collide. The dispute highlights how ambiguous or contested governance structures in family-owned enterprises can lead to intra-family coalitions and legal battles over strategic direction.

## Leadership Lesson
Family-owned organizations must establish crystal-clear governance charters that define decision-making authority, voting rights, and succession protocols—especially when multiple family members hold ownership stakes. CHROs and COOs in family enterprises should advocate for formal governance frameworks that prevent factional disputes from destabilizing the organization.
