## Summary
Abu Dhabi is investing $3.5 billion to become an 'AI-native' government, using its TAMM app and AutoGov system to automate routine citizen services like license renewals and health appointments, while training 20,000 government workers in AI use by year-end.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights how high institutional trust and pre-aligned cross-departmental goal-setting (agreeing on objectives before meetings even start) enable large-scale organizational change that would otherwise stall in siloed bureaucracies.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should prioritize building a track record of credible delivery before attempting major digital transformation, and structure cross-functional collaboration so that shared goals are established prior to meetings rather than negotiated during them.
