## Summary
New research identifies three critical workplace metrics that CEOs and CHROs should prioritize: manager effectiveness, employee burnout levels, and trust in AI. The findings highlight growing leadership gaps as organizations struggle with middle-management quality, widespread burnout, and employee skepticism toward AI adoption.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story directly addresses core organizational psychology constructs—manager effectiveness as a predictor of engagement, burnout as a function of job demands-resources imbalance, and trust as a moderator of technology adoption. These three metrics reflect well-established research streams in occupational health psychology and organizational behavior.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build measurement systems around manager effectiveness (not just engagement scores), proactively monitor burnout indicators before they manifest as turnover, and address AI trust deficits through transparency and participative change management rather than top-down mandates.
