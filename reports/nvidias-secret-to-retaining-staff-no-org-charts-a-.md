# Nvidia’s secret to retaining staff? No org charts, a flat structure, and two extra days off every quarter

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Thu, 08 Oct 2026 04:44:38 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/10/08/nvidia-secret-to-retaining-staff-no-org-charts-a-flat-structure-two-extra-days-off-every-quarter/](https://fortune.com/2026/10/08/nvidia-secret-to-retaining-staff-no-org-charts-a-flat-structure-two-extra-days-off-every-quarter/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Nvidia maintains a flat organizational structure with no formal org charts, encourages employees to voice suggestions directly regardless of seniority, and provides quarterly company-wide 'free days' off—policies that HR director Febe Punsalan credits with producing a 3.7% global turnover rate versus the tech industry's 21.9% average.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how flat hierarchies, direct voice mechanisms (suggestion systems with guaranteed responses), and listening-based policy design can foster psychological safety and organizational commitment, even within a high-pressure, high-performance culture.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs can reduce turnover and boost engagement by creating structured channels for employee voice (not just symbolic ones) and translating listening into tangible policy changes—while recognizing that flat structures only work if leadership genuinely decentralizes decision-making rather than just removing titles.
