# What to Consider Before Giving Advice on a Global Team

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-03T12:25:15Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/what-to-consider-before-giving-advice-on-a-global-team](https://hbr.org/2026/08/what-to-consider-before-giving-advice-on-a-global-team)

## Summary
Research on communication styles among American, Chinese, and Indian employees reveals that the same advice or guidance can be interpreted in dramatically different ways across cultures, leading to erosion of trust. The study highlights how cultural norms around directness, hierarchy, and feedback shape how advice is given, received, and acted upon in global teams.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses cross-cultural communication and trust dynamics in multinational teams—a core area of organizational psychology. Understanding how cultural schemas shape the interpretation of workplace guidance is critical for avoiding misattribution of intent and maintaining psychological safety across diverse teams.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders managing global teams should avoid assuming universal communication norms when providing feedback or advice. CHROs and COOs should invest in cross-cultural competency training and establish team-level norms that explicitly account for different cultural interpretations of directness, deference, and advisory intent to preserve trust and collaboration.
