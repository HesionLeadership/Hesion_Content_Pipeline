## Summary
A University of Kentucky administrator secured a religious/philosophical exemption from using AI at work based on secular humanist beliefs, becoming one of the first people to publicly use this legal strategy as AI adoption mandates spread through workplaces.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates how top-down technology mandates can clash with individual values and identity, and how organizations must navigate accommodation requests that blend legal compliance, employee autonomy, and change management around new technology adoption.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should anticipate that AI rollout mandates will trigger values-based resistance beyond mere skill gaps, and should build flexible, case-by-case accommodation frameworks now rather than reactively, to preserve both legal defensibility and employee trust during tech transitions.
