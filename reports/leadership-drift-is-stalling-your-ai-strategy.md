# “Leadership Drift” Is Stalling Your AI Strategy

**Source:** HBR

## Summary
HBR research identifies 'leadership drift'—the unintentional, collective inertia that prevents leadership teams from making timely decisions about AI strategy. The concept describes how leadership teams collectively stall without any individual intending to delay, resulting in organizational paralysis on critical strategic initiatives.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Leadership drift reflects well-documented phenomena in group decision-making including diffusion of responsibility, pluralistic ignorance, and status quo bias, where no single leader feels accountable for driving action, and each assumes others share their hesitation or will take the lead.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should actively diagnose collective inertia in their leadership teams by assigning clear decision ownership, setting explicit timelines for strategic AI decisions, and creating accountability mechanisms that counteract the natural tendency for groups to defer difficult, ambiguous choices.
