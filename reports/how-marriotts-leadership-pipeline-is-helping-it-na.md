## Summary
Marriott International is addressing Europe's hospitality talent shortage through internal leadership development programs like 'Elevate' and 'Voyage,' promoting from within and retraining refugees to build its talent pipeline.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how structured internal mobility, mentorship, and skill-building programs can increase perceived organizational support and procedural justice, which in turn drive retention and promotion outcomes.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs facing talent shortages should invest in formalized internal career pathways and coaching programs rather than relying solely on external hiring, since structured development programs (with measurable retention and promotion multipliers) can convert frontline talent into future leaders and reduce turnover costs.
