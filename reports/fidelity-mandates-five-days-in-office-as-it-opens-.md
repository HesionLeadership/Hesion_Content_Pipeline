# Fidelity Mandates Five Days in Office as it Opens New Boston Home

**Source:** Bloomberg  
**Published:** Fri, 04 Sep 2026 12:00:07 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-04/fidelity-mandates-five-days-in-office-as-it-opens-new-boston-home](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-09-04/fidelity-mandates-five-days-in-office-as-it-opens-new-boston-home)

## Summary
Fidelity Investments is opening a new 735,000-square-foot headquarters in Boston with premium amenities including harbor views, a private bar, and an in-office concierge, while simultaneously mandating all Boston employees return to the office five days a week, ending its hybrid work arrangement.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Fidelity's strategy of pairing a full return-to-office mandate with lavish workplace amenities reflects a 'sweetening the deal' approach—using environmental enrichment to offset the autonomy loss employees experience when flexible work is revoked. This raises questions about whether extrinsic perks can compensate for the intrinsic motivation derived from work autonomy.

## Leadership Lesson
When revoking flexible work arrangements, leaders should recognize that amenities alone may not offset perceived losses in autonomy. CHROs should proactively address the psychological contract breach employees may feel, combining environmental upgrades with transparent communication about the strategic rationale and genuine efforts to preserve elements of employee control and well-being.
