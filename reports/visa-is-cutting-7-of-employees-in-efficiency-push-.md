# Visa is cutting 7% of employees in efficiency push as AI reshapes work

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Tue, 28 Jul 2026 15:35:48 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/visa-is-cutting-7percent-of-employees-in-efficiency-push-as-ai-reshapes-work.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/visa-is-cutting-7percent-of-employees-in-efficiency-push-as-ai-reshapes-work.html)

## Summary
Visa is cutting approximately 2,600 employees (7% of its workforce) as CEO Ryan McInerney pursues operational efficiency, with AI reshaping how work is done at the payments giant.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Large-scale AI-driven restructuring raises critical questions about survivor syndrome, employee trust erosion, and how organizations frame technological displacement as 'efficiency' rather than addressing the psychological contract violations experienced by both departing and remaining employees.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs leading AI-driven workforce reductions must proactively address survivor guilt and uncertainty among remaining employees through transparent communication about how roles will evolve, not just which roles will be eliminated. Framing layoffs purely as 'efficiency' without articulating a clear people strategy risks undermining the engagement and innovation capacity needed to actually realize AI's potential.
