## This Week's Themes
Two dominant threads today: the psychology of leaders under pressure, and the hidden fragility inside "well-functioning" organizations. Meanwhile, stories 4, 6, 7, and 10 surface a quieter theme: organizations are building invisible risk into their systems — through AI shortcuts, concentrated knowledge, unresolved leadership friction, and murky accountability — that won't show up until it's expensive to fix. If there's an emerging idea worth naming, it's this: organizational resilience is being redefined as a psychological and structural design problem, not an efficiency problem.
