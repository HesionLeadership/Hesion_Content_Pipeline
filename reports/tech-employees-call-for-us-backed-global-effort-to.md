## Summary
Tech employees are calling for a US-backed global initiative to manage the risks associated with advanced artificial intelligence, signaling growing internal concern within the tech industry about AI safety governance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects employee voice and upward influence dynamics, where technical workers leverage collective action to push organizational and governmental leaders toward responsible innovation governance. It highlights the tension between rapid technological advancement and employee-driven demands for ethical guardrails.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should create structured channels for employee voice on ethical and safety concerns related to emerging technologies; failing to do so risks losing talent, facing public pressure campaigns, and missing critical risk signals from those closest to the work.
