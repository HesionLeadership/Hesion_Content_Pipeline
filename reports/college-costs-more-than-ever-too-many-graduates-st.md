# College Costs More Than Ever. Too Many Graduates Still Arrive at Work Missing the Basics

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Tue, 01 Sep 2026 06:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/howard-tullman/college-costs-new-graduates-entry-level-job-training-skills-ai/91398426](https://www.inc.com/howard-tullman/college-costs-new-graduates-entry-level-job-training-skills-ai/91398426)

## Summary
Employers report that despite rising college costs, many new graduates arrive lacking basic workplace skills such as communication, critical thinking, and professionalism, forcing companies to invest heavily in remedial training.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the growing gap between educational credentialing and actual job readiness, raising questions about onboarding design, training investment ROI, and whether organizations need to fundamentally restructure how they develop entry-level talent rather than relying on educational institutions as proxies for competence.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit their onboarding programs to explicitly address foundational professional skills rather than assuming new hires possess them. Building structured developmental onboarding—covering communication, problem-solving, and workplace norms—can accelerate time-to-productivity and reduce costly turnover among early-career employees.
