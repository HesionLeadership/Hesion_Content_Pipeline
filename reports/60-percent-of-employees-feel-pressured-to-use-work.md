# 60 Percent of Employees Feel Pressured to Use Workplace Bots. The Errors Are Piling Up

## Summary
A survey reveals that 60% of employees feel pressured to use workplace automation and AI tools, leading to skipped accuracy checks, concealed errors, and weakened accountability. The rush to adopt bots without proper training or psychological safety is creating a culture where mistakes compound silently.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story highlights the tension between technology adoption pressure and error management culture. When organizations mandate tool use without fostering psychological safety, employees engage in error concealment rather than transparent correction—a classic breakdown in organizational learning.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure that automation rollouts are paired with explicit permission to question tool outputs, training on verification processes, and a blame-free culture around AI-generated errors. Pressuring adoption without building accountability structures creates hidden risk accumulation that can be far more costly than slower adoption.
