# Want Projects Done on Time? Remove These 2 Words From Your Messages

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Thu, 13 Aug 2026 15:45:31 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/want-projects-done-on-time-remove-these-2-words-from-your-messages/91390405](https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/want-projects-done-on-time-remove-these-2-words-from-your-messages/91390405)

## Summary
New research suggests that removing the words 'as soon as possible' (ASAP) from project communications and replacing them with specific deadlines significantly reduces late delivery rates. The data shows that vague urgency language leads to ambiguity, deprioritization, and ultimately missed deadlines, while concrete timelines improve accountability and on-time completion.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This connects to goal-setting theory (Locke & Latham), which demonstrates that specific, measurable goals consistently outperform vague directives in driving performance. The ambiguity of 'ASAP' creates diffusion of responsibility and allows individuals to subjectively interpret priority, undermining task completion.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit organizational communication norms around task assignment—replacing vague urgency cues like 'ASAP' with explicit deadlines, clear owners, and defined deliverables. Training managers in precise communication can meaningfully reduce project delays without adding process overhead.
