## Summary
Nvidia named longtime CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO following Jensen Huang's resignation; Kress guided the company's finances through its transformation into a $4 trillion AI chipmaking giant and has emphasized curiosity-driven learning and surrounding herself with talented people as core leadership traits.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights internal succession planning (promoting a tenured, trusted insider) and a leadership philosophy rooted in learning orientation and intellectual curiosity rather than overt ambition for titles.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize the value of developing deep bench strength through long-tenured executives who understand the business from multiple angles, and cultivate a 'learning culture' where curiosity and willingness to be taught by subordinates are seen as leadership assets, not weaknesses.
