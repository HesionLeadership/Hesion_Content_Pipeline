## Summary
A hedge-fund manager has built his firm to be powered entirely by AI agents, replacing traditional human roles in finance operations. This represents a growing trend on Wall Street as firms explore the benefits and limits of AI in finance and its impact on employees.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story raises critical questions about human-AI collaboration, workforce displacement, and how organizational identity and culture shift when AI agents replace human workers. It challenges fundamental assumptions about what constitutes an 'organization' when the workforce is largely non-human.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively design frameworks for human-AI workforce integration, including redefining roles, managing employee anxiety about displacement, and establishing governance structures for AI-driven decision-making before full automation becomes the default.
