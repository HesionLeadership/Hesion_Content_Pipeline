## Summary
The CEO of ConnectOne Bank argues that leaders facing economic uncertainty should offer 'clarity' rather than 'certainty,' drawing a parallel to Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's communication style of sharing frameworks and reasoning instead of specific forecasts.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The piece touches on leadership communication under uncertainty, specifically the psychological distinction between reducing ambiguity (clarity on decision-making frameworks) versus falsely eliminating risk (certainty about outcomes), which relates to sensemaking theory in organizations.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should train leaders to communicate decision-making frameworks and triggers for change rather than overpromising specific forecasts—this builds trust and enables employee/stakeholder action even when the future is unclear, avoiding both paralysis and false confidence.
