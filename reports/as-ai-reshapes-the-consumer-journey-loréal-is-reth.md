**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
L'Oréal's Chief Digital and Marketing Officer Asmita Dubey describes how AI is reshaping marketing, requiring closer integration of marketing, data, and IT teams, new skills in 'generative engine optimization,' and hands-on experimentation—including having L'Oréal's 20 most senior executives personally create an AI-generated ad from start to finish.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates experiential learning as a change management tool (leaders modeling hands-on AI adoption rather than reading about it) and the breakdown of functional silos as cross-disciplinary collaboration (marketing, data, IT) becomes necessary for organizational adaptation.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should prioritize experiential, hands-on learning for senior leaders during major technology shifts—mandating that executives personally use new tools builds genuine understanding and credibility, rather than relying on top-down directives or theoretical training.
