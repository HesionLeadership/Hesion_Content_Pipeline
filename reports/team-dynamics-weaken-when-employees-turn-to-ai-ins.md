## Summary
A new study examines how reliance on AI tools may be eroding transactive memory systems—the shared understanding within teams of 'who knows what'—that traditionally underpins coordination and trust among colleagues.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story applies Dan Wegner's transactive memory systems theory, suggesting that when employees offload knowledge-seeking to AI instead of colleagues, teams may lose the interpersonal awareness and trust structures that enable effective coordination.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should monitor whether AI adoption is substituting for peer-to-peer knowledge exchange, and intentionally design workflows that preserve opportunities for employees to build mutual awareness of each other's expertise, not just efficiency gains from AI tools.
