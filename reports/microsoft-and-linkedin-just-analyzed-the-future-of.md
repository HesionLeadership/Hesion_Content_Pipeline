# Microsoft and LinkedIn just analyzed the future of work and AI. It all points to one key skill set

**Source:** Fast Company  
**Published:** 2026-08-14T08:00:00  
**URL:** [https://www.fastcompany.com/91583725/microsoft-linkedin-future-work-ai-human-connection](https://www.fastcompany.com/91583725/microsoft-linkedin-future-work-ai-human-connection)

## Summary
Microsoft and LinkedIn research suggests that as AI automates technical execution, emotional intelligence and community building become the most critical leadership skills. The article recommends leaders optimize for unstructured connection, reward vulnerability over perfection, and elevate human milestones to combat workplace isolation and build psychological safety.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
As AI commoditizes technical output, the scarcity shifts to human connection and psychological safety—leaders who cultivate trust, empathy, and belonging create the conditions for innovation and engagement that algorithms cannot replicate.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should deliberately redesign work structures to counteract AI-driven isolation: create intentional unstructured interaction spaces, model vulnerability from the top to establish psychological safety, and invest in recognition systems that celebrate human milestones—these are the levers that sustain engagement and collaboration as technical work becomes increasingly automated.

## Original Story Summary
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Algorithms can now write code, draft legal contracts, and generate entire <a href="https://www.inc.com/marketing" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">marketing</a> campaigns in seconds. As <a href="https://www.inc.com/artificial-intelligence" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">artificial intelligence</a> automates increasingly complex work, it’s easy to assume <a href="https://www.inc.com/entrepreneurs-organization/the-skill-that-will-matter-most-in-the-age-of-ai-isnt-technical/91354801" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">technical expertise</a> will become the defining trait of great leadership. &nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The evidence points in the opposite direction.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index/ai-at-work-is-here-now-comes-the-hard-part)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">Recent data from Microsoft and LinkedIn</a> reveals a fascinating reality. While <a href="https://www.fastcompany.com/section/artificial-intelligence" title="AI">AI</a> is automating execution, leaders are aggressively prioritizing soft skills like emotional intelligence. As tools become more artificial, humans crave the authentic. &nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The ultimate competitive moat is no longer technical execution. It is the ability to forge genuine human connection.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">If you are a founder or an executive, community building fueled by high emotional intelligence is the single most important leadership skill you must master.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-the-isolation-crisis-nbsp">The isolation crisis&nbsp;</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A massive psychological shift is happening in the workplace. Gallup research confirms that <a href="https://www.gallup.com/workplace/349484/state-of-the-global-workplace.aspx)" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">employee stress remains at record highs</a>, and loneliness is a massive factor. When you introduce generative models into your daily operations, your team members spend more time prompting machines and less time talking to each other.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">This creates a vacuum of trust. Humans are biologically wired for social connection.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When people feel isolated, their brains enter a state of chronic stress. You can deploy the most advanced foundational models in the world, but if your team feels disconnected, your output will plummet.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The smartest leaders recognize that their job is not to manage workflows. Their job is to manage energy and connection.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-the-empathy-premium-nbsp">The empathy premium&nbsp;</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">When technical output becomes a commodity, what becomes scarce? The answer is human resonance.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The American Psychological Association recently found that <a href="https://www.apa.org/topics/healthy-workplaces/artificial-intelligence-workplace-worry" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">workers who are worried about artificial intelligence</a> are significantly more likely to feel tense, stressed, and isolated. A machine can generate a flawless and sterile piece of text. A human brings vulnerability, shared struggle, and nuanced understanding. In a market flooded with synthetic perfection, people will pay a premium for authentic imperfection.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The same principle applies to your internal culture. Your team doesn’t want a flawless manager who acts like an algorithm. They want a leader who understands their anxieties about the future of work. They want someone who can build a safe environment where it is acceptable to experiment, fail, and learn together. Empathy is the engine of psychological safety, and psychological safety is the engine of true innovation.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-your-blueprint-for-human-connection-nbsp">Your blueprint for human connection&nbsp;</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">How do you operationalize emotional intelligence and community building inside your company? It requires a deliberate approach to how you structure your daily operations.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Optimize for unstructured connection.</strong> Don’t just schedule meetings for status updates—a machine can read a status update. Instead, create intentional spaces where your team can connect over shared interests, challenges, and ideas without a rigid agenda.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Reward vulnerability over perfection.</strong> If you want your team to trust you, you must go first. Share your own challenges and uncertainties about navigating the new tech landscape. When leaders admit they don’t have all the answers, it gives the team permission to be honest and collaborative.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><strong>Elevate human milestones.</strong> Algorithms don’t care about birthdays, work anniversaries, or personal triumphs. You must. Celebrate the unique human moments that machines cannot replicate.&nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The future of leadership isn’t about competing with algorithms. It’s about doubling down on the things algorithms can’t do. &nbsp;</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Step away from the dashboard, look your team in the eye, and start building a culture rooted in genuine connection.&nbsp;</p>
