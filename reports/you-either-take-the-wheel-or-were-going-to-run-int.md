# ‘You either take the wheel, or we’re going to run into the wall’: The founder who sold Wrike for $2.25 billion forced his engineers to build around AI

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Fri, 20 Nov 2026 15:00:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/20/andrew-filev-wrike-hops-ai-engineers-take-the-wheel/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/20/andrew-filev-wrike-hops-ai-engineers-take-the-wheel/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Andrew Filev, who sold Wrike for $2.25 billion, built his new startup Hops around AI agents that out-produce human engineers, then forced his team to restructure their workflow entirely around AI or be left behind. He reports a 5-10x increase in output, but says even his best engineers resisted the change for months, requiring repeated explanation and enforcement.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates deliberate disequilibrium as a change strategy—intentionally creating unsustainable conditions ('flooring the pedal') to force adaptation—paired with a redefinition of managerial competencies (from people/project management to 'process management' of AI agents) and new hiring criteria emphasizing agency and ownership over technical skill.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that technology-driven transformation often fails not from lack of individual productivity gains but from unaddressed organizational bottlenecks (approval chains, cross-functional handoffs); forcing structural change may require imposed urgency and redefined success metrics, but leaders must be transparent about the difficulty and expect resistance even from top performers, requiring sustained reinforcement over months.

## Strength Score
**7/10** — The story goes beyond typical AI-hype coverage to describe concrete organizational mechanisms: forced behavioral change, redefinition of managerial roles and selection criteria, and explicit acknowledgment of resistance from high performers. These map directly onto established change management and job redesign literature, though the account is self-reported by the founder with no independent verification of outcomes, which tempers the score.
