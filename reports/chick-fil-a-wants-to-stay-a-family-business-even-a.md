# Chick-fil-A wants to stay a family business even as it expands in the U.S. and abroad

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Sun, 04 Oct 2026 12:08:54 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/04/chick-fil-a-ceo-andrew-cathy-family-ownership-growth.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/04/chick-fil-a-ceo-andrew-cathy-family-ownership-growth.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Chick-fil-A CEO Andrew Cathy discusses balancing the chain's traditional family-business values (closed Sundays, human-to-human hospitality) with calculated expansion, menu innovation, and new ventures like Daybright and Acrew Home Professionals, while explicitly rejecting AI voice ordering and IPO plans to preserve long-term, private control.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how organizational identity and core values (hospitality, family ownership, long-term orientation) can be deliberately preserved as stabilizing anchors while operational practices evolve, reflecting tension between organizational stability and adaptive change.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs can learn that scaling successfully doesn't require abandoning cultural identity—leaders should clearly distinguish 'what never changes' (mission, values) from 'what must evolve' (processes, offerings) to maintain employee and customer trust during growth.
