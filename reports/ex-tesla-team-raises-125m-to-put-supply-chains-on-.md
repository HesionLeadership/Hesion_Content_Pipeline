## Summary
Supply chain startup Atomic, founded by ex-Tesla engineers, raised a $12.5M Series A to expand its AI-driven inventory and operations decision-making platform, which now autonomously handles purchasing decisions for customers like DoorDash and HelloFresh.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story touches on organizational decision-making speed as a competitive advantage and the psychological shift required for leaders to delegate operational decisions from humans to autonomous AI systems, once implicit decision rules are made explicit.

## Leadership Lesson
COOs should treat operational data with the same priority CFOs give financial data, and consider that codifying tacit decision rules can accelerate organizational decision speed—a compounding advantage over slower-moving competitors.
