# Ancestry’s CEO says a billionaire boss taught him to eat lunch with employees in the break room—it gives him access to ‘unfiltered information’

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Ancestry CEO Howard Hochhauser eats lunch with employees in the break room, a habit learned from former Bear Stearns boss Ace Greenberg, to gain access to candid, unfiltered feedback. The article also cites similar practices at ABN Amro, Apple, Duolingo, and First Watch, where executives use informal shared meals to build trust and surface honest employee sentiment.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Informal, proximate contact between leaders and employees reduces status barriers and information filtering that typically occurs through hierarchical layers, enabling more accurate upward communication and perceived leader accessibility.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider institutionalizing informal, low-hierarchy touchpoints (shared meals, open seating, skip-level chats) as a structured complement to formal feedback channels like engagement surveys, since proximity and informality can surface concerns that formal channels filter out or sanitize.
