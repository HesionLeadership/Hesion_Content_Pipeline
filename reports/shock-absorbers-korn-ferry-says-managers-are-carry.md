# ‘Shock absorbers’: Korn Ferry says managers are carrying AI change that executives aren’t ready to lead

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sat, 07 Nov 2026 11:30:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/07/korn-ferry-survey-ai-transformation-leaders-middle-managers/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/07/korn-ferry-survey-ai-transformation-leaders-middle-managers/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Korn Ferry's Talent Trends 2027 report finds only 6% of talent and senior leaders believe their top executives are fully prepared to lead AI-driven transformation, leaving middle managers to absorb the burden of guiding teams through change without adequate context or support. Related Gallup data show that clear AI communication plans and supportive managers correlate with significantly higher employee engagement.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates role conflict and role ambiguity theory: managers are forced to communicate and defend decisions they had no part in making, which research links to burnout, reduced trust, and disengagement when sensemaking and authority are misaligned.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat middle managers as a critical transmission layer in change management—briefing them early with context and rationale, equipping them with job-specific training, and building communication cascades before top-down mandates reach frontline teams, rather than expecting them to improvise explanations for decisions made without their input.
