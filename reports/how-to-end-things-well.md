## Summary
The article addresses how leaders can manage organizational endings—such as disbanding teams, closing projects, retiring products, or ending partnerships—with intentionality and care, rather than focusing exclusively on beginnings and launches.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Endings trigger loss, identity disruption, and uncertainty for employees, making how leaders handle closures a critical determinant of trust, morale, and future engagement. Research on psychological contracts and organizational transitions supports that poorly managed endings can erode organizational commitment far beyond the immediate event.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should develop deliberate 'ending rituals' and communication frameworks for project closures, team disbandments, and organizational transitions—treating endings as leadership moments that shape culture, psychological safety, and employee trust for future initiatives.
