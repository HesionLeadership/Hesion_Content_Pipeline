## Summary
Visa laid off 2,600 employees but its announcement missed three critical leadership messages, creating unnecessary trust and morale problems despite clear and quick communication.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Layoff communication that lacks empathy, survivor support messaging, and a clear rationale beyond cost-cutting undermines procedural and interactional justice, eroding trust among both departing and remaining employees.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should ensure layoff announcements go beyond operational clarity to include acknowledgment of affected employees' contributions, transparent rationale tied to strategic vision, and explicit commitments to remaining employees—these elements are critical for maintaining organizational trust and reducing survivor syndrome.
