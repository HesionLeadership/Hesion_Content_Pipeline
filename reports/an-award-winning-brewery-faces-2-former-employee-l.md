# An Award-Winning Brewery Faces 2 Former Employee Lawsuits. Its CEO Says Chapter 11 Was the Cheaper Option

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Tue, 25 Aug 2026 15:33:01 -0400

## Summary
An award-winning craft brewery filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy after facing two lawsuits from former employees, with its CEO stating bankruptcy was cheaper than fighting the lawsuits. The brewery lists roughly $301,000 in assets against nearly $547,000 in liabilities.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case highlights how unresolved employee grievances and potential workplace misconduct can escalate into existential threats for small organizations, illustrating the outsized impact of poor people management on organizational survival.

## Leadership Lesson
Small business leaders must invest in proactive HR practices—fair treatment, proper documentation, and early conflict resolution—because the cost of neglecting employee relations can far exceed the cost of prevention, potentially threatening the entire enterprise.
