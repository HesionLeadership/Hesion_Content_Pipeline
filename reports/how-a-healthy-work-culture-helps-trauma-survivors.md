## Summary
The article examines how healthy workplace cultures can support employees who are survivors of childhood adversity (adverse childhood experiences/ACEs), exploring the intersection of pre-workforce trauma and organizational well-being.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Organizations that cultivate psychologically safe and supportive cultures can serve as healing environments for employees carrying the effects of childhood trauma, which affects a significant portion of the workforce. This highlights the role of workplace design and leadership behavior in addressing hidden vulnerabilities that impact employee performance, engagement, and well-being.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that a substantial portion of their workforce may carry the effects of adverse childhood experiences, and should invest in trauma-informed management practices, psychological safety, and supportive workplace cultures—not just as wellness initiatives but as strategic enablers of sustained performance and retention.
