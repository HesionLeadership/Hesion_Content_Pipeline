# Xero CEO: Why America’s entrepreneurship boom is only half the story

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sat, 07 Nov 2026 10:30:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/07/america-entrepreneurship-boom-small-business-owner-pay-xero-sukhinder-singh-cassidy/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/07/america-entrepreneurship-boom-small-business-owner-pay-xero-sukhinder-singh-cassidy/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
The Xero CEO argues that while U.S. business formation is booming (5.7M new applications in 2025, driven by AI), most small business owners are overworked and underpaid—earning a median of $57,600/year while working 50+ hours a week—revealing an 'Ownership Gap' between business success metrics and owner wellbeing. He calls for policymakers, investors, and economists to measure owner health (income, time, resilience) alongside business growth metrics.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the job demands-resources model and self-determination theory: external success metrics (revenue, growth) can mask internal depletion when autonomy, financial security, and control over time are lacking, leading to burnout even amid 'success.'

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should pair performance/output metrics with wellbeing and sustainability metrics (time autonomy, financial security, stress load) to avoid rewarding growth that quietly burns out the people driving it.

## Strength Score
**6/10** — While framed around small business owners rather than traditional organizational employees, the core tension—output metrics vs. human sustainability—is a legitimate and well-supported org psych theme (burnout, JD-R model, success vs. wellbeing misalignment). It's somewhat tangential to corporate leadership but still offers a transferable insight for how organizations measure 'success.'
