# This startup is paying employees to post on LinkedIn—and solving its hiring problem

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Mon, 21 Sep 2026 12:27:43 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/09/21/this-startup-is-paying-employees-to-post-on-linkedin-and-its-solving-its-hiring-problem/](https://fortune.com/2026/09/21/this-startup-is-paying-employees-to-post-on-linkedin-and-its-solving-its-hiring-problem/)

## Summary
Startup Warp built an employee-generated content program that pays employees to post about their work on LinkedIn, which has become a key driver of inbound hiring and helps filter out the flood of AI-generated job applications by attracting candidates who already understand the company culture.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Employee advocacy programs leverage authentic employee voice as both an employer branding mechanism and a self-selection filter for person-organization fit, reducing information asymmetry in the hiring process while simultaneously reinforcing employees' organizational identification.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should consider structured employee advocacy programs as a dual-purpose tool: they strengthen employer brand through authentic narratives while creating a natural screening mechanism that attracts culturally aligned candidates—particularly valuable as AI-generated applications degrade traditional applicant quality signals.
