## Summary
The article discusses how toxic leaders can infiltrate organizations because technical brilliance often masks serious leadership risks. It highlights warning signs that appear during the hiring process and advises companies to look beyond competence to assess character, interpersonal behavior, and cultural fit before making leadership hires.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses the concept of destructive leadership and the hiring biases that allow toxic individuals to ascend into positions of power. Research on the 'dark triad' traits (narcissism, Machiavellianism, psychopathy) shows these individuals often perform well in interviews and early evaluations, making structured behavioral assessment critical.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should implement structured, behaviorally-anchored hiring processes for leadership roles that explicitly assess interpersonal competencies, emotional intelligence, and past patterns of team impact—not just technical expertise or charisma. Reference checks focused on how candidates treated subordinates, not just what they achieved, can surface red flags before a toxic leader is embedded.
