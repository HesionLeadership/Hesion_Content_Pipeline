## Summary
EA cofounder Bing Gordon recounts Amazon CEO Andy Jassy's signature interview question—'What's your strategy for constant learning?'—which he uses to quickly screen candidates, ending interviews early if they can't answer. The article also surveys other executives' idiosyncratic interview questions and tactics, including IBM, LinkedIn, Sonova, Bupa, and Twilio leaders.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This touches on signature interview questions as heuristics for assessing growth mindset and self-directed learning, but also illustrates how executives can over-rely on a single proxy question or snap judgment, risking interviewer bias and unreliable predictive validity for job performance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should be cautious about leaders relying on a single 'gotcha' question as a hiring filter; structured, multi-rater interviews with validated competency frameworks are more predictive of performance than one executive's pet question, however insightful it may seem.
