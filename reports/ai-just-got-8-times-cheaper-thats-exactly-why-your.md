## Summary
As AI costs drop dramatically, the article argues that forward-thinking companies should reinvest savings into expanding AI capabilities rather than simply cutting budgets, framing this as a strategic differentiation opportunity.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects a growth mindset vs. fixed mindset distinction at the organizational level—teams that view cost reductions as investment opportunities rather than savings targets demonstrate exploration-oriented strategic thinking, which research links to sustained competitive advantage and organizational learning.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should foster an organizational culture that treats efficiency gains as reinvestment capital rather than pure cost savings. Leaders who frame resource windfalls as expansion opportunities—and empower teams to experiment—build adaptive organizations that outpace cost-cutting competitors.
