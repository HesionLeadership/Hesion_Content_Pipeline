# McKinsey research shows AI creating more jobs than automation displaces — but 11 million American workers will need help this decade

**Source:** Fortune

**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/10/11/mckinsey-research-11-million-americans-new-job-next-decade/](https://fortune.com/2026/10/11/mckinsey-research-11-million-americans-new-job-next-decade/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
New McKinsey research finds AI will create more U.S. jobs than it displaces by 2035, but roughly 11 million workers will need to switch occupations, with credentialing requirements (especially employer-preferred, not legally mandated ones) as the biggest barrier to transitions like administrative assistants becoming project managers.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights how organizational hiring conventions (credential screens, degree requirements) rather than actual skill gaps are the primary barrier to internal talent mobility, revealing a disconnect between what organizations say they value (skills) and what they actually screen for (credentials).

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit credential requirements to distinguish legally mandated ones from mere hiring conventions, build internal mobility pathways and apprenticeships using skills-based assessments, and recognize that failing to redeploy existing talent (while hiring externally for similar roles) creates self-inflicted talent shortages.
