# 54 Percent of Gen-Z Applicants Ghost After a Job Offer. The Real Problem Isn’t Gen-Z, an Expert Says

## Summary
54% of Gen-Z job applicants ghost after receiving a job offer, but an industry expert argues the problem stems from broken hiring practices rather than generational attitudes. The article suggests that lengthy, impersonal hiring processes and lack of communication from employers contribute to candidates disengaging.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects a reciprocity breakdown in the employer-candidate psychological contract: when organizations treat candidates as interchangeable through impersonal, drawn-out processes, candidates mirror that transactional behavior by withdrawing without notice. The phenomenon highlights how perceived procedural injustice in recruitment erodes candidate commitment before employment even begins.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should audit their hiring processes for speed, communication frequency, and candidate experience quality—ghosting is often a symptom of organizational dysfunction in recruitment rather than generational deficiency. Building genuine engagement touchpoints throughout the hiring funnel creates reciprocal commitment and reduces offer abandonment.
