# This Founder Has Never Had an Employee Quit. His Retention Secret Has Nothing to Do With Salary.

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Wed, 19 Aug 2026 04:00:00 -0400

## Summary
Founder Lukas Kerrebijn has achieved zero employee turnover by using sports sponsorships (athletes, football clubs, racing teams) as a hiring pipeline. Rather than boosting sales, these sponsorships help him identify and attract culturally aligned candidates who are already passionate about the brand, resulting in exceptional retention.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights person-organization fit as a retention mechanism—by recruiting from communities already emotionally invested in the brand's identity, Kerrebijn creates strong value congruence from day one, which research consistently links to lower turnover intentions.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should consider unconventional talent pipelines that pre-select for cultural alignment and intrinsic motivation. Building community engagement strategies (sponsorships, brand ambassador programs) can double as sourcing channels that yield candidates with higher person-organization fit, reducing costly turnover.
