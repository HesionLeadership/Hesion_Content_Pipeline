## Summary
Researchers adapted the Big Five personality test into a visual tool for illustrating personality preferences among team members, testing it with over 600 ad hoc teams in IMD leadership development programs and validating it with 100 senior executive teams. The approach helps teams diagnose and address dysfunctional dynamics by making individual personality differences transparent and actionable.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses team composition and interpersonal dynamics through the lens of trait psychology, offering a structured intervention for surfacing personality-driven friction and leveraging complementary strengths within teams.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider integrating personality-mapping tools into team development initiatives—not just for hiring or individual coaching, but as an ongoing diagnostic for improving collaboration, reducing conflict, and building self-awareness within intact leadership teams.
