# Honest feedback wins

**Source:** Fast Company  
**Published:** 2026-08-05T08:00:00  
**URL:** [https://www.fastcompany.com/91584922/honest-feedback-wins](https://www.fastcompany.com/91584922/honest-feedback-wins)

## Summary
Walmart's Greg Cathey discusses how delivering direct, honest feedback is a leadership skill that improves with practice, emphasizing that it's not about being harsh but about building a constructive feedback culture.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Feedback delivery is a well-studied leadership competency in organizational psychology, linked to employee development, trust-building, and performance improvement. The framing of feedback as a 'muscle' aligns with skill-based models of leadership development.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should invest in training leaders to deliver candid, constructive feedback regularly rather than avoiding difficult conversations. Building organizational norms around direct feedback—paired with psychological safety—accelerates talent development and reduces performance ambiguity.
