# Twilio CMO: We shrank 2 weeks of work down to one day with AI. Then the unsexy work began

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Twilio's CMO describes how the company reduced campaign creation time from two weeks to under a day using AI, but argues this required extensive unglamorous groundwork (data hygiene, brand governance, human oversight) rather than AI alone. The author proposes a three-phase framework for enterprise AI adoption (personal productivity, team workflows, full autonomy) and argues most companies overstate how far along they are, citing survey gaps between company confidence and customer satisfaction with AI.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The piece highlights the gap between organizational rhetoric/vision-setting and ground-level operational readiness, and how overselling transformation (versus honestly naming the current phase) erodes stakeholder trust—a classic organizational change management and expectation-setting problem.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should resist pressure to oversell AI transformation internally and externally; instead, they should invest in unglamorous infrastructure (data governance, cross-platform integration, human-in-the-loop quality control) before scaling automation, and communicate transparently about which maturity phase the organization is actually in to maintain employee and customer trust.
