# How to Avoid Innovation One-Hit Wonders

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Mon, 10 Aug 2026 11:00:41 +0000  
**URL:** [https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/how-to-avoid-innovation-one-hit-wonders/](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/how-to-avoid-innovation-one-hit-wonders/)

## Summary
The article explores why organizations often struggle to sustain innovation beyond initial successes, examining how leaders can avoid becoming 'one-hit wonders' by rethinking how they manage breakthrough wins, learning processes, and the transition from one successful innovation to the next.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Organizations frequently fall into competency traps and success biases—over-indexing on what worked previously rather than fostering the exploratory mindset needed for continued innovation. This reflects core tensions between exploitation and exploration in organizational learning theory.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design systems that resist the natural organizational tendency to codify and replicate past successes rigidly. Instead, build cultures that treat each innovation cycle as requiring fresh hypothesis-testing, diverse team composition, and tolerance for ambiguity—rather than simply scaling the playbook from the last win.
