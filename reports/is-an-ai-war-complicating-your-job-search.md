## Summary
The article examines an escalating 'AI war' in recruitment, where candidates increasingly use AI to craft applications while employers use AI tools to screen them, raising concerns about bias, lack of transparency, and candidates' unawareness of their right to request human review of automated rejections.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights issues of procedural justice and perceived fairness in algorithmic decision-making, showing how opacity in AI-driven hiring processes can erode candidate trust, trigger identity concealment behaviors among diverse applicants, and create a mutually escalating arms race between applicant and employer AI use.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit AI recruitment tools for bias, ensure transparency about how automated decisions are made, and preserve meaningful human review points—since trust and fairness perceptions in hiring processes affect both candidate experience and organizational reputation.
