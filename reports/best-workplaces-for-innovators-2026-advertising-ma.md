# Best Workplaces for Innovators 2026: Advertising, marketing, and PR category winners

**Source:** Fast Company  
**Published:** 2026-09-08T11:00:00  
**URL:** [https://www.fastcompany.com/91590135/advertising-marketing-pr-best-workplaces-for-innovators-2026](https://www.fastcompany.com/91590135/advertising-marketing-pr-best-workplaces-for-innovators-2026)

## Summary
Fast Company's Best Workplaces for Innovators 2026 recognized eight advertising, marketing, and PR companies for enabling employee innovation. Notable practices include Autumn Communications' bicoastal office swap program for tenured employees and David&Goliath's 'bravestorming' areas designed to encourage spontaneous cross-disciplinary collaboration. Other winners include Allstream Energy Partners, Hook, MiQ, Mission North, Paul Street, and R/GA.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
These organizations demonstrate deliberate environmental and structural design to foster innovation — from physical spaces that promote serendipitous interaction to cross-location exposure programs that broaden employee perspectives and break siloed thinking.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should consider that innovation doesn't just come from hiring creative people — it requires intentional design of physical spaces, cultural rituals, and mobility programs that create the conditions for cross-pollination of ideas and psychological safety to experiment.
