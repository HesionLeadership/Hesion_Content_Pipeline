# Want to increase voter turnout? Start with the workplace

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Sun, 01 Nov 2026 12:30:00 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/01/voter-turnout-employers-ceos-workplace-election-day/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/01/voter-turnout-employers-ceos-workplace-election-day/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
New research linking LinkedIn employment data to voter files finds that employers significantly influence whether employees vote, with workers who move to higher-scoring employers becoming up to 5.8 percentage points more likely to vote. The authors argue companies can boost civic participation through simple, low-cost steps like reminders, paid time off, and poll worker recruitment—without requiring political statements from leadership.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates how organizational environments and implicit cultural norms—not just formal policy—shape individual behavior, and how habit formation within a workplace context can generalize to other life domains (voting) and spread to employees' households.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs can treat civic participation like any other culture-building initiative: use simple structural nudges (calendar reminders, paid leave, removing logistical friction) to shift behavior at scale without requiring leaders to take substantive political positions.
