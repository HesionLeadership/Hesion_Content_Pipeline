## Summary
Honeywell CEO Vimal Kapur and C.H. Robinson CEO Dave Bozeman discuss how AI is reshaping industrial and logistics work, emphasizing reskilling over job replacement; C.H. Robinson reports over 60% productivity gains since 2022 using AI agents to automate repetitive tasks like freight quotes and order processing.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story touches on employee anxiety about AI-driven job displacement and how leadership framing (augmentation vs. replacement) and personal leader involvement in upskilling can shape organizational trust and change adoption.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should model the reskilling they expect from employees (e.g., leaders personally training on new tools) and proactively redesign workflows—not just deploy technology—to reduce fear and build buy-in during AI transitions.
