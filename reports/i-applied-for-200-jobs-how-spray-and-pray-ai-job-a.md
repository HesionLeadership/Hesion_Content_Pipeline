# 'I applied for 200 jobs' - How 'spray and pray' AI job applications can backfire

**Source:** BBC Business  
**Published:** Wed, 14 Oct 2026 08:27:17 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c623dnkn8pkyo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c623dnkn8pkyo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Job seekers are increasingly using AI to mass-produce job applications ('spray and pray'), while employers simultaneously use AI to screen candidates and report rising 'AI misconduct' in interviews and assessments, creating an escalating arms race that both sides describe as unfair and exhausting.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates an arms-race dynamic between candidate authenticity signaling and organizational selection validity, where AI erodes the signal value of traditional application materials and undermines perceived procedural fairness in recruitment.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should redesign selection processes around work-sample, behavior-based, and structured interview methods that are harder to game with AI, rather than relying on CVs/cover letters, and should be transparent about AI use on both sides to preserve candidate trust and fairness perceptions.
