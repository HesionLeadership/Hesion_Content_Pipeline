## Summary
The article traces Tim Cook's evolution as Apple CEO across Fortune's coverage from 2008 to 2015, showing his transformation from an under-the-radar 'operations whiz' seen as Steve Jobs's likely successor, to a CEO who established his own leadership identity distinct from Jobs's combustible style.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates successful leadership succession following a charismatic founder, showing how a successor can build legitimacy not by imitating the predecessor but by developing an authentic, contrasting leadership style while still honoring the original culture.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs managing founder-to-successor transitions should resist pressure to have new leaders mimic their predecessor's style; instead, support successors in establishing credibility through consistent, authentic behavior over time, since legitimacy is built incrementally rather than granted at appointment.
