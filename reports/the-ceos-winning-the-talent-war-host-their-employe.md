## Summary
The article argues that CEOs winning the talent war treat employees like valued guests by 'hosting' them—demonstrating genuine care for employees as whole people, which drives engagement, retention, and performance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This connects directly to perceived organizational support (POS) theory: when employees believe their organization genuinely values their well-being and contributions, they reciprocate with higher engagement, loyalty, and discretionary effort.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should embed hospitality-minded practices into leadership development—training managers to show authentic personal interest in employees' lives, not just their output. This relational investment is a low-cost, high-return lever for retention in competitive talent markets.
