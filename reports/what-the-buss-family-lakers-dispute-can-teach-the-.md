# What the Buss family Lakers dispute can teach the wealthy about trusts

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Thu, 27 Aug 2026 12:25:09 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/buss-family-lakers-trust-dispute.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/buss-family-lakers-trust-dispute.html)

## Summary
Jeanie Buss, governor of the Lakers, is contesting her siblings' decision to sell the family's remaining stake in the NBA team, highlighting how family trust structures can create governance conflicts and power struggles among beneficiaries with competing interests.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case illustrates classic family business governance challenges where trust structures intended to preserve unity can instead become battlegrounds for control, revealing how role ambiguity (operational leader vs. equal beneficiary) creates intra-family conflict and decision-making paralysis.

## Leadership Lesson
When designing governance structures for family-owned or closely-held organizations, leaders must clearly delineate decision rights, establish conflict resolution mechanisms, and separate operational authority from ownership rights to prevent destructive power struggles among stakeholders.
