## Summary
In an HBR IdeaCast interview, former American Express CEO Kenneth Chenault discusses leading through crises like 9/11 and the 2008 financial crisis, maintaining core principles like diversity commitments amid political backlash, and fostering innovation within large organizations.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The interview touches on sensemaking in crisis leadership (defining reality and giving hope), the tension between espoused values and enacted values under external pressure, and organizational resistance to change (status quo bias) as a barrier to innovation.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should help leadership teams articulate clear, simple strategic priorities during crises (e.g., liquidity, profitability, selective growth) rather than trying to solve every uncertainty, and should build internal coalitions to defend core cultural commitments (like diversity) with business rationale rather than abandoning them under political pressure.
