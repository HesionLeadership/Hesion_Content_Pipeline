# ADHD has rewired the workplace. This is what it means for bosses and workers

## Summary
As ADHD diagnoses have risen significantly in the adult workforce, workplaces are grappling with how to accommodate neurodivergent employees and whether traditional work structures are suited to their needs.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This story directly addresses neurodiversity as a dimension of workforce inclusion, touching on job design, accommodation practices, and the tension between standardized work processes and individual cognitive differences. It raises questions about whether organizational structures inadvertently disadvantage neurodivergent employees and how inclusive design can benefit all workers.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should move beyond reactive, individual accommodations toward proactive, universal job design principles—such as flexible deadlines, reduced open-plan distractions, and clear task structuring—that support neurodivergent employees while often improving productivity and engagement for the entire workforce.
