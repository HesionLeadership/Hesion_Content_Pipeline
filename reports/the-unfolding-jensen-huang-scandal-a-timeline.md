## Summary
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned after admitting to extramarital affairs, including with a direct report and with an executive (Alice Huang) whom U.S. officials suspect is a Chinese spy who helped Nvidia customers evade chip export controls. Nvidia's board launched an investigation, ousted Huang, and named CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO amid stock declines and congressional scrutiny.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how extreme CEO power concentration (Huang had ~60 direct reports and no succession plan), weak board oversight, and blurred personal/professional boundaries (an affair with a direct report) can create compounding governance, ethical, and national security risks that erupt simultaneously in a crisis.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat concentrated executive authority and the absence of succession planning as structural risk factors, not just efficiency tradeoffs—robust conflict-of-interest policies, clear reporting-line boundaries for romantic relationships, and active succession pipelines are essential safeguards against single-point-of-failure leadership crises.
