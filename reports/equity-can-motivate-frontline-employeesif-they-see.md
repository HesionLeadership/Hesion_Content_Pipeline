# Equity Can Motivate Frontline Employees—If They See Their Impact on the Bottom Line

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-09-21T12:15:18Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/09/equity-can-motivate-frontline-employees-if-they-see-their-impact-on-the-bottom-line](https://hbr.org/2026/09/equity-can-motivate-frontline-employees-if-they-see-their-impact-on-the-bottom-line)

## Summary
Researchers studied a southern U.S. company with 12 locations and 292 employees to understand how employee ownership programs can improve retention. The key finding is that equity compensation motivates frontline employees primarily when they can see a clear connection between their individual work and the company's financial performance.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the importance of line-of-sight—employees need to perceive a direct link between their efforts and organizational outcomes for ownership incentives to be motivating. Without perceived instrumentality, equity compensation becomes an abstract benefit with limited behavioral impact.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs implementing employee ownership or equity programs should invest equally in transparency mechanisms—such as open-book management, real-time performance dashboards, and regular financial literacy training—so frontline employees can connect their daily contributions to bottom-line results. Equity alone isn't enough; perceived impact is the catalyst for retention and engagement.
