## Summary
A business professor proposes a 'mental board' method where leaders apply Theory of Mind by imagining diverse advisory perspectives to challenge their assumptions, broaden their thinking, and improve decision-making under pressure.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Theory of Mind—the ability to understand others' mental states—has strong roots in cognitive and social psychology, and applying it as a structured cognitive exercise for leaders connects to research on perspective-taking, cognitive debiasing, and strategic decision quality.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs can encourage leaders to systematically adopt multiple stakeholder perspectives before making high-stakes decisions, using structured mental simulation techniques to counteract groupthink, confirmation bias, and narrow framing.
