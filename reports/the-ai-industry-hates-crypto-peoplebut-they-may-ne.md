# The AI industry ‘hates crypto people’—but they may need them all the same

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Mon, 05 Oct 2026 12:26:46 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/10/05/ai-industry-hates-crypto-people-ai-agents-proof-of-control-verification-advanced-ai-society-tricia-wang/](https://fortune.com/2026/10/05/ai-industry-hates-crypto-people-ai-agents-proof-of-control-verification-advanced-ai-society-tricia-wang/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
AI industry professionals reportedly harbor strong disdain for crypto people, viewing blockchain association as a stigma—yet a new group called the Advanced AI Society (led by Tricia Wang) argues blockchain tools like distributed ledgers and zero-knowledge proofs may be essential for monitoring AI agents at scale, since human-in-the-loop oversight won't work for trillions of transactions. The group's 'proof of control' standard is backed by notable non-crypto figures (Bruce Schneier, Clay Shirky) and is being housed at the Linux Foundation.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates in-group/out-group bias and professional tribalism, where one tech subculture (AI) stereotypes and dismisses another (crypto) based on reputation rather than merit, potentially blinding them to useful cross-domain solutions.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should actively counter tribal in-group bias within their organizations—talent and solutions from stigmatized or out-of-favor domains can still offer critical value, and credentialed, cross-group endorsement (e.g., neutral third-party validators like the Linux Foundation) can help overcome status-based resistance to adopting outside expertise.
