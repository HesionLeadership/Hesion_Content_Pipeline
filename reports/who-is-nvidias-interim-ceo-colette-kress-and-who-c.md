## Summary
Nvidia co-founder and CEO Jensen Huang resigned following reports of extramarital affairs, including one with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy, prompting CFO Colette Kress to step in as interim CEO. The article explores potential permanent successors (internal candidates like Chris Malachowsky and Ian Buck, or external figures like Tim Cook and Rene Haas) and notes investor concerns about losing Huang's personal trust-based relationships, with Nvidia shares dropping over 13% and major firms downgrading forecasts.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates 'key person risk' and founder-dependency syndrome, where an organization's strategic partnerships, investor confidence, and market valuation become dangerously entangled with one individual's personal reputation and relationships rather than institutionalized trust in the organization itself.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should proactively build robust succession plans and diversify stakeholder trust across multiple leaders and institutional processes—rather than allowing critical business relationships, governance, and market confidence to hinge on a single charismatic founder, which creates existential vulnerability when that leader's personal conduct becomes a liability.
