# The Conversation Your Team Is Avoiding Could Be Costing You Your Best People

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Sun, 23 Aug 2026 11:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/entrepreneurs-organization/the-conversation-your-team-is-avoiding-could-be-costing-you-your-best-people/91393044](https://www.inc.com/entrepreneurs-organization/the-conversation-your-team-is-avoiding-could-be-costing-you-your-best-people/91393044)

## Summary
The article argues that teams avoiding difficult conversations—about performance, expectations, or interpersonal tensions—are silently driving away their highest-performing employees. What appears as workplace harmony is often conflict avoidance that erodes trust, engagement, and retention.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly maps to Amy Edmondson's psychological safety research: environments where people feel unable to speak up create a false calm that masks disengagement, and top performers—who have the most options—leave first when candor is suppressed.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat sustained team silence and absence of constructive conflict as a warning signal, not a sign of health. Invest in building norms of candid feedback and equip managers to facilitate difficult conversations before quiet attrition claims your strongest contributors.
