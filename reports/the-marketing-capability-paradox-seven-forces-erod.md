## Summary
The article identifies seven forces eroding marketing team effectiveness: AI disruption rewriting content and targeting rules, skill gaps between current capabilities and market demands, organizational silos fragmenting customer-related activities, talent retention challenges, process complexity, misalignment between marketing strategy and execution, and the difficulty of maintaining adaptive organizational know-how in rapidly changing environments.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects the dynamic capabilities framework in organizational psychology — how teams must continuously reconfigure skills, processes, and knowledge structures to maintain competitive advantage, and the systemic forces (structural, technological, and human capital) that degrade collective organizational competence over time.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should conduct regular capability audits that assess not just individual skills but the organizational processes, cross-functional integration, and knowledge management systems that enable teams to adapt. Investing in reskilling without addressing structural and process barriers will fail to close capability gaps.
