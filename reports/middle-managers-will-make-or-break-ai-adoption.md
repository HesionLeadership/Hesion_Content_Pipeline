# Middle Managers Will Make or Break AI Adoption

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-09-01T12:25:07Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/09/middle-managers-will-make-or-break-ai-adoption](https://hbr.org/2026/09/middle-managers-will-make-or-break-ai-adoption)

## Summary
Middle managers are critical to AI adoption success, and most fit one of five distinct profiles that differ in how they respond to risk, evidence, incentives, and support. Leaders need to understand these profiles to tailor their change management approach for effective AI implementation.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses technology adoption resistance and change management at the middle management level, recognizing that individual differences in risk tolerance, evidence needs, and motivation shape how organizational change cascades through hierarchies. The typology approach mirrors diffusion of innovation theory and suggests differentiated influence strategies rather than one-size-fits-all mandates.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should assess their middle management population for distinct adoption profiles rather than treating AI rollout as a uniform change initiative. Tailoring communication, incentives, training, and psychological support to each profile—whether risk-averse skeptics or eager experimenters—will dramatically improve adoption rates and reduce passive resistance.
