# The 5 Feelings That Motivate Employees to Be Productive and Do Their Best Work

## Summary
The article identifies five feelings that motivate employees to be productive and do their best work: feeling respected, feeling a sense of purpose, feeling autonomous, feeling connected to colleagues, and feeling that they are growing and developing professionally.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This aligns directly with self-determination theory (autonomy, competence, relatedness) and research on intrinsic motivation, suggesting that psychological need fulfillment at work drives engagement and performance beyond extrinsic rewards.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should design work environments, management practices, and recognition systems that systematically nurture these five emotional states—treating them not as soft perks but as core performance drivers that can be measured and managed through engagement surveys and manager training.
