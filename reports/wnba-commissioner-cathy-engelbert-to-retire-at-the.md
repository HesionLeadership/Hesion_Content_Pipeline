# WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert to retire at the end of 2026

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Fri, 04 Sep 2026 19:29:31 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/wbna-commissioner-cathy-engelbert-to-retire-at-the-end-of-2026-.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/04/wbna-commissioner-cathy-engelbert-to-retire-at-the-end-of-2026-.html)

## Summary
WNBA Commissioner Cathy Engelbert will retire at the end of 2026 after overseeing unprecedented growth for the league, including expansion teams, a new media rights deal, and rising attendance. Despite these achievements, she has faced significant criticism from players over leadership decisions, compensation, and working conditions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Engelbert's tenure illustrates the paradox of a leader delivering strong organizational performance metrics while simultaneously failing to build trust and satisfaction among key internal stakeholders (players). This highlights the distinction between outcome-based leadership effectiveness and relational leadership quality.

## Leadership Lesson
Delivering strong business results does not insulate a leader from stakeholder dissatisfaction. CHROs and COOs should ensure that leaders who drive growth also invest in relational trust with the workforce—otherwise, legacy and tenure become defined by the gap between external success and internal discontent.
