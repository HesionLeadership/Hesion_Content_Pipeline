# David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz announce Skydance leadership team

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Paramount Skydance CEO David Ellison and incoming co-CEO Ynon Kreiz announced the leadership team that will run the newly merged Paramount Skydance/Warner Bros. Discovery entity, combining executives from both companies into co-chair and co-CEO structures across news, content, and streaming divisions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The new org chart relies heavily on co-leadership and co-chair arrangements (e.g., three executives co-chairing two divisions, dual CEOs at the top), a structure that research on shared leadership shows can either foster complementary strengths or create role ambiguity and conflict if boundaries aren't clearly defined.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs overseeing M&A integration should treat co-leadership structures as high-risk/high-reward: they can preserve talent and signal fairness to both legacy organizations, but require explicit decision rights, conflict-resolution protocols, and clear accountability lines to avoid paralysis or turf battles.
