## Summary
Former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned after revelations of two extramarital affairs—one with a woman suspected of being a Chinese intelligence asset, and another with a direct-report executive (Kimberly Powell)—triggering a $550B stock drop and summons to testify before Congress alongside Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick regarding AI chip export oversight.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates how power asymmetry in a CEO-subordinate relationship can create both personal and organizational risk, and how a board's tolerance for early warning signs (the first affair) can escalate into a full governance crisis once a conflict-of-interest/security dimension emerges.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat any romantic relationship between a senior executive and a direct report as a governance and conflict-of-interest issue requiring immediate disclosure and mitigation—not just a personal matter—especially when it intersects with access to sensitive information or foreign-national contacts.
