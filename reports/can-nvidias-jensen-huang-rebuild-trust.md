## Summary
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang acknowledged an extramarital affair with a woman reportedly linked to Chinese intelligence, prompting an independent board investigation into whether he was targeted by a foreign intelligence operation, while the board continues to publicly support him.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The case illustrates how personal judgment lapses by a high-profile leader can trigger organizational trust crises that extend beyond the individual to affect stakeholder confidence in governance, security, and leadership integrity.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and boards should have pre-established protocols for independent investigations and transparent communication when a leader's personal conduct raises governance or security concerns, ensuring trust repair efforts are credible rather than reactive.
