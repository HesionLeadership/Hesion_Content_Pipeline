# AI Transformation Requires Redesigning Work, Not Cutting Roles

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-28T12:05:44Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/ai-transformation-requires-redesigning-work-not-cutting-roles](https://hbr.org/2026/08/ai-transformation-requires-redesigning-work-not-cutting-roles)

## Summary
HBR argues that AI transformation should focus on redesigning work processes and roles rather than defaulting to headcount reduction. Leaders who simply cut roles end up with smaller but not smarter organizations, missing the opportunity to redeploy talent toward higher-value work.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This connects directly to job design theory and sociotechnical systems thinking—when organizations introduce new technology, the work system (tasks, roles, and human capabilities) must be co-designed alongside the technology to realize performance gains. Defaulting to layoffs signals a fixed-resource mindset that undermines psychological safety and adaptive capacity.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should resist the pressure to treat AI as a cost-cutting tool and instead invest in systematic work redesign—decomposing roles into tasks, identifying which tasks AI augments vs. automates, and redeploying human talent toward judgment-intensive, creative, and relational work. This preserves institutional knowledge and sustains employee engagement during transformation.
