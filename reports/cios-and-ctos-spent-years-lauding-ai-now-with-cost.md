# CIOs and CTOs spent years lauding AI. Now, with costs rising, they’re putting limits on how it’s used

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Wed, 12 Aug 2026 16:38:40 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/cios-and-ctos-spent-years-lauding-ai-now-with-costs-rising-theyre-putting-limits-on-how-its-used/](https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/cios-and-ctos-spent-years-lauding-ai-now-with-costs-rising-theyre-putting-limits-on-how-its-used/)

## Summary
After years of enthusiastically promoting AI adoption, CIOs and CTOs are now imposing usage caps and retraining employees to use smaller, cheaper AI models as costs spiral. Leaders are shifting from broad AI evangelism to cost-conscious governance, recognizing that many tasks don't require expensive large language models.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects the classic hype-to-rationalization cycle in technology adoption, where initial enthusiasm leads to uncritical diffusion, followed by corrective governance. It also highlights the challenge of changing employee behavior after organizations have already established norms of unrestricted usage—a sensemaking and habit-change problem.

## Leadership Lesson
When introducing transformative tools, leaders should build cost-awareness and appropriate-use frameworks from the start rather than offering unlimited access and then retroactively imposing constraints. Pulling back resources employees have come to rely on creates friction, erodes trust, and signals poor planning. CHROs and COOs should partner with technology leaders to design adoption strategies that include guardrails, training on right-sizing tool selection, and clear usage policies before scaling.
