# Spot New Tech Skills Emerging From the Workforce

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Thu, 27 Aug 2026 11:00:10 +0000  
**URL:** [https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/spot-new-tech-skills-emerging-from-the-workforce/](https://sloanreview.mit.edu/article/spot-new-tech-skills-emerging-from-the-workforce/)

## Summary
Companies are investing heavily in reskilling programs based on forecasted tech skills, but MIT Sloan Review argues that organizations should also look inward at skills already emerging organically from their workforce rather than relying solely on top-down predictions of future skill needs.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This highlights the tension between top-down strategic workforce planning and bottom-up emergent learning, suggesting that employees on the front lines often develop novel competencies before leadership formally recognizes them—a form of organizational sensemaking and adaptive learning.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should complement forecasting-driven reskilling programs with systematic methods for detecting and cataloging skills that employees are already developing on the job, creating feedback loops that surface grassroots innovation and reduce wasted training investment.
