# Disney laying off around 300 employees in latest cuts under new CEO Josh D'Amaro

## Summary
Disney is laying off around 300 employees, mostly in HR and technology roles, as part of ongoing cost-cutting under new CEO Josh D'Amaro. This follows earlier 2025 rounds of cuts affecting Pixar, National Geographic, and other divisions as part of D'Amaro's 'One Disney' integration strategy.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Repeated, incremental layoffs (April, July, now) under a new CEO can erode psychological safety and trust across the organization, as employees face prolonged uncertainty rather than a single decisive restructuring event.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that staggered layoffs, even when individually smaller, often cause more cumulative anxiety and disengagement among surviving employees than one larger, clearly communicated restructuring—leaders should weigh the psychological cost of 'death by a thousand cuts' against short-term flexibility gains.
