# 'I didn't speak the Queen's English': Why people worry about their accents

**Source:** BBC Business  
**Published:** Sun, 23 Aug 2026 23:25:44 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c05q318501yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/c05q318501yo?at_medium=RSS&at_campaign=rss)

## Summary
A survey reveals that 'accent bias' remains prevalent in workplaces, with many employees feeling judged, overlooked for promotions, or pressured to modify their accents to fit in professionally. People from regional or non-standard accent backgrounds report self-consciousness and discrimination in professional settings.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Accent bias is a form of workplace discrimination rooted in social identity and in-group/out-group dynamics, directly impacting psychological safety, inclusion, and employee engagement. It represents a subtle but measurable barrier to equity in hiring, promotion, and daily workplace interactions.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize accent bias as a legitimate dimension of workplace diversity and inclusion—train hiring managers and leaders to identify and counteract accent-based stereotyping, ensure structured interviews minimize subjective bias, and foster cultures where linguistic diversity is valued rather than penalized.
