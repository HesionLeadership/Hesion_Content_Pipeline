## Summary
Following allegations of extramarital affairs involving Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, this piece speculates on potential successors, including: Lisa Su (AMD CEO), Eric Schmidt (former Google CEO), Lip-Bu Tan (Intel CEO), Bret Taylor (Sierra CEO, former OpenAI board chair), Ian Buck (Nvidia VP, CUDA co-creator), Andrew Bell (Nvidia SVP of hardware engineering), and Lori Huang (Jensen's wife, co-lead of family trusts).

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story highlights a critical gap in succession planning at a company valued for its visionary founder-CEO, illustrating how leadership scandals can expose the risks of over-reliance on a single charismatic leader without a clear succession pipeline.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat succession planning as an ongoing governance requirement, not a reactive exercise—especially for founder-led companies where leadership is closely tied to brand identity and strategic vision. Boards should maintain active bench-strength assessments and crisis protocols independent of how irreplaceable a leader seems.

## Strength Score
**6/10** — While the piece is speculative and gossip-driven (focused on scandal and parlor-game listicle style), it does surface a genuine organizational psychology issue: the absence of succession planning at a company led by a highly visible, singular leader. The analysis of internal vs. external candidates and considerations like 'builder' vs. 'operator' archetypes has some substantive organizational relevance, though the framing is more entertainment than analysis.
