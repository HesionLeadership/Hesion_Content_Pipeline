# How Much Time Do Your Employees Spend Botsitting?

**Source:** HBR  
**Published:** 2026-08-05T12:05:57Z  
**URL:** [https://hbr.org/2026/08/how-much-time-do-your-employees-spend-botsitting](https://hbr.org/2026/08/how-much-time-do-your-employees-spend-botsitting)

## Summary
AI tools can automate hours of work weekly, but employees spend significant hidden time on 'botsitting'—monitoring, correcting, and overseeing AI outputs. Leaders need to recognize and account for this overlooked labor when calculating AI's true productivity gains.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The concept of 'botsitting' represents a new form of invisible work that can lead to role ambiguity, increased cognitive load, and employee frustration when organizations fail to formally acknowledge or resource this labor—paralleling research on shadow work and emotional labor.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should conduct time audits to quantify the hidden oversight labor AI adoption creates, then formally integrate botsitting responsibilities into job descriptions, workload planning, and performance metrics rather than assuming AI simply frees up employee time.
