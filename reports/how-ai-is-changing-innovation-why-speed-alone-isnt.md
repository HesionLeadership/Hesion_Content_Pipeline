## Summary
Cisco's Chief Product Officer Jeetu Patel discusses how AI is reshaping innovation by making prototyping faster and cheaper, arguing that human judgment, taste, and restraint become more critical as speed becomes commoditized. He emphasizes deciding what not to build, maintaining rigor despite AI-driven speed, and managing risks from increasingly autonomous AI agents.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The discussion touches on how abundance of capability (via AI) shifts the psychological burden from execution to judgment and discernment, and how organizations must rebuild trust and accountability structures (human provenance, oversight) as automation increases.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should resist conflating speed with value—build cultures that reward critical judgment and selective focus over sheer output volume, and ensure clear human accountability is attached to AI-assisted work to maintain quality control and trust.
