# Develop Your Team’s Social Capital, Not Just Their Skills

**Source:** MIT Sloan Review  
**Published:** Mon, 24 Aug 2026 11:00:55 +0000

## Summary
Leaders must go beyond developing direct reports' technical skills and performance capabilities by also building their social capital — the networks, relationships, and collaborative connections that enable them to lead effectively across organizational boundaries. The article argues that as strategies increasingly depend on cross-functional collaboration, leaders who invest in their team members' relational networks create more capable future leaders.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Social capital theory suggests that a leader's effectiveness depends not only on individual competencies but on the strength and breadth of their relational networks. This aligns with research on leader-member exchange (LMX) and network-based models of leadership emergence, where sponsorship and brokerage behaviors shape developmental trajectories.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should redesign leadership development programs to explicitly include social capital building — such as cross-functional exposure, sponsorship opportunities, and network mapping — rather than relying solely on skill-based training and performance metrics to identify and grow future leaders.
