# Scrubs & Beyond stores are closing: Clothing retailer says all locations will shutter, offers no explanation

**Source:** Fast Company  
**Published:** 2026-09-16T12:31:00  
**URL:** [https://www.fastcompany.com/91608235/scrubs-and-beyond-closing-stores-list-doomed-locations-grows](https://www.fastcompany.com/91608235/scrubs-and-beyond-closing-stores-list-doomed-locations-grows)

## Summary
Scrubs & Beyond, the largest U.S. retailer of healthcare apparel with 115+ stores across 30 states, has abruptly announced closure of all retail locations without explanation. The company, merged into parent company Kindthread by private equity firm LKCM Headwater Investments in 2022, had just undergone a 'revamped retail experience' less than four years ago, and now shows signs of operational collapse including disconnected phone lines and unfulfilled customer orders.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case illustrates the risks of private equity roll-up strategies where multiple companies are merged under a new parent entity without sufficient integration planning or operational transparency. The complete absence of communication to employees, customers, and stakeholders during an abrupt mass closure signals a catastrophic breakdown in organizational communication and stakeholder management.

## Leadership Lesson
When organizations undergo major structural changes like PE-driven mergers, leaders must maintain transparent communication channels—especially during crises. An abrupt shutdown with no public explanation, disconnected customer service lines, and unfulfilled orders destroys trust with employees and customers alike, and CHROs should ensure crisis communication and workforce transition plans are in place before closures are announced, not after.

## Original Story Summary
<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The apparel industry is no stranger to store closures, and this week another retailer has joined the ranks of those shuttering stores. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Healthcare clothing company Scrubs &amp; Beyond has announced that it will close all of its stores, which include more than 100 locations across 30 states. Here’s what you need to know about the store closures.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-is-scrubs-amp-beyond">What is Scrubs &amp; Beyond?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Scrubs &amp; Beyond is a specialty clothing retailer that sells healthcare apparel for medical professionals—doctors and nurses who work in hospitals and healthcare offices across the country.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">According to its website, Scrubs &amp; Beyond says it is the <a href="https://www.scrubsandbeyond.com/about">largest retail provider</a> of healthcare apparel and accessories in America. It has been in operation for more than 20 years. In 2013, it acquired two other professional uniform companies, Uniform City and Life Uniform.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-private-equity-merger">Private equity merger</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In March 2022, private equity firm LKCM Headwater Investments <a href="https://naumd.com/kindthread-launches-with-a-mission-to-transform-healthcare-apparel-industry/">merged</a> Scrubs &amp; Beyond with three other uniform companies—Chefwear, Landau, and White Cross—to form Kindthread, an overarching parent company of the four uniform retailers.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">In October of that year, Kindthread announced a completely revamped retail and online experience for Scrubs &amp; Beyond, which included “an elevated and modernized in-store experience,” <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kindthread-elevates-and-modernizes-retail-experience-for-healthcare-professionals-with-the-relaunch-of-industry-leader-scrubs--beyond-301648270.html">according</a> to a company press release.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">“The scale of Scrubs &amp; Beyond, and its importance to our family of brands, enables us to bring about a positive change in the retail experience for an incredibly important group of people &#8211; healthcare professionals,” Kindthread CEO David Murphy said at the time.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">But fast-forward less than four years later, and now Scrubs &amp; Beyond has abruptly announced it is closing all its retail stores.</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-why-is-scrubs-amp-beyond-closing-its-retail-stores">Why is Scrubs &amp; Beyond closing its retail stores?</h2>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Neither Scrubs &amp; Beyond nor its parent company, Kindthread, has publicly stated why it chose to close all its retail stores.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The announcement of Scrubs &amp; Beyond’s store closures was made on the retailer’s website, which simply stated “All Retail Stores Closing” in a banner across the top of the site. </p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">The chain says is offering 20% to 40% off all products in its retail stores nationwide.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">Based on the language on the store’s website, it is unclear whether Scrubs &amp; Beyond’s online operations will also cease. It is also unclear how many employees will be affected by the nationwide store closures.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">A customer service line for Scrubs &amp; Beyond was disconnected and no longer in service when called by <em>Fast Company</em> on Wednesday.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph">On the Scrubs &amp; Beyond <a href="https://www.instagram.com/p/DQo90YvjtWb/?hl=en&amp;img_index=1">Instagram account</a>, a number of customers have complained recently that they ordered merchandise from the brand but never received it.</p>



<p class="wp-block-paragraph"><em>Fast Company</em> has reached out to Kindthread for comment.</p>
