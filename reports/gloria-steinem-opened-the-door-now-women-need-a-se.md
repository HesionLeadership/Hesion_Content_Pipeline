## Summary
Despite significant gains in education and workforce participation, women remain underrepresented in leadership positions and underfunded as entrepreneurs. The article argues that building women's confidence and professional skills is essential to closing persistent gaps in corporate leadership and venture capital funding.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The persistent leadership gender gap reflects systemic barriers and confidence disparities that organizational psychology research has extensively documented, including stereotype threat, lack of sponsorship, and gendered evaluation criteria that disadvantage women in promotion and funding decisions.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should move beyond awareness of gender gaps to implementing structured interventions—such as sponsorship programs, bias-interrupted promotion processes, and leadership development pipelines specifically designed to address the confidence and skill-building barriers women face in advancing to senior roles.
