# What Causes Poor Leadership? Research Points to Four Leadership Failures

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Fri, 07 Aug 2026 16:50:56 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/marcel-schwantes/what-causes-poor-leadership-research-points-to-four-leadership-failures/91386663](https://www.inc.com/marcel-schwantes/what-causes-poor-leadership-research-points-to-four-leadership-failures/91386663)

## Summary
Companies often create their own leadership problems by promoting capable individual contributors without preparing them to lead. The four leadership failures identified are: (1) failing to build relationships, (2) failing to adapt to change, (3) failing to deliver results through teamwork, and (4) failing to develop others.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly maps onto the well-documented 'Peter Principle' and the transition failure from individual contributor to manager, highlighting that technical competence does not predict leadership competence without deliberate developmental intervention.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should implement structured leadership development programs that begin before promotion, not after—focusing on relational skills, adaptability, team-based execution, and coaching capabilities rather than assuming high performers will naturally become effective leaders.
