# Your Company Culture Is Built One Story at a Time, Says This Psychologist. Here Are 4 Ways to Get It Right

## Summary
A psychologist argues that company culture is shaped by the stories and rituals employees repeat, offering 4 ways to get it right: (1) identify the stories already being told, (2) create rituals that reinforce desired values, (3) make leaders the storytellers-in-chief, and (4) be intentional about which stories get amplified.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
Organizational culture is transmitted and sustained through narrative sensemaking and ritualized behavior, not just formal policies. This aligns with research on symbolic management, organizational storytelling, and how shared narratives create collective identity and normative expectations.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit the informal stories circulating within their organizations—these narratives reveal the true culture more than any values statement. Leaders must intentionally shape, model, and amplify stories that reinforce desired cultural norms, and design rituals that embed those values into daily practice.
