# North Korea used LinkedIn, fake job interviews to trick Binance engineer in $6.3 billion hack

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Tue, 03 Nov 2026 15:16:44 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/03/binance-hack-details-linkedin/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/03/binance-hack-details-linkedin/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Binance disclosed that North Korean hackers used a multi-month social engineering campaign—fake LinkedIn recruiters, staged job interviews, and a tampered Homebrew software download—to compromise an employee and steal $6.3 billion in the largest crypto hack in history. The company has since recovered or replenished the vast majority of stolen funds.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The attack exploited normal human psychological drivers—career ambition, trust in professional networking platforms, and curiosity about job opportunities—rather than purely technical vulnerabilities, highlighting how organizational security is fundamentally a human behavior problem.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat security policy enforcement (e.g., LinkedIn restrictions for sensitive roles) as a culture and compliance issue, not just an IT directive—policies are only effective if actively monitored, reinforced through training, and paired with accountability structures that address why employees bypass them.
