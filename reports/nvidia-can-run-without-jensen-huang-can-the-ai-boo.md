# Nvidia can run without Jensen Huang. Can the AI boom?

**Source:** Fortune  
**Published:** Thu, 19 Nov 2026 16:29:53 +0000  
**URL:** [https://fortune.com/2026/11/19/nvidia-jensen-huang-downdall-investment-ai-boom/](https://fortune.com/2026/11/19/nvidia-jensen-huang-downdall-investment-ai-boom/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned after revelations of extramarital affairs—one with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset and another with a direct report—prompting congressional scrutiny and federal investigations. The company, which had no president, COO, or clear successor, appointed CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO, and its first earnings report without Huang beat estimates even as questions remain about Nvidia's aggressive AI-ecosystem investments and lobbying influence built around him personally.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a textbook case of key-person dependency risk and succession planning failure: a company valued at trillions had no COO, no president, and no clear No. 2, leaving it organizationally fragile despite its market dominance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat founder/CEO succession planning as a core risk-management function, not an afterthought—building bench strength and distributed decision authority before a crisis forces the issue, especially in founder-led companies where personal reputation and business strategy become inseparable.
