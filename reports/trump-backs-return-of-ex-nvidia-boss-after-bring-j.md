## Summary

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The episode highlights key-person dependency risk (deals reportedly sealed on personal 'handshakes' rather than institutionalized processes) and tests how boards balance due process and governance integrity against pressure from powerful stakeholders demanding the reinstatement of a charismatic leader.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat this as a cautionary tale about over-reliance on a single leader's personal relationships for critical business functions, and about ensuring that executive conduct investigations and succession decisions are insulated from external pressure campaigns to preserve governance credibility.
