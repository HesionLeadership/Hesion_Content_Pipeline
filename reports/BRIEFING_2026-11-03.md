# Morning Briefing — November 03, 2026

## This Week's Themes

The dominant theme this week is **trust and accountability in the age of AI and automation**—who's watching the watchers, and who's responsible when things go wrong. **Why HR AI Needs Rules, Not Just Reasoning** (HBR), **The AI Governance Paradox** (MIT Sloan Review), **The "Human-in-the-Loop" Fallacy** (HBR), and **EqualAI CEO: Your AI assistant is acting in your name. Who signed off?** (Fortune) all explore how ambiguous or diffused accountability structures erode confidence and oversight as organizations hand more decisions to automated systems. A second theme is **human psychology as the real attack surface**—both for bad actors and for well-intentioned change: **North Korea used LinkedIn, fake job interviews to trick Binance engineer in $6.3 billion hack** (Fortune) shows how career ambition and trust were exploited, while **Your Company's Fraud Defenses Aren't Ready for AI Threats** (HBR) shows organizations substituting institutional trust for direct verification. A third theme is **organizational identity and leadership under pressure**, visible in **Why Wendy's is losing the burger wars—and is now a takeover target for Nelson Peltz** (Fortune) and **ASML's CEO is putting his paycheck on the line for employee morale** (Fortune), both showing how leadership stability (or instability) and perceived fairness shape trust and performance.
