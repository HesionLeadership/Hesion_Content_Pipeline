# Morning Briefing — September 26, 2026

## This Week's Themes
Leadership psychology under pressure dominates this week — stories on role stress diagnosis (1), leadership anxiety during transformation (2), and agency-building amid uncertainty (3) all point to the same underlying issue: leaders defaulting to reactive, one-size-fits-all responses instead of diagnosing what's actually happening psychologically. A second cluster centers on organizational fragility — concentrated knowledge risk (6), co-founder/TMT friction (7), and the illusion of team alignment (8) all show how invisible structural and relational gaps quietly erode performance until they become crises. AI is the connective tissue running through several stories (4, 10, and tangentially 9) — not as a productivity story, but as a talent-development and accountability problem: what happens to human judgment when AI absorbs the reps beginners need, and who owns a decision when AI shaped it? Airbnb's HR reinvention (5) stands out as the lone "positive model" story, offering a counter-narrative to the risk-heavy theme dominating the rest of the lineup.

## Top Story
The MIT Sloan Review piece on leadership anxiety derailing transformation is the standout — four years of ethnographic research, 760 hours of observation, 300+ interviews. It names something most CHROs sense but rarely articulate: leaders sabotage the very change they champion because their emotional threat response overrides their cognitive commitment. That's a boardroom-level insight, not just an HR talking point.
