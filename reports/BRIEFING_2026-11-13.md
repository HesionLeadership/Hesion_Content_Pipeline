# Morning Briefing — November 13, 2026

## This Week's Themes
The dominant theme this week is founder-centric leadership and key-person risk, driven almost entirely by the Nvidia crisis — see **Jensen Huang is the AI boom's key man. Now, that's a problem** (Fortune), **How fallout from the Nvidia scandal could dampen the hype around the AI boom** (Fast Company), and **The 'charismatic' leader trap: Why organizations shouldn't depend on one individual, no matter how appealing** (Fast Company). A closely related thread is succession planning failure: **NVIDIA Announces CEO Transition** (Hacker News) and **Who could replace Jensen Huang? A highly unscientific list of possible successors** (TechCrunch) both expose how a visionary founder with ~60 direct reports left no viable bench. A third theme is board governance capture — **Nvidia's new chairman Harvey Jones is a 'charming elder statesman' set to lead the search for the next CEO. But did he enable the current crisis?** (Fortune) and **Nvidia is investigating Jensen Huang. But its board may have no interest in challenging its leader** (Fast Company) both question whether long board tenure neutralizes oversight. Finally, power asymmetry in executive-subordinate relationships recurs across **Jensen Huang was having a third affair, Reuters reports** (TechCrunch) and **Ex-Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang summoned to testify before Congress about affair with alleged Chinese spy** (BBC Business), framing conflicts of interest as governance risk, not personal matters.
