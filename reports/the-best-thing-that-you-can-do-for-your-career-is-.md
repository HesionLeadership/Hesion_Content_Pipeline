## Summary
Stellantis engineers describe how AI tools have dramatically accelerated vehicle software development (e.g., a hands-free driving system built in six weeks instead of a year), while leadership pushes a culture of ownership, redundancy-seeking, and simplification across a company still integrating from multiple 2021 mergers (Fiat Chrysler and PSA Group) and facing financial pressure (1.8% operating margin vs. a 7% 2030 target).

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story illustrates tension between rapid technological change and employee psychological security—engineers express both excitement and fear about AI eroding core skills, while leadership tries to reframe 'making yourself redundant' as career growth rather than threat, a classic challenge in managing change-induced anxiety and skill identity.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should recognize that technology-driven efficiency gains (like AI-accelerated workflows) can increase psychological burden even as they increase output speed; leaders must pair speed initiatives with explicit reassurance, redeployment pathways, and ownership structures (like single-owner accountability) to prevent burnout and skill-loss anxiety.
