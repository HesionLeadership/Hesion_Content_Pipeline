## Summary
The article argues that a culture demanding flawless execution stifles innovation by creating fear, and advocates for a cultural shift toward tolerating failure to spark breakthroughs.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This directly addresses psychological safety—when leaders punish mistakes, employees avoid risk-taking and creative experimentation. Research by Amy Edmondson and others consistently shows that psychologically safe environments are prerequisites for learning behavior and innovation.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should audit whether their performance management systems and leadership norms inadvertently penalize intelligent failure. Build explicit mechanisms—such as 'learning from failure' retrospectives and innovation-safe zones—that decouple experimentation outcomes from punitive consequences.
