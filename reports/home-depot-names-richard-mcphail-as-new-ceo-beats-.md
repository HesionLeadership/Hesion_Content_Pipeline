# Home Depot names Richard McPhail as new CEO, beats earnings expectations

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Tue, 17 Nov 2026 15:27:51 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/home-depot-hd-q3-earnings-2026.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/home-depot-hd-q3-earnings-2026.html)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Home Depot announced CFO Richard McPhail will become CEO, replacing Ted Decker who is stepping down to become board chair after returning from medical leave. The company simultaneously promoted Ann-Marie Campbell to President/COO and Jordan Broggi to CFO, while reporting quarterly earnings and revenue that beat analyst expectations.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This is a textbook internal succession planning case: promoting from within (CFO to CEO), repositioning the outgoing CEO as board chair rather than an abrupt exit, and reshuffling multiple executive roles simultaneously to maintain institutional knowledge and signal stability to markets and employees.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should note how Home Depot used a pre-planned succession pathway (promoting a long-tenured insider who explicitly emphasized 'we're not going to change course') combined with a graceful exit for the outgoing CEO into a board role, which can reduce organizational disruption and signal continuity during leadership transitions.
