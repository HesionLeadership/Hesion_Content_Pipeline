# Why Nvidia turned to ‘strategic’ CFO Colette Kress to clean up Jensen Huang’s mess

**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned amid reports of two affairs and an alleged Chinese spy connection, and the board named longtime CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO while retaining her CFO title. Kress, who joined Nvidia in 2013 and previously held senior finance roles at Cisco and Microsoft, is seen as a steady hand given her deep institutional knowledge of the business.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This illustrates the organizational fragility created by highly centralized, founder-dependent leadership (Huang reportedly had ~60 direct reports), and the board's reliance on a trusted internal successor to maintain continuity and investor confidence during a reputational crisis.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should treat key-person dependency as an enterprise risk: maintain active succession plans and empower a small group of deeply knowledgeable senior leaders (not just the CEO) so the organization can pivot quickly during unplanned leadership crises without destabilizing operations or investor trust.
