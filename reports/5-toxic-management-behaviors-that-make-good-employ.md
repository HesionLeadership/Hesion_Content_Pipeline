# 5 Toxic Management Behaviors That Make Good Employees Stop Caring

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Fri, 14 Aug 2026 17:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/marcel-schwantes/5-toxic-management-behaviors-that-make-good-employees-stop-caring/91391005](https://www.inc.com/marcel-schwantes/5-toxic-management-behaviors-that-make-good-employees-stop-caring/91391005)

## Summary
The article identifies 5 toxic management behaviors that cause good employees to disengage: (1) micromanagement that signals distrust, (2) taking credit for employees' work, (3) inconsistent or unfair treatment/favoritism, (4) withholding information or context from teams, and (5) avoiding difficult conversations or conflict. These behaviors erode motivation and drive talented people to quietly quit or leave.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This maps directly onto well-established research on leader-member exchange (LMX) quality, procedural justice, and autonomy as a core psychological need (self-determination theory). When managers violate trust, fairness, and autonomy, they systematically undermine the intrinsic motivation that sustains high performance.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs and COOs should build manager accountability systems that detect these five behaviors early—through 360-degree feedback, engagement pulse surveys, and exit interview pattern analysis—rather than waiting for turnover signals. Investing in manager development around trust-building, transparency, and courageous conversations is a higher-ROI retention strategy than compensation adjustments.
