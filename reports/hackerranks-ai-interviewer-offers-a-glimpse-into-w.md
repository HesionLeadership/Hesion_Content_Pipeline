# HackerRank’s AI interviewer offers a glimpse into what job interviews could become

**Source:** TechCrunch  
**Published:** Mon, 05 Oct 2026 16:43:35 +0000  
**URL:** [https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/hackerranks-ai-interviewer-offers-a-glimpse-into-what-job-interviews-could-become/](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/hackerranks-ai-interviewer-offers-a-glimpse-into-what-job-interviews-could-become/)  
**Content basis:** Full article text

## Summary
HackerRank launched Chakra, an AI interviewer that evaluates candidates' reasoning and judgment (not just outputs) by observing them work through real-world coding tasks, often with AI assistance, and asking follow-up questions in real time. The company claims this combined assessment approach reduces cheating flags by 70-80% compared to traditional assessments, while positioning AI to score candidates and humans to make final hiring decisions.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
The story touches on algorithmic decision-making in personnel selection, the validity of AI-based competency assessment, and the tension between standardized evaluation criteria and algorithmic bias—core concerns in industrial-organizational psychology's selection and assessment literature.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should recognize that shifting to AI-mediated hiring doesn't automatically eliminate bias—it requires rigorous validation, bias auditing, and transparency, since 'consistent' algorithmic criteria can still encode and scale historical biases if the underlying rubric or training data is flawed.
