# Wealth managers face a new challenger: Their clients’ AI chatbots

**Source:** CNBC Business  
**Published:** Thu, 30 Jul 2026 15:02:24 GMT  
**URL:** [https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/wealth-managers-ai-chatbots.html](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/30/wealth-managers-ai-chatbots.html)

## Summary
AI chatbots are increasingly being used by wealthy clients to challenge or second-guess their financial advisors' recommendations, forcing wealth management firms to adapt their client relationship strategies.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This represents a fundamental shift in expert-client power dynamics, where AI tools democratize access to information and erode traditional authority based on specialized knowledge. Advisors must transition from information-asymmetry-based authority to relationship-based trust and emotional intelligence.

## Leadership Lesson
Leaders in professional services should proactively prepare their teams for AI-augmented clients by investing in relational competencies, emotional intelligence training, and value propositions that emphasize judgment, empathy, and personalized counsel—capabilities that AI cannot fully replicate. Redefine what 'expertise' means in your organization before clients redefine it for you.
