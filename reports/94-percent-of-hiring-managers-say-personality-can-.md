# 94 Percent of Hiring Managers Say Personality Can Matter More Than Skills

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Sat, 12 Sep 2026 06:00:00 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/94-percent-of-hiring-managers-say-personality-can-matter-more-than-skills/91398419](https://www.inc.com/bruce-crumley/94-percent-of-hiring-managers-say-personality-can-matter-more-than-skills/91398419)

## Summary
A survey finds that 94% of hiring managers say a candidate's personality can matter more than their skills or experience when making hiring decisions, emphasizing traits like adaptability, communication, and cultural fit as key factors in the selection process.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This reflects the growing emphasis on person-organization fit and non-cognitive attributes in selection, though it raises concerns about the validity and potential biases of personality-based hiring judgments, particularly when relying on unstructured first impressions rather than validated assessment tools.

## Leadership Lesson
CHROs should ensure that when hiring managers prioritize personality, they do so through structured, evidence-based methods (e.g., structured behavioral interviews, validated personality assessments) rather than gut-feel impressions, which are prone to similarity bias and can undermine diversity and predictive validity.
