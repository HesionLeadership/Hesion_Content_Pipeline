# Mom’s Organic Market Banned Masks for Employees to Improve Customer Interactions. Now Workers Are Threatening to Quit

**Source:** Inc  
**Published:** Mon, 17 Aug 2026 16:59:30 -0400  
**URL:** [https://www.inc.com/sarah-bregel/moms-organic-market-banned-masks-for-employees-now-workers-threatening-to-quit/91392088](https://www.inc.com/sarah-bregel/moms-organic-market-banned-masks-for-employees-now-workers-threatening-to-quit/91392088)

## Summary
Mom's Organic Market, a Maryland-based grocer, implemented a policy banning employees from wearing medical face masks at work, citing the desire to improve customer interactions through visible facial expressions. Employees are pushing back strongly, with some threatening to quit over health and autonomy concerns.

## Organizational Psychology Angle
This case highlights the tension between top-down policy mandates aimed at customer experience and employee autonomy over personal health decisions. Unilateral policies that override workers' sense of bodily autonomy and safety can severely damage psychological safety and organizational trust, even when leadership intentions are customer-focused.

## Leadership Lesson
Before implementing policies that affect employees' personal health choices, leaders must engage in genuine dialogue with their workforce. CHROs should recognize that policies perceived as coercive—regardless of business rationale—can trigger reactance, erode trust, and increase turnover intentions. A participatory approach or offering alternatives would better balance customer experience goals with employee well-being.
